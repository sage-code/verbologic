# Status, 2026-10-30: alphabet articles in every language

Where the lecture and alphabet work stands, and how to continue.

## Decisions taken

- **No translation.** Articles are written natively in each language (`scripts/generate-alphabet-content.py --locales ...`), never translated. `scripts/translate.py`, `./run.sh translate` and `content/jobs/translations.json` were removed. Stories follow the same rule, but `scripts/create-stories.py` still translates: see "Open questions".
- **Reading scheme for lecture articles** (`synthesize_sections_mixed` in `scripts/generate-audio.py`): prose is read by the reader's voice, each `(word)` by the native voice of the item's `target` language, IPA is shown but never read, and a sentence that has IPA but no `(word) /ipa/` pair is left out of the recording. Edge TTS cannot read IPA (`<phoneme>` is rejected); eSpeak NG was tried and dropped as it sounded bad.
- Lecture topics LT06, LT07 and LT12 were renumbered (new: Modal verbs & auxiliaries, Time/dates/telling time, Word formation). The 9 single-word and wrong articles and the duplicate LT02T07 were removed. Details: `planning/lecture-articles-review.md`.
