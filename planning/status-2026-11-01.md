## How it is built

- T01/T02: facts in `en.json` (hand-checked IPA) → `generate-alphabet-content.py` (pages per language) → `converge-alphabet-pages.py` (retries only the pages the rules reject) → `alphabet-to-articles.mjs` → `generate-audio.py --read`.
- T03 to T06: seed rows (title, focus, example, IPA, gloss, extras) → `create-lecture-articles.py --target en --topic LT01T0n --locales all --apply`. A page may only quote the seed's words with the seed's IPA. English goes through the same native prompt as the other languages.
- Audio rule: when reader language equals the taught language the page is read in ONE voice (no switch for the examples); other readers hear the native voice for `(word)`.

## Quality notes

- Every English page was read by hand; factual errors found and fixed: letter-variant duplicates, *paper* open/closed, doubled consonants "held longer", *daughter* vowel, *-s* depends on the last sound, *I've* = "I possess", wrong example translations in 15 new T02 groups, French "n ou m" false positive.
- Not reviewed by native speakers: all non-English pages and all IPA (General American, hand-written). Hungarian and Russian wording is the stiffest. Two pages are hand-written (German letter J, English `ci`).
- Dropped on purpose: `ti` (cognate examples), 23 letter-variant pages, 5 duplicate digraph pages, 12 silent-letter pages (replaced by 6 lessons).
