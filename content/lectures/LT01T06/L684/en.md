## The sound
In Romanian, you change your pitch to ask a simple question.
A yes-no question does not change word order.
Instead, your voice rises at the end.
Listen to (Ești român?) /ˈeʃtʲ roˈmɨn/, which means Are you Romanian?
The tone climbs smoothly on the final syllable.

## How to say it
Start speaking at your normal pitch.
Keep your pitch flat through the first words.
When you reach the last syllable, push your voice upward.
Make your tone climb higher like a musical note.
Open your throat and let the final sound ring clearly.
Do not drop your voice at the end.

## Reading rules
Look for a question mark at the end of the sentence.
Romanian questions often have the same word order as statements.
For example, (vine) /ˈvi.ne/ means he comes.
To ask a question, raise your pitch at the end.
The question mark signals that your voice must climb.

## Common mistakes
Many learners drop their pitch at the end of every sentence.
If your voice falls, the question sounds like a plain statement.
Do not rise too early in the sentence either.
Keep your voice level until the end.
Only lift your pitch on the final stressed part.
