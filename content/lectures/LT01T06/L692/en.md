## The sound
Three dots mark a pause in Romanian speech.
Your voice slows down before the silence.
The pitch stays level or floats downward gently.
Your speech sounds unfinished on purpose.
You hesitate, think, or show doubt.
Listen to (Ei... nu știu) /ej nu ʃtʲiu/, which means well, I do not know.

## How to say it
Keep your lips and tongue relaxed.
Do not drop your pitch suddenly.
Hold the vowel slightly longer before you stop speaking.
Leave your mouth slightly open during the pause.
Let your breath trail away softly into silence.
Then start the next part with fresh air and a calm tone.

## Reading rules
Look for three dots in a row in written text.
This mark tells you to stop making sound for a moment.
The mark acts like a long comma.
Your voice does not finish like a period.
Instead, your tone hangs in the air while you wait.

## Common mistakes
Do not rush past the three dots.
People often skip the pause and say every word together.
Do not drop your pitch hard like the end of a sentence.
A sudden drop sounds final and certain.
Keep your voice level so the listener waits for your next word.
