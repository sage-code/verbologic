## The sound
You use (vă rog) /və ˈroɡ/ to say please.
This phrase sounds soft and gentle.
Your voice does not jump up at the end.
Instead, the pitch falls slightly on the last part.
Keep your voice warm and friendly.
A gentle fall sounds very polite in Romanian.

## How to say it
Start quietly with the short word (vă) /və/.
Relax your mouth and make a quick sound like 'a' in 'sofa'.
Then say (rog) /roɡ/ with more weight.
Round your lips for the middle vowel.
Let your pitch drop gently on this second word.
Smile softly to keep the tone kind.

## Reading rules
The letter ă in (vă) /və/ is a relaxed sound.
Never say it as a hard vowel.
In (rog) /roɡ/, the letter o sounds like 'or' without the r.
The letter g at the end is hard, like in 'go'.
Put the main stress on the second word.

## Common mistakes
Do not raise your pitch like a question.
A rising tone can sound surprised or rude.
Do not push the air out too hard.
If you speak too loudly, you lose the gentle feeling.
Keep (vă rog) /və ˈroɡ/ soft, smooth, and low at the end.
