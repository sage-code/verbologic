## The polite pattern

In Romanian, you say **Vă rog.** /və ˈroɡ/ when you want to say "please". It is a short expression that shows good manners. People use it every day when asking for help or ordering food. It has two small syllables. The first word is light and unstressed. The second word carries the main stress. Because it is a polite request, the melody of your voice is very important. A gentle voice helps the listener feel respected and comfortable.

## How to say it

Start with the word **vă**. Keep your lips relaxed and place your tongue in the middle of your mouth for the /ə/ sound. Speak this first syllable lightly. Next, move smoothly to the word **rog**. Place the stress on this syllable. Tap the tip of your tongue once for the sound /r/. Round your lips slightly for the vowel /o/. As you say this syllable, let the musical pitch of your voice gently fall downward. Keep your tone soft and warm throughout the phrase.

## Mistakes to avoid

Many English speakers use a rising pitch at the end of requests. A rising tone here can sound uncertain or like a surprised question. Another common mistake is shouting the word **rog** too loudly. When you push too much air, the request can sound demanding or impatient. Keep your volume calm and even. Make sure the vowel /ə/ in **vă** does not turn into an English short "a". Smile slightly as you speak to keep the sound friendly.
