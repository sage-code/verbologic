## The Sound of W

In German, the letter **W** is pronounced as a voiced labiodental fricative `/v/`. Unlike in English, it never makes the rounded `/w/` sound heard in words like *water*. Instead, it sounds exactly like the English **V** in *vase* or *vine*.

## How to Pronounce W

Place your upper front teeth lightly against the inside of your lower lip. Push air gently through the contact point while vibrating your vocal cords. Keep your lips unrounded so you do not accidentally slip into an English `/w/` sound.

## Spelling and Occurrence

The letter **W** is extremely common in German and can appear at the beginning of words (*Wort*) or inside them (*löwe*, *zwei*). It is capitalized as the initial letter of nouns and at the start of sentences. German never doubles the letter **W** in native vocabulary.

## Avoiding Common Pitfalls

The biggest trap for English speakers is pronouncing German **W** like an English `/w/`, turning words like *Wasser* into */ˈwasɐ/* instead of `/ˈvasɐ/`. Remember that German **W** equals English **V**, while German **V** often sounds like an English **F**. Practice contrast pairs like *Wein* (`/vaɪ̯n/`) and *fein* (`/faɪ̯n/`) to solidify this distinction.
