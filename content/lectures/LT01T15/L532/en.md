## The Sound of F

The Portuguese letter **F** represents the voiceless labiodental fricative `/f/`. It sounds exactly like the English 'f' in **father** or **leaf**. It is entirely consistent and does not alter its sound based on adjacent vowels or stress.

## How to Produce /f/

Gently rest your upper front teeth against the inner edge of your lower lip. Blow air steadily through the narrow gap between your teeth and lip without activating your vocal cords. Keep the airflow continuous and friction-like.

## Spelling Patterns

The letter **F** is written as uppercase **F** and lowercase **f**. It appears initially in words like **fogo** and medially in words like **café**, but it never doubles in native Portuguese spelling (unlike English). It never occurs at the very end of native Portuguese words.

## Nuances and Common Mistakes

Because `/f/` is identical across English and Portuguese, it poses almost no pronunciation challenge for learners. The main pitfall is not the consonant itself, but forgetting European Portuguese vowel reduction in surrounding syllables, such as pronouncing unstressed **e** or **o** too clearly. Always focus on keeping the consonant crisp while reducing the unstressed vowels correctly.
