## The Sound of Hungarian U

The letter **U** represents the short close back rounded vowel `/u/`. It sounds very similar to the 'oo' in English *book* or *look*, but it is articulated with more tightly rounded lips and a slightly higher tongue position. Unlike its long counterpart **Ú**, this vowel is kept distinctly crisp and brief.

## How to Pronounce U

Pull your tongue back toward the soft palate while keeping the tongue body high in the mouth. Round your lips tightly into a small circle, pushing them slightly forward. Produce a clean, short vocal sound without letting it glide or lengthen into a diphthong.

## Spelling and Occurrence

Hungarian **U** appears frequently across all word positions, including prefixes, word stems, and grammatical suffixes. It is written as a simple **U** in uppercase and **u** in lowercase. Do not confuse it with **Ú** (long), **Ü** (front rounded), or **Ű** (long front rounded), which are entirely distinct letters with different sounds.

## Contrasting U with Similar Sounds

A frequent mistake for English speakers is making **U** too lax or lengthening it into **Ú** `/uː/`, which changes word meanings entirely, as seen in *hurok* (loop) versus *húrok* (strings). Additionally, take care not to confuse it with front-vowel **Ü** `/y/`, where the tongue is pushed forward instead of back. Always keep short **U** fully back, rounded, and clipped.
