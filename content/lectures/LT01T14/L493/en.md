## The Sound of É

The letter **É** represents a long, close-mid front unrounded vowel `/eː/`. It sounds very similar to the vowel in French *fée* or German *See*, but it is held distinctly longer. Unlike the English "long a" in *cake*, it is a pure, steady monophthong that never glides into an "ee" sound.

## How to Pronounce É

Spread your lips into a gentle smile, similar to pronouncing a high "ee" sound. Keep your tongue high and pushed forward against your lower teeth, raising the arch slightly lower than for Hungarian **Í**. Hold the vocal tract completely stationary throughout the duration of the long sound.

## Spelling and Occurrence

**É** is written with an acute accent over the letter **E**, which indicates both length and a completely different vowel quality from short **E** `/ɛ/`. It can occur at the beginning, middle, or end of words (such as *kávé* 'coffee'). In Hungarian morphology, a word-final **e** usually lengthens to **é** when adding suffixes, as in *fekete* becoming *feketét*.

## Comparisons and Pitfalls

The most critical distinction is between short **E** `/ɛ/` (open, like in *bed*) and long **É** `/eː/` (close and tense). Confusing them changes the meaning of words, such as *egy* ('one') versus *égy* (archaic/dialectal form) or *ment* ('went') versus *mént*. English speakers must be careful not to turn **É** into a diphthong like /eɪ/.
