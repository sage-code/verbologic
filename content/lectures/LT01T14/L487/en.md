## The Sound of C

In Hungarian, the letter **C** represents a voiceless alveolar affricate `/ts/`, identical to the *ts* in English *cats* or the German *z*. Unlike in English, this sound frequently appears at the very beginning of words, such as in `cica`. It is never pronounced as a hard `/k/` or a soft `/s/`.

## How to Pronounce C

Place the tip of your tongue against the upper gum ridge behind your front teeth, just as you would for `/t/`. Release the tongue tip slightly into an `/s/` sound while allowing air to hiss through, without vibrating your vocal cords. Think of it as a single, explosive unit rather than two separate sounds strung together.

## Spelling and Rules

The letter **C** is always pronounced `/ts/` unless it is followed by a voiced consonant like *b*, *d*, or *g*, in which case it assimilates to its voiced counterpart `/dz/`. When lengthened, it is written doubled as **cc** (e.g., *vicc*), pronounced as a held `/tsː/`. Do not confuse single **c** with digraphs like **cs** (`/tʃ/`).

## Comparing and Common Pitfalls

English speakers often instinctively read **c** as `/k/` before back vowels or as `/s/` before front vowels, which leads to unintelligible pronunciation in Hungarian. Compare the pure affricate in `cica` with words containing **cs** such as `csiga` (`/tʃ/`), ensuring you keep the sound sharp and dental rather than postalveolar.
