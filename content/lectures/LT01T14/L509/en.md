## The Sound of Hungarian Ö

The letter **Ö** represents the short, close-mid front rounded vowel `[ø]`. It is very similar to the German short *ö* in *können* or the French vowel in *peu*, but it has no direct equivalent in standard English. English speakers often substitute it with the vowel in *bird* or *cup*, but **Ö** is strictly fronted and pronounced with rounded lips.

## How to Pronounce Ö

Start by producing the English vowel sound in *bed* (`[e]`), keeping your tongue pressed forward against the backs of your lower front teeth. Without moving your tongue at all, round your lips tightly as if you were going to whistle or say *oh*. Keep the sound short, crisp, and pure, avoiding any diphthong glide.

## Spelling and Distribution

Hungarian **Ö** is written with two dots (an umlaut) and is treated as an independent letter in the alphabet, coming immediately after **O**. It can appear at the beginning, middle, or end of words, such as in *öt* (five) or *sör* (beer). Be careful not to confuse it with **Ő**, which has two long accents (a double acute) and represents a distinctly long vowel.

## Comparing Ö and Ő

The most critical distinction in Hungarian is vowel length between short **Ö** (`/ø/`) and long **Ő** (`/øː/`). A classic contrast is *öt* (five) versus *őt* (him/her, accusative), where lengthening the vowel completely changes the meaning. Avoid unrounding your lips into an English *uh* or dragging the short **Ö** out into a long syllable.
