## The sound of J

The Hungarian letter **J** represents the voiced palatal approximant `/j/`. It is identical to the English **y** in *yes* or *yellow*. In standard Hungarian, it shares the exact same pronunciation as the digraph **LY**.

## How to say it

Raise the middle part of your tongue towards the hard palate, close to the position for the vowel `/iː/`. Allow your vocal cords to vibrate as the air glides smoothly through the gap without friction. Keep your lips relaxed or adapt them to the following vowel.

## Spelling conventions

Capital **J** and lowercase **j** appear in all positions of a word, including initials, medials, and finals. Because historical sound changes caused both **J** and **LY** to merge into `/j/`, native speakers must memorize which spelling a word requires by convention. When lengthened, it is written doubled as **jj**.

## Comparing sounds and common pitfalls

Be careful not to pronounce **J** as an English soft 'j' `/dʒ/` or a French `/ʒ/`. Avoid turning it into a harsh fricative `/ʝ/`, except occasionally after voiceless consonants at word boundaries. Remember that words like *jó* ('good') and *folyó* ('river') use the exact same sound `/j/` despite their different spellings.
