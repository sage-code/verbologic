## The Sound of Hungarian E

The Hungarian letter **E** represents an open-mid front unrounded vowel, transcribed as `/ɛ/`. It is fairly open and short, very similar to the English vowel in words like *bed*, *pet*, or *dress*. Be careful not to confuse it with Hungarian **É** `/eː/`, which is noticeably more closed, longer, and higher in vowel quality.

## How to Pronounce E

Drop your jaw slightly so your mouth is moderately open, wider than for an English *say*. Keep your tongue relaxed forward with its tip resting gently behind your lower front teeth. Vibrate your vocal cords to make a crisp, short, unrounded vowel without gliding into any other sound.

## Spelling Rules and Occurrence

The letter **E** appears freely at the beginning, middle, and end of words, as in **este** ('evening') or **kefe** ('brush'). Hungarian spelling is consistently phonetic, meaning a single **E** is practically always pronounced `/ɛ/`. When a word ends in **-e** and takes a suffix, this final vowel routinely lengthens into **-é-** (for example, *este* becomes *estét*).

## Dialects and Common Pitfalls

In Standard Hungarian, **E** is consistently `/ɛ/`, though many regional dialects distinguish between open `/ɛ/` and a closer, short `/e/` (often called *zárt e*). The most common mistake for foreign learners is lengthening **E** or confusing it with **É**, which changes meaning drastically (e.g., *egy* 'one' versus *égy*). Avoid diphthongising the vowel into an *ay*-like glide at the end of syllables.
