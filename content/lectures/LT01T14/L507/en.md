## The Sound of O

The Hungarian letter **O** represents a short close-mid back rounded vowel, transcribed as `/o/`. Unlike the English vowel in words like *boat* or *go*, it is a pure, steady monophthong that does not glide into a `/w/` sound at the end. It sounds very similar to the pure Spanish or Italian short `o`.

## How to Pronounce O

Shape your lips into a tight, firm circle and pull your tongue slightly back in your mouth toward the soft palate. Raise the back of your tongue to a mid-high position without touching the roof of your mouth. Keep the lips and tongue completely motionless while producing the sound to avoid creating a diphthong.

## Spelling and Occurrence

The letter **O** is extremely frequent in Hungarian and can appear at the beginning, middle, or end of words. It contrasts directly with its long accented counterpart **Ó**, which has a similar quality but lasts roughly twice as long. Always write and pronounce it without an accent to keep the vowel short.

## Comparing Sounds and Common Mistakes

Native English speakers tend to diphthongize this vowel into `[oʊ]`, which sounds foreign in Hungarian. Be careful not to confuse short **o** in words like *kor* (age) with long **ó** in *kór* (disease), as vowel length changes the entire meaning. Always keep the sound clipped, clean, and strictly monophthongal.
