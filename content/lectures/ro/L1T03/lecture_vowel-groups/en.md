Romanian allows vowel clusters inside one syllable. These clusters create glide sounds that differ from the isolated vowels. Learn the **movement** between the vowels, not only the single letters.

## Diphthongs

A diphthong is two vowels pronounced as one smooth sound. Read them together, never as separate letters. In the video, Emil reads the example words — follow along with the table.

::youtube{id="zpkmyGwY8dE" title="Romanian diphthongs"}
::

| Group | English approximation | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **ea** | `ye` in **yes**, a quick glide from e to a | *stea* (star) |
| **oa** | `wa` in **water**, a rounded opening toward a | *soare* (sun) |
| **ia** | `ya` in **yard**, starting with a light y-glide | *iată* (behold) |
| **ie** | `ye` in **yet**, short and fluid | *ierburi* (herbs) |
| **io** | `yo` in **yolk**, a brief glide before the vowel | *milion* (million) |
| **iu** | `yu` in **you**, moving smoothly toward u | *chiu* (shout of joy) |
| **ua** | `wa` in **watt**, shifting gently from u to a | *rouă* (dew) |
| **ue** | `we` in **wet**, light and unstressed | *rouă* (unstressed variants) |
| **ai** | `eye` in **high**, a clear closing glide | *mai* (May / more) |
| **ei** | `ay` in **day**, rising toward i | *lei* (lions / money) |
| **oi** | `oy` in **boy**, with a rounded start | *doi* (two) |
| **ui** | a fast `oo-ee` glide | *pui* (chicken) |
| **au** | `ow` in **cow**, opening wide from a to u | *taur* (bull) |
| **eu** | a quick `eh-oo` glide | *zeu* (god) |

::term{addresses="letter_ea,letter_oa,letter_ia,letter_ie,letter_io,letter_iu,letter_ua,letter_ue,letter_ai,letter_ei,letter_oi,letter_ui,letter_au,letter_eu"}
::

## Triphthongs

A triphthong is three vowels that flow as one rapid sound. Keep the motion continuous so the group stays natural. Play the video and listen for the examples.

::youtube{id="tBRK3jPNrdo" title="Romanian triphthongs"}
::

| Group | English approximation | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **eai** | a rapid `ye-ye` glide: e through a into i | *băteai* (you were beating) |
| **iai** | a quick `ye-ye` movement, very short opening | *tăiai* (you were cutting) |
| **eau** | `yow`, a continuous glide ending in u | *stăteau* (they were staying) |
| **iau** | `yow` with a tighter start | *tăiau* (they were cutting) |
| **ioa** | `ywa`, a brief glide into a broad final vowel | *aripioare* (little wings) |

::term{addresses="letter_eai,letter_iai,letter_eau,letter_iau,letter_ioa"}
::

::callout{type="tip"}
If a group sounds like two syllables when you say it, you are pausing between the vowels. Say it faster, as one beat: *soa-re*, not *so-a-re*.
::
