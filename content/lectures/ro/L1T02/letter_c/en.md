**C c** · /k/ or /tʃ/

Before **e** and **i**, **c** is *ch*: *cel* sounds "chel". Everywhere else it is a hard *k*: *casa* sounds "kah-sa".

::callout{type="note"}
This e/i rule is the only conditional spelling rule in Romanian. It applies to **c** and **g** alike.
::
