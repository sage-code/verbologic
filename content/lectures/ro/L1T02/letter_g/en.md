**G g** · /g/ or /dʒ/

Before **e** and **i**, **g** is *j* as in "gem": *girofar*. Everywhere else it is a hard *g*: *gust* (taste).

::callout{type="note"}
This e/i rule is the only conditional spelling rule in Romanian. It applies to **c** and **g** alike.
::
