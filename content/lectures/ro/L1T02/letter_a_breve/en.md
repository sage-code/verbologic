**Ă ă** · /ə/

A short central vowel, like the unstressed *a* at the start of English "again". It appears in the middle or at the end of a word: *măr* (apple), *casă* (house).

::callout{type="tip"}
The diacritics are not decoration. *patru* (four) and *pătru* are different words; dropping ă/â/î is the fastest way to be misunderstood in writing.
::
