Romanian does something English never dared: **it spells exactly what it speaks.** Every letter has one sound, every sound has one letter. There are no silent letters waiting to ambush you, no "though / through / tough" traps. Read the word and you have spoken it — correctly — the first time.

That is the single most useful fact about this language, and it changes how you should study it: **learn the 31 letters once, and the entire dictionary becomes pronounceable forever.**

Watch the video several times, pause often, and repeat each sound group aloud.
