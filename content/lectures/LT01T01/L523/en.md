## The letter name

The letter is named (dupla vé) /dupləveː/. Its main sound is the sound /v/, as in (WC) /ˈveːtseː/, which means toilet. This letter makes two sounds.

## Pronouncing the sounds

To make the first sound, place your top teeth gently against your bottom lip. Push air through while your vocal cords vibrate. For the second sound, round your lips tightly and pull your tongue back. Let your vocal cords vibrate as air flows smoothly out. Place your fingers on your throat to feel the strong buzz.

## Reading the variants

In almost all borrowed words and common terms, this letter makes the sound /v/. You hear it in (whisky) /ˈviski/. In some newer borrowed terms or names, some people pronounce it as the sound /w/. You hear this variation in (western) /ˈvɛstɛrn/.

## Avoiding mistakes

Do not read this letter as the sound in water when you see foreign words. Most speakers use the sound from very instead. Press your upper teeth against your lower lip to make the right buzz.
