## The letter equis

In Spanish, this letter is called (equis) /ˈekis/. Its main sound is the sound /s/, heard in (xilófono) /siˈlofono/, which means xylophone. This letter can make three different sounds.

## How to say equis

For the first sound, press the back of your tongue against the roof. Release the air sharply, then blow air through your front teeth without voice. For the second sound, hiss air through your front teeth. For the third sound, blow rough air from deep in your throat. Put your hand on your throat to check that your voice stays off.

## Spelling rules for equis

Between vowels, it makes the sound /ks/, as in (éxito) /ˈeksito/. At the end of a word, it also makes the sound /ks/, as in (tórax) /ˈtoɾaks/. Before a consonant, it makes the sound /s/, as in (texto) /ˈtesto/. In traditional place names, it sounds like the sound /x/, as in (México) /ˈmexiko/ or (Don Quixote) /doŋ kiˈxote/.

## Mistakes to avoid

Do not voice the sound between vowels as in English exact. Keep it crisp without vocal cord vibration, like the end of box. When it sits before a consonant, do not insert a full k sound. Pronounce it simply as a smooth hiss, just like the s in English best.
