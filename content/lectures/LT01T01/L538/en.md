## Sound of the letter ele

The letter (ele) /ˈɛli/ sounds mainly like the sound /l/, that is a light l. You hear this sound in (lua) /ˈlu.ɐ/, which means moon. This letter makes two different sounds.

## How to say the letter ele

For the first sound, place the tip of your tongue against your upper gum. Let the air pass freely around the sides of your tongue. For the second sound, keep the tip there and raise the back of your tongue toward the soft roof of your mouth. Touch your throat to feel your voice box vibrate continuously.

## Spelling of the letter ele

Before a vowel, the letter makes the sound /l/, that is a light l. You can hear it in (falar) /fɐˈlaɾ/. At the end of a syllable or word, it makes the sound /ɫ/, that is a dark l. You can hear it in (alto) /ˈaɫ.tu/, and in (sol) /sɔɫ/.

## Mistakes with the letter ele

Do not drop the dark sound into a w sound like in law. Keep your tongue tip firmly on your upper gum until the sound ends. Do not use a dark sound before vowels like in full. Keep the tongue flat and light, like in the English word leaf.
