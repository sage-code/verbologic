## Letter sound

The letter (i grec) /i ɡʁɛk/ makes the sound /j/. You hear it in (yeux) /jø/, which means eyes. This letter has two different sounds.

## Mouth shape

For the first sound, lift your tongue high toward the roof of your mouth. Keep your lips relaxed and let the voiced air glide quickly into the next vowel. For the other sound, spread your lips wide and smile. Keep your tongue very high and forward. Place a hand on your throat to feel continuous vibration.

## Reading rules

Before a vowel, it sounds like the sound /j/, as in (yoga) /jo.ɡa/. Between two consonants, it makes the sound /i/, as in (cycle) /sikl/. At the end of a word, it also makes the sound /i/, as in (jury) /ʒy.ʁi/.

## Common mistakes

Do not let this letter glide into a double sound like in the English word my. French vowels stay pure and steady. Keep your jaw still and avoid shifting your lips at the end of the sound.
