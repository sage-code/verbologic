## Sound of letter gê

The name of this letter is (gê) /ʒe/. Its primary sound is the sound /ɡ/, heard in the word (gato) /ˈɡa.tu/, which means cat. Across Portuguese words, this letter represents two distinct sounds.

## How to say gê

For the first sound, press the back of your tongue against the soft roof of your mouth. Release the air suddenly while your vocal cords vibrate. For the second sound, raise the blade of your tongue near the roof of your mouth. Blow air smoothly through the narrow gap with voice. Place a hand on your throat to feel the strong buzz.

## Spelling rules for gê

Before the vowels a, o, u, or before another consonant, say the sound /ɡ/, as in (grande) /ˈɡɾɐ̃.dɨ/. When the letter sits right before e or i, make the sound /ʒ/, as in (gente) /ˈʒẽ.tɨ/. You also use the sound /ʒ/ before i, as in the word (girafa) /ʒiˈɾa.fɐ/.

## Comparing gê with English

Do not make the sound in bridge or gem when reading this letter before e or i. Avoid that extra pop at the beginning. Keep the airflow continuous and buzzing, exactly like the middle sound in the English word measure.
