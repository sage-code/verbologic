## The letter name

The letter name is (í) /iː/. It makes the long sound /iː/, as heard in the word (íz) /ˈiːz/, which means taste. This letter has only one sound in Hungarian. You hear the same pure vowel sound in every position.

## Mouth shape

Spread your lips wide into a smile. Raise the front of your tongue close to the roof of your mouth. Keep the air flowing steadily and let your vocal cords vibrate. Hold the vowel steady for twice as long as a short vowel. Place two fingers on your throat to feel the steady vibration throughout the sound.

## Reading the vowel

The letter with an acute accent always makes the long sound /iː/ in all positions. You read it in the word (híd) /hiːd/. You also read it the same way in the word (tíz) /tiːz/.

## Avoiding mistakes

Do not let this vowel glide into a y-like ending as in the English word see. Hungarian long vowels remain completely steady from start to finish. Keep your tongue high and firmly fixed in place until the sound ends. Do not shorten it, because length changes the meaning of words.
