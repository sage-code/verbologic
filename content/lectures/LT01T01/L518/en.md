## Sound of letter u

The letter (u) /u/ is named (u) /u/. It represents the sound /u/. You hear this sound in the word (utca) /ˈut.tsɒ/, which means street. This letter makes only one sound.

## How to say u

Pull the back of your tongue high towards the roof of your mouth. Push your lips forward and make a small tight circle. Let your vocal cords vibrate as air flows out freely. Touch your throat to feel the steady buzz.

## Spelling of letter u

This letter always produces the same sound in every position. It sounds as the sound /u/ everywhere, like in the word (falu) /ˈfɒ.lu/.

## Comparing letter u

Do not pronounce this like the vowel in the English word cut. It is closer to the vowel in the English word boot. Keep your lips pushed forward into a small circle, and keep the vowel pure without gliding.
