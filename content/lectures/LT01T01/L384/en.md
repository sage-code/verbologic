## The letter name

The letter is called (эль) /ɛlʲ/. Its main sound is the sound /ɫ/, that is a hard l, as in the word (лампа) /ˈɫam.pə/, which means lamp. This letter makes two sounds.

## How to say it

For the first sound, touch your upper teeth with the tip of your tongue. Pull the back of your tongue upward and let your voice vibrate. For the second sound, press the flat blade of your tongue against the roof of your mouth. Keep the tip low behind your bottom teeth. Touch your throat to feel the strong vibration of your vocal cords.

## When to use each

This letter makes the sound /ɫ/, that is a hard l, before hard vowels, before consonants, or at the end of a word. You can hear it in (стол) /stoɫ/. It makes the sound /lʲ/, that is a soft l, before soft vowels or the soft sign. You can hear it in (лето) /ˈlʲe.tə/ and in (соль) /solʲ/.

## Common mistakes

Do not replace the hard sound with the light l in light. Keep the back of your tongue raised to make it dark and heavy, like the ending of ball. Do not pronounce the soft sound like the l in leaf. Flatten your tongue against the roof of your mouth, as if you are saying million.
