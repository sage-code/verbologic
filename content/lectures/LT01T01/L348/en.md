## The letter Ge

The letter is named (Ge) /ɡeː/. Its main sound is heard in the word (Garten) /ˈɡaʁtn̩/, which means garden. This letter makes four different sounds in the language.

## How to say it

For the first sound, press the back of your tongue against the soft roof of your mouth. Release the air with your voice. For the second sound, make the same movement without your voice. For the third sound, raise the middle of your tongue toward the hard roof. Blow air through the narrow gap. For the fourth sound, push your lips forward and make a gentle buzzing sound. Touch your throat to feel the voice.

## Spelling rules

At the start of a word or syllable, the letter makes the sound /ɡ/, like in (gut) /ɡuːt/. At the end of a word or syllable, it sounds like the sound /k/, as in (Tag) /taːk/. At the end of a word in the suffix with an i, it makes the soft sound /ç/, as in (könig) /ˈkøːnɪç/. In words borrowed from French, it makes the sound /ʒ/, like in (Garage) /ɡaˈʁaːʒə/.

## Common mistakes

At the end of a word, you might keep your voice on, as in the English word bag. Always end with a crisp sound like the k in back. In the ending with an i, do not make a hard k sound. Whisper air smoothly across the roof of your mouth instead.
