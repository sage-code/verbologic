## The group ough

The letters ough are called the sound /oʊ/. Their main sound is the sound /oʊ/, as in (though) /ðoʊ/, which means although. This letter group makes four different sounds in common everyday speech.

## How to say it

For the first sound, round your lips and move them inward into a smaller circle. For the second sound, push your lips forward into a tight ring. For the third sound, open your jaw wide and pull your tongue back. For the fourth sound, open your mouth slightly and touch your lower lip to your upper teeth.

## When to use it

The letters ough make the sound /oʊ/ in (dough) /doʊ/. They make the sound /uː/ in (through) /θruː/. They make the long sound /ɔː/ before t in (thought) /θɔːt/. They make the sound /ʌf/ with an f-sound in (rough) /rʌf/.

## Common mistakes

Do not speak every word with this pattern the same way. Check each word in a dictionary because the letters do not follow one single rule.
