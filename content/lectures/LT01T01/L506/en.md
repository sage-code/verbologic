## The letter enny

The letter (enny) /ɛɲ/ makes the sound /ɲ/. You hear it in the word (nyár) /ˈɲaːr/, which means summer. This letter has only one sound in Hungarian.

## How to say it

Press the broad middle part of your tongue flat against the roof of your mouth. Keep the tip of your tongue down behind your lower front teeth. Send air through your nose while your vocal cords vibrate. Touch your throat to feel the gentle buzz of your voice.

## Spelling rules

This letter always produces the same sound in every position. You pronounce it as the sound /ɲ/ at the start, middle, or end of a word. For example, listen to the word (anya) /ˈɒɲɒ/.

## Mistakes to avoid

Do not pronounce two separate sounds like the letters in the English word canyon. It is a single unified nasal sound. Make sure the middle of your tongue seals the roof of your mouth completely.
