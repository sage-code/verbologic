## Letter D sound

The letter (D) /diː/ makes the sound /d/. You hear it in the word (dog) /dɒɡ/, which means dog. The letter makes four different sounds in English words.

## How to say D

To make the first sound, place your tongue tip against the ridge behind your upper teeth. Stop the air briefly, release it quickly, and vibrate your vocal cords. For the second sound, make the same quick release with no voice. For the third sound, press your tongue flat behind your teeth, then pull it back as your voice buzzes. Touch your throat to feel the voice buzz.

## Spelling rules for D

Usually, this letter makes the sound /d/, as in (bed) /bɛd/. In regular past tense endings after a voiceless consonant, it sounds like /t/, as in (hoped) /həʊpt/. Before the letter u in certain words, it makes the sound /dʒ/, as in (educate) /ɛ.dʒʊ.keɪt/. In a few special words, the letter is silent and makes no sound at all, as in (handkerchief) /hæŋ.kə.tʃiːf/.

## Common mistakes with D

Do not turn the voiced sound into an unvoiced stop at the end of words. Keep your vocal cords vibrating so the ending does not sound like a sharp tap. In special words where the letter is silent, do not pronounce it at all.
