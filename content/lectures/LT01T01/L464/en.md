## The letter gé

The letter (gé) /ʒe/ mostly makes the sound /ɡ/, like in the word (gare) /ɡaʁ/, which means train station. This letter has two different spoken sounds in French. Sometimes it stays completely silent.

## How to say it

For the first sound, press the back of your tongue against the roof of your mouth. Release the air suddenly while using your voice. For the second sound, push your lips forward slightly into a soft circle. Blow air gently through your teeth while vibrating your vocal cords. Touch your throat to feel your voice buzz.

## Reading this letter

You say the sound /ɡ/ when the letter sits before a, o, u or a consonant, as in (gros) /ɡʁo/. You say the sound /ʒ/ before e, i or y, as heard in (gel) /ʒɛl/ and (girafe) /ʒi.ʁaf/. At the end of some words, the letter is not pronounced, like in (doigt) /dwa/ and (sang) /sɑ̃/.

## Common mistakes

Do not add a puff of air when you say the hard sound. Keep the sound /ɡ/ crisp and smooth. Do not add a d sound before the soft variant. You must say the sound like the middle of the English word measure, not like the start of jump.
