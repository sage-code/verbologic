## The letter sound

The letter name is (ы) /ɨ/, that is a close central vowel. This letter makes the vowel sound in (сын) /sɨn/, which means son. It has two sounds in spoken Russian.

## Making the sound

Pull your tongue back into the middle of your mouth. Keep your lips flat and spread slightly. Vibrate your vocal cords with a tense throat. For the unstressed sound, relax your tongue slightly downwards. Touch your throat to feel the strong steady vibration.

## Reading the variants

In stressed syllables after hard consonants, say the sound /ɨ/, that is a close central vowel, as in (дым) /dɨm/. In unstressed syllables, make the shorter sound /ɨ̞/, that is a lower central vowel, as in (рыбак) /rɨ̞ˈbak/.

## Common mistakes

Do not say the sound in the English word sit. Do not make the sound in the English word see. Pull your tongue farther back in your mouth instead.
