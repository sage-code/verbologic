## Sound of G

The letter (G) /dʒiː/ usually makes the sound /ɡ/, like in (gas) /ɡæs/, meaning gas. This letter has three spoken sounds in English, and it can also be silent.

## How to say G

To make the first sound, press the back of your tongue against the soft roof of your mouth. Release the air suddenly and make a sound with your throat. For the second sound, press your tongue behind your front teeth, push air through, and use your voice. For the third sound, push your lips out, keep the air flowing steadily, and vibrate your vocal cords. Touch your throat to feel the vibration.

## Spelling rules for G

You use the sound /ɡ/ before the vowels a, o, or u, before consonants, and at the end of words, as in (dog) /dɒɡ/. The sound /dʒ/ often appears before the vowels e, i, or y, as in (gem) /dʒɛm/ and (giant) /dʒaɪ.ənt/. Words borrowed from French use the sound /ʒ/ before e or i, such as (regime) /reɪ.ʒiːm/ and (genre) /ʒɒn.rə/. The letter is silent before n or m, as seen in (sign) /saɪn/ and (gnat) /næt/.

## Common mistakes with G

Do not drop the voice when you say the hard sound at the end of words. Keep your vocal cords buzzing so it does not sound like a k. When you say the soft sound, make sure your tongue touches the roof of your mouth first. Do not glide softly into it. Finally, remember to keep the letter completely silent when it stands directly before the letter n.
