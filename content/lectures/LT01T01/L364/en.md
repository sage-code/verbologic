## The letter We

The letter (We) /veː/ is named with a long vowel. It makes the sound /v/ as in (Wasser) /ˈvasɐ/, which means water. This letter makes one single sound in German words. You will hear it clearly in many common words.

## How to say We

To make this sound, rest your upper front teeth lightly against your lower lip. Blow air gently through the small gap between them. Turn on your voice box so that you make a clear buzzing sound. Touch your throat with two fingers to feel the steady vibration.

## Spelling of We

This letter stands for the sound /v/ in almost every regular German word. It appears at the beginning of words and inside them. An example is the word (zwei) /tsvaɪ̯/. The spelling always gives this exact voiced sound.

## Common mistakes with We

Do not pronounce this letter like the first sound in the English word water. Your lips must never form a small round circle. Use the sound of the letter in the English word vine instead. Keep your upper teeth firmly against your lower lip.
