## The letter Be

The letter (Be) /beː/ sounds like the start of (Buch) /buːx/, meaning book. This letter makes two different sounds in German.

## Making the sounds

For the first sound, press your lips together tightly. Release a gentle puff of air while your voice hums. Touch your throat to feel that vibration. For the other sound, keep your lips in the exact same spot. Push the air out firmly without any voice. Your throat stays completely still.

## Spelling rules

At the start of a word or syllable before a vowel, you pronounce the sound /b/. You can hear this in (Ball) /bal/. At the end of a syllable or word, the sound changes to the sound /p/. You can hear this in (ab) /ap/, and also in (gelb) /ɡɛlp/.

## Common mistakes

At the end of words, you might want to use a soft voiced sound like in the English word club. Do not hum with your throat there. Make it completely quiet and crisp like the final sound in the English word tap.
