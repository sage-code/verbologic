## Sound of the letter

The letter (A) /eɪ/ makes the sound /æ/ in the word (apple) /æ.pəl/, which means apple. This letter has four different sounds in English words.

## How to say it

Open your mouth wide and flatten your tongue low in your mouth. Next, glide from an open vowel to a soft smile sound. Then, relax your lips and tongue completely to make a weak, quick murmur. Finally, open your jaw wide and pull your tongue back without rounding your lips. Place a hand on your throat to feel your voice buzz on each sound.

## Spelling rules

In closed syllables with consonants after it, this letter makes the sound /æ/ as in (cat) /kæt/. In open syllables or before an ending silent e, it sounds like the sound /eɪ/ as in (make) /meɪk/. In weak, unstressed syllables, it softens to the murmur sound /ə/ as in (about) /ə.baʊt/. Before the letter r or in open foreign words, it makes the long back sound /ɑː/ as in (father) /fɑː.ðər/.

## Common mistakes

Do not confuse the short flat sound with the sound in pet. Drop your jaw lower than you think. Do not replace the long vowel with a pure sound. Make sure your tongue moves from open to tight at the end. Keep the unstressed sound very weak and quick.
