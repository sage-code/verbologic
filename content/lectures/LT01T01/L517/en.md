## Letter sound

You call this letter (tyé) /ceː/. Its main sound is the sound /c/, heard in the word (tyúk) /ˈcuːk/, which means hen. This letter has only one sound.

## How to say it

Press the middle of your tongue against the roof of your mouth. Keep the tip down behind your bottom front teeth. Build air behind the tongue, then drop it cleanly away. Do not vibrate your vocal cords. Hold a finger before your lips to feel a sharp burst of breath.

## Reading rules

This letter always makes the sound /c/ everywhere in a word. You hear it inside the word (kutya) /ˈkucɒ/.

## Common mistakes

Do not say this like the first sound in cheese. That sound rubs air through teeth, while this sound is a clean stop. Do not say two separate sounds like the start of tune. Keep your tongue wide and still against the roof until you release it.
