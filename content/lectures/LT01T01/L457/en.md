## Letter zeta sound

The letter (zeta) /ˈθeta/ makes the sound /θ/. You hear it in (zapato) /θaˈpa.to/, which means shoe. This letter has only one sound in standard Spanish from Spain.

## How to say zeta

Put the tip of your tongue lightly between your upper and lower front teeth. Blow air gently through the small gap without vibrating your vocal cords. You can feel cool air flowing across your tongue tip.

## Reading zeta in words

This letter always makes the sound /θ/ in standard European Spanish. You can find it at the end of a word like (luz) /luθ/.

## Mistakes to avoid

Do not buzz this sound like the English letter z in zoo. Also, do not use the hissing s sound from sun. Keep your tongue between your teeth and blow soft air.
