## Sound of letter X

You call this letter (X) /eks/. It usually makes the sound /ks/, as in (box) /bɒks/, which means box. This letter can make three different sounds.

## How to say X

Lift the back of your tongue, release a burst of air, then blow air through your teeth. For the next sound, repeat this while buzzing your vocal cords. For the last sound, keep your tongue tip near your upper teeth and buzz your voice. Touch your throat to feel the buzz.

## Spelling rules for X

At the end of a syllable or word, it sounds like /ks/, as in (six) /sɪks/. It makes the sound /ɡz/ between vowels before a stressed syllable, as in (exam) /ɪɡ.zæm/. At the start of a word, it sounds like /z/, as in (xylophone) /zaɪ.lə.fəʊn/.

## Common mistakes with X

Do not drop the first stop sound at the end of a word. Make sure you do not say two sounds at the start of a word. Start words with a plain buzz, just like the sound in zoo.
