## Sound of a

The letter (a) /a/ is named (a) /a/. It makes the sound /a/, like in the word (amore) /aˈmo.re/, which means love. This letter always makes only one sound.

## How to say a

Open your mouth wide and let your jaw drop comfortably. Keep your tongue low and flat on the floor of your mouth. Let your lips stay neutral and relaxed. Vibrate your voice freely as air passes through your mouth. Place a hand on your throat to feel the strong buzzing.

## Spelling of a

This letter always makes the sound /a/ in every position, as in the word (casa) /ˈka.za/.

## Mistakes with a

Do not pronounce this sound like the weak murmur at the end of comma. Do not say it like the vowel in cat. Keep your mouth opened wide and clear, just like the open vowel in father.
