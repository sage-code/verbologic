## The letter name

The letter name is (бэ) /bɛ/. Its main sound is heard in (брат) /brat/, which means brother. This letter makes four different sounds in speech.

## Making the sounds

For the first sound, press your lips tight and voice the burst. For the second sound, arch your tongue high toward the roof while releasing voiced lips. For the third sound, close your lips and blow out air with no voice. For the fourth sound, arch your tongue forward while popping air out silently. Touch your throat to feel when your vocal cords buzz.

## Reading this letter

This letter sounds like the sound /b/, that is a hard b, in (бык) /bɨk/. Before softening vowels or a soft sign, it sounds like the sound /bʲ/, that is a soft b, in (белый) /ˈbʲe.ɫɨj/. At the end of a word, it sounds like the sound /p/, that is a hard p, in (лоб) /lop/. Before a soft sign at word end, it gives the sound /pʲ/, that is a soft p, in (дробь) /dropʲ/.

## Avoiding mistakes

Do not puff out a heavy breath when you speak the voiceless sounds. Keep your lips tighter than in the English word bat. For the softened sounds, raise the middle of your tongue at the same moment.
