## Letter sound

French spells this letter as (vé) /ve/. It makes the sound /v/. You hear it clearly in the word (vélo) /ve.lo/, which means bicycle. Across French words, this letter has only one sound.

## How to say it

Press your top front teeth gently against your lower lip. Blow air smoothly through that small gap. Turn your voice on at the same time. Place your fingers on your throat to feel the strong buzz.

## Spelling rules

This letter always makes the sound /v/ in French words. You hear it at the start of a word, as in (vent) /vɑ̃/. You also hear it in the middle of words.

## Common mistakes

This sound matches the first sound in the English word vine. Do not round your lips as you would for the letter w in water. Keep your upper teeth firmly on your lower lip so the sound never slips.
