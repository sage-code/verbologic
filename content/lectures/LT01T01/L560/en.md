## Letter sound

The letter (H) /eɪtʃ/ makes the sound /h/. You hear it in the word (hat) /hæt/, which means a hat. This letter makes one spoken sound, but sometimes stays completely quiet.

## Mouth movements

Open your mouth slightly. Keep your tongue relaxed low in your mouth. Push air gently up from your throat. Do not vibrate your vocal cords. Hold a hand before your lips to feel a soft puff of warm air.

## Spelling rules

This letter makes the sound /h/ at the start of a syllable before a vowel, as in (help) /hɛlp/. In a few words, the letter is not pronounced at all at the start, as in (hour) /aʊ.ər/ and (honest) /ɒn.ɪst/.

## Common mistakes

Do not drop the breath at the start of words. Push a soft puff of air so you do not say words with an open vowel. Do not scrape your throat tightly. Keep the air flow gentle and smooth.
