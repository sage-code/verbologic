## Sound of letter tê

The name of this letter is (tê) /te/. It makes one single sound, heard in (tempo) /ˈtẽ.pu/, meaning time or weather.

## How to say it

Touch the back of your upper front teeth with the tip of your tongue. Stop the air completely for a brief moment. Release the tongue cleanly without puffing out a breath of air. Keep your vocal cords silent. You can hold your hand before your mouth to feel no burst of air.

## Reading rules

This letter always makes the sound /t/ in every position. You hear it at the end of (noite) /ˈnoj.tɨ/.

## Common mistakes

In English words like tea, you puff out air. Keep your breath back completely. Never turn this sound into the ch sound in cheap when an e follows. Place the tongue tip strictly against your upper teeth.
