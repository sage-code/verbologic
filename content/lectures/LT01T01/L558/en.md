## Sound of F

The letter (F) /ef/ makes the sound /f/, as in the word (fish) /fɪʃ/. This word means a creature that swims in water. This letter can make two different sounds in English words.

## How to Say F

Gently place your top teeth on your bottom lip. Blow air smoothly through the small gap between them. Do not use your voice for this sound. Place your hand on your neck to check for no vibrations. For the voiced sound, keep the same mouth shape, but make a steady humming sound with your voice.

## Reading F

In almost every English word, this letter sounds like /f/. You can hear this sound at the beginning of the word (fast) /fæst/. You also hear this sound at the end of the word (leaf) /liːf/. The main exception is one very common small word. In the word (of) /əv/, this letter sounds like the voiced sound /v/.

## Common Mistakes

Do not close both lips together when you make this sound. Keep your top teeth lightly touching your lower lip so air flows freely. Do not confuse this sound with the sound /p/ or the sound /b/. Remember to turn off your voice when you say the regular sound.
