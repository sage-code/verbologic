## Sound of letter essz

The letter (essz) /ɛs/ makes the sound /s/. You hear this sound in the word (szem) /ˈsɛm/, which means eye. This letter has two sounds in Hungarian speech.

## How to say essz

For the first sound, place your tongue tip near your upper teeth. Blow air through the narrow gap without using your voice. For the second sound, make the exact same mouth shape, but turn your voice on. Place your fingers on your throat to feel the strong vibration.

## Spelling rules for essz

In regular positions, this letter makes the sound /s/, as in (tavasz) /ˈtɒvɒs/. When it stands before a voiced sound, it changes to the sound /z/. You can hear this change in the word (szembogár) /ˈzɛmboɡaːr/ and in the word (részben) /ˈreːzbɛn/.

## Mistakes to avoid

Do not read this letter as the sound in the English word shoe. Instead, always make the clean hiss heard in the English word sun. When it voices, do not keep the hissing sound, but let it buzz like the consonant in the English word zoo.
