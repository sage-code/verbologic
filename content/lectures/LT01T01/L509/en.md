## Letter sound

The name of this letter is (ö) /ø/. It makes the sound /ø/, as in the word (öt) /øt/, which means five. This letter has only one sound in Hungarian.

## How to say it

Shape your lips into a tight circle. Keep your tongue pushed forward, near your lower teeth. Let your voice buzz smoothly. Touch your throat to feel the strong vibration.

## Reading this letter

This letter always makes the sound /ø/ wherever it appears. For example, you hear it in the word (kör) /kør/.

## Common mistakes

Do not say the sound in the English word bird. Round your lips firmly, and push your tongue forward.
