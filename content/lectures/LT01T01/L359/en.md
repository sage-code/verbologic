## The letter Er

The letter (Er) /ɛʁ/ has two sounds. Its main sound is the consonant in (Rot) /ʁoːt/, which means red. You also hear it as a soft vowel at the end of syllables.

## Making the sounds

For the first sound, pull the back of your tongue toward your throat. Push air through the small gap to make a gentle friction. Your vocal cords vibrate. For the second sound, relax your tongue and open your mouth slightly. Make a short, low vowel sound. Put your fingers on your throat to feel the vibration.

## Reading the letter

At the start of a word or between vowels, make the sound /ʁ/. You hear this in (hören) /ˈhøːʁən/. At the end of a syllable or after a long vowel, use the soft vowel sound /ɐ/. You hear this in (Uhr) /uːɐ̯/ and in (Vater) /ˈfaːtɐ/.

## Avoiding mistakes

Do not curl your tongue tip back like in the English word red. Keep your tongue tip resting down behind your lower front teeth instead. For the vowel sound at the end, do not make the hard ending of water. Keep your mouth relaxed and make a short, open sound.
