## Letter sound

The letter (ene) /ˈene/ sounds like the start of (noche) /ˈno.t͡ʃe/, which means night. This Spanish letter makes four different sounds.

## How to say

Touch the ridge behind your upper teeth with your tongue tip. Let voiced air flow through your nose. Close both lips completely to let voiced air pass through your nose. Press the back of your tongue against the soft roof of your mouth. Press the flat middle of your tongue against your hard roof. Place a hand on your throat to feel continuous vibration.

## Spelling rules

Before vowels and at the start of a word, it sounds like /n/ as in (mano) /ˈma.no/. Before the letters p, b or v, it sounds like /m/ as in (un beso) /um ˈbe.so/. Before c, qu, g or j, it makes the sound /ŋ/ as in (cinco) /ˈθiŋ.ko/. Before ch, y or ll, it sounds like /ɲ/ as in (ancho) /ˈaɲ.t͡ʃo/.

## Common mistakes

You might keep the tip of your tongue raised before letters like p or b. Do not keep your lips open there. Close your lips fully as you would in the English word input. Before sounds made at the back of the mouth, do not keep the tongue fronted. Let your tongue shift to match the next sound smoothly.
