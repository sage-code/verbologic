## Letter sound kú

The name of this letter is (kú) /kuː/. Its main sound is heard in (quasi) /ˈkvɒzi/, which means quasi, as if. This letter has only one sound.

## How to say kú

Raise the back of your tongue against the soft roof of your mouth. Block the air briefly, then release it sharply with no voice. Put a hand on your throat to feel no vibration.

## Reading the letter kú

This letter appears only in foreign loanwords and names, followed by the letter u. It always makes the sound /k/, as in (Aquincum) /ˈɒkviŋkum/.

## Common mistakes with kú

You might want to pronounce this letter like the sound in the English word quick. In Hungarian, the following letter u sounds like the v in vine, not like w.
