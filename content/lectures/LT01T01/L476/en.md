## The letter esse

The letter (esse) /ɛs/ makes the sound /s/ in the word (sac) /sak/, which means bag. This letter has three different sounds across French words.

## Making the sounds

For the first sound, place your tongue tip near your upper teeth. Push air through the small gap without your voice. For the second sound, keep the same mouth shape, but turn your voice on. Touch your throat to feel the strong buzz. For the third variant, keep your mouth still and make no sound at all.

## Reading this letter

At the start of a word or next to a consonant, it makes the sound /s/ as in (poste) /pɔst/. When it stands between two vowels, it makes the sound /z/ as in (rose) /ʁoz/. At the end of many words, you do not pronounce this letter, as in (bras) /bʁa/.

## Common mistakes

Do not buzz the letter between vowels if you want the sound in sun. When this letter appears between vowels, always pronounce it like the buzzing sound in zero. At the ends of most words, remember to stay silent, unlike English plurals such as cats or dogs.
