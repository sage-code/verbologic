## Sound of the letter

The letter (G) /dʒ/ takes its name. It makes the main sound /ɡ/ as in the word (gas) /ɡæs/, which means gas. This letter can make four sounds in total.

## How to say it

Press the back of your tongue against the soft roof of your mouth. Release the air suddenly with your voice on. For the next sound, touch your tongue tip behind your upper teeth. Push air through while your voice buzzes. Next, push air smoothly through flat lips with voice. Put a hand on your throat to feel the vibration.

## Reading rules

Before the letters a, o, u or consonants, this letter sounds like /ɡ/ in (gap) /ɡæp/. Often before the letters e, i or y, it gives the sound /dʒ/ in (gem) /dʒɛm/. In words borrowed from French, it makes the sound /ʒ/ in (genre) /ʒɑn.rə/. Before the letter n at the start or end of a root, the letter is not pronounced, like in (gnat) /næt/.

## Common mistakes

Do not drop your voice on the hard sound. If you switch off your vocal cords, you make the sound of the letter k. Keep your throat buzzing all the way through the release. Also do not pronounce this letter before n when it begins a word.
