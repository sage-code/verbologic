## Sound of dzé

The name of this letter is (dzé) /dzeː/. Its primary sound is the sound /ˈd͡ʒuŋɡɛl/, heard in the word (dzsungel) /ˈd͡ʒuŋɡɛl/, which means jungle. This letter makes two distinct sounds in Hungarian.

## Mouth shape

For the first sound, place your tongue tip against the ridge behind your upper teeth. Release the air slowly with vibration from your vocal cords. For the second sound, pull your tongue slightly further back against the roof of your mouth. Push the air through firmly while your vocal cords vibrate. Put your fingers on your throat to feel the strong buzzing.

## Spelling patterns

In native roots and everyday loanwords, this letter makes the sound /ˈbod͡zɒ/, as in the word (bodza) /ˈbod͡zɒ/. When followed by the letter s, it combines to produce the sound /ˈd͡ʒɛki/, as in the word (dzseki) /ˈd͡ʒɛki/.

## Common traps

Do not split the first sound into two separate letters. It is a single smooth blend, like the middle of the English word kids. For the second sound, avoid an unvoiced puff of air. Keep your vocal cords buzzing steadily, exactly like the first consonant in the English word jam.
