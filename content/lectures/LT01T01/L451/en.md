## Letter te sound

The letter (te) /te/ makes the sound /t/. You hear this sound in the word (tiempo) /ˈtjem.po/, which means time, weather. This letter has only one sound in standard speech.

## How to say letter te

Press the tip of your tongue against the back of your top front teeth. Stop the air completely, then release it quickly without puffing out air. Your vocal cords do not vibrate. Hold your hand before your mouth. You should feel no burst of breath when you release the air.

## Reading letter te

This letter always makes the sound /t/ in all positions. You hear it at the start of the word (taza) /ˈta.θa/. You also hear it in the middle of the word (patio) /ˈpa.tjo/.

## Pitfalls with letter te

Do not puff out a burst of air as you do in top. Keep the release crisp and dry. Do not place your tongue behind your teeth on the gum ridge. Always touch the upper teeth directly.
