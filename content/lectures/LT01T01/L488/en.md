## Sound of csé

The letter (csé) /tʃeː/ makes the sound /t͡ʃ/ in (csillag) /ˈtʃil.lɒɡ/, which means star. This letter makes two sounds in Hungarian.

## How to say it

To make the first sound, touch the roof of your mouth behind your upper teeth. Release the air with a soft burst. Keep your voice quiet. To make the second sound, shape your mouth the same way. Turn on your voice. Put a hand on your throat to feel the buzz.

## Reading the letter

Most of the time, this letter sounds like the sound /t͡ʃ/, as in (kocsi) /ˈkot͡ʃi/. Before certain voiced consonants, it changes to the sound /d͡ʒ/, as in (tanácsba) /ˈtɒnaːd͡ʒbɒ/.

## Common mistakes

Do not soften this letter into the sound in sheet. Make a clean, crisp stop like the sound in cheese. When it turns voiced, match the sound in jump.
