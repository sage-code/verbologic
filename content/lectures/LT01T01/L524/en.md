## The letter iksz

The letter (iksz) /iks/ is pronounced with two sounds in Hungarian. Its main sound is the cluster /ks/. You hear this in the word (xilofon) /ˈksilofon/, which means xylophone.

## Pronouncing the sounds

For the first sound, press the back of your tongue against the roof of your mouth. Release the air without your voice, then push air through slightly open teeth. For the second sound, make that exact same combination, but vibrate your vocal cords throughout. Put your fingers on your throat to feel the strong buzz on the second sound.

## When sounds change

This letter usually sounds like the cluster /ks/. This happens at the start of words and inside borrowed words, like (taxi) /ˈtɒksi/. Between two vowels in certain borrowed words, it sounds like the cluster /ɡz/. You hear this pattern in the word (egzotikus) /ˈɛɡzotikuʃ/.

## Avoiding common errors

At the start of words, do not say a single buzzing sound like the first sound in zebra. Always begin clearly with the sound /k/ followed by the sound /s/. Also, do not drop the first sound. Say both parts crisply.
