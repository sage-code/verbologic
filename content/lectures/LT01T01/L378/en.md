## Sound of yo

The letter (ё) /jo/ is named (ё) /jo/. Its main sound appears in the word (ёлка) /ˈjɵlkə/, which means fir tree, Christmas tree. This letter can make three sounds depending on where it appears. It is always stressed in standard Russian words.

## How to say yo

To make the first sound, lift your tongue high like a y sound. Then move your tongue forward and round your lips tightly. For the second sound, keep your tongue forward and round your lips into a tight circle. For the third sound, pull your tongue back and make your lips round. Place a hand on your throat to feel your voice buzz throughout all three sounds.

## Spelling of yo

At the start of a word or after another vowel, you say the sound /jɵ/, as in (ёж) /jɵʂ/. After most consonants, the letter softens that consonant and sounds like the sound /ɵ/, as in (мёд) /mʲɵt/. After hard hissing consonants, it sounds like the sound /o/, as in (жёлтый) /ˈʐoltɨj/.

## Comparing yo to English

Do not turn this vowel into the glide in the English word boat. Keep your lips held in one fixed circle without sliding toward a w sound. Do not drop the soft sound of the consonant before it. Press the flat middle of your tongue against the roof of your mouth before you shape the vowel.
