## Sound of letter enn

The name of this letter sounds like (enn) /ɛn/. Its standard pronunciation is the sound /n/, heard in the word (nap) /ˈnɒp/, which means sun or day. This letter produces two different sounds in spoken words.

## How to say enn

Press your tongue tip against the ridge behind your upper front teeth. Let air flow out through your nose while your vocal cords vibrate. For the second sound, press the back of your tongue against the soft roof of your mouth. Send the air through your nose with vibrating vocal cords. Touch your throat to feel the strong buzz.

## Spelling rules for enn

In most positions, you pronounce this letter as the sound /n/, as in (anya) /ˈɒɲɒ/. It changes to the sound /ŋ/ directly before the letters k or g. You can hear this sound before a letter g in the word (hang) /ˈhɒŋɡ/. You can also hear it before a letter k in the word (bank) /ˈbɒŋk/.

## Mistakes to avoid

You might drop the following consonant after the nasal sound, as in the English word sing. Always pronounce the following consonant clearly. Do not swallow the sound at the end of a syllable. Keep your tongue pressed firmly against your teeth ridge until the sound finishes cleanly.
