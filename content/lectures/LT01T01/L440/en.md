## Letter sound jota

The name of this letter is (jota) /ˈxota/. It makes the sound /x/, as heard in (jugar) /xuˈɣaɾ/, which means to play. This letter always represents just one single sound in Spanish.

## How to say jota

Raise the back of your tongue toward the soft roof of your mouth. Leave a narrow gap so air rubs through with friction. Do not vibrate your vocal cords. You can feel a steady stream of warm air against your hand held before your lips.

## Spelling rules for jota

This letter always makes the sound /x/ in every position. You can hear it at the end of the word (reloj) /reˈlox/.

## Mistakes to avoid with jota

Do not pronounce this letter like the soft sound in the English word house. Also avoid using the sound in English jump. Push air strongly through the back of your mouth, just like the sound in Scottish loch.
