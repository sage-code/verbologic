## Letter sound

The letter (T) /ʃ/ makes the sound /t/ in (table) /teɪ.bəl/, which means table. This letter has three sounds.

## How to say it

For the first sound, press your tongue tip behind your top teeth. Release a small puff of air with no voice. For the second sound, press your tongue tip higher. Then push air through gently, like the start of church. Put your hand on your throat. Your vocal cords do not vibrate.

## Spelling rules

This letter usually makes the sound /t/ at the start of a word, as in (ten) /tɛn/. Before an unstressed u, it makes the sound /tʃ/, as in (nature) /neɪ.tʃər/. In groups like sten or stle, it is silent, as in (castle) /kæ.səl/.

## Common mistakes

Do not voice the first sound into a d. Keep your vocal cords still and let a quick puff of air escape. Do not say the silent letter in words like (listen) /lɪ.sən/. Drop the sound completely.
