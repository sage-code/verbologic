## Sound of esse

The letter (esse) /ˈɛsi/ makes the sound /s/ in the word (sol) /ˈsɔɫ/, which means sun. This letter has four different sounds in European Portuguese. Each sound depends on the position in the word and the letters around it.

## How to say esse

For the first sound, place your tongue tip near your upper teeth. Blow air through the narrow gap without voice. For the second sound, keep the same mouth shape, but turn your voice on. For the third sound, pull your tongue back and push air forward softly. For the fourth sound, use that back shape with your voice turned on. Touch your throat to feel the buzzing sounds.

## Spelling rules for esse

At the start of a word, it sounds like /s/, as in (sapo) /ˈsa.pu/. Doubled between vowels, it also makes the sound /s/, as in (passo) /ˈpa.su/. A single letter between vowels makes the sound /z/, as in (casa) /ˈka.zɐ/. At the end of a syllable before an unvoiced consonant, it sounds like /ʃ/, as in (festa) /ˈfɛʃ.tɐ/. At the very end of a word, it also makes /ʃ/, as in (português) /puɾ.tuˈɡeʃ/. Before a voiced consonant, it sounds like /ʒ/, as in (mesmo) /ˈmeʒ.mu/.

## Common mistakes with esse

You might want to pronounce the final letter as the sound in bus. Do not do that. Make the sound of ship instead. Before a voiced consonant, you might make the sound in zoo. Instead, make the buzzing sound found in the middle of vision.
