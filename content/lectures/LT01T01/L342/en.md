## The letter A

You call this letter (A) /aː/. Its main sound is heard in the word (Apfel) /ˈapfəl/, which means apple. This letter makes two sounds in German.

## How to say it

For the first sound, drop your jaw wide. Keep your tongue low and flat. Let your voice flow freely with unshaped lips. For the second sound, open your mouth wide. Pull your tongue slightly back and hold the tone longer. You can place a hand on your throat to feel your vocal cords vibrate.

## Spelling rules

Before two consonants, the letter makes the sound /a/, that is a short open a. A good example is (Kamm) /kam/. In an open stressed syllable or before the letter h, it sounds long. There it makes the sound /aː/, that is a long held a, as in (Abend) /ˈaːbənt/.

## Common mistakes

Do not replace the short vowel with the flat sound in the English word cat. Keep your mouth open wider and your tongue flat. Do not swallow the long sound into the vowel heard in father. Hold the sound clean without moving your tongue.
