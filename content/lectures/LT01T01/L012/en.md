## The sound of Î

The letter **Î** represents the close central unrounded vowel `/ɨ/`. It is phonetically identical to the Romanian letter **Â**, sounding quite similar to the unstressed vowel in the English word *roses* or the Russian letter *ы*. It differs from English `/i/` because the tongue is drawn backward rather than pushed forward against the teeth.

## How to pronounce Î

Prepare your mouth as if you are about to pronounce the vowel `/i/` as in *machine*, keeping your lips completely relaxed and unrounded. Without rounding your lips, pull the body of your tongue further back toward the center of your mouth. Produce voice while keeping your mouth slightly open, creating a tight, central, somewhat guttural vowel sound.

## Spelling rules for Î

Under standard Romanian orthography, **Î** is used almost exclusively at the very beginning and at the very end of words, such as in *înger* or *a coborî*. It is also retained inside words if an initial **î** becomes medial due to a prefix, as in *neîntrerupt*. Everywhere else inside root words, the identical sound is spelled with **Â**.

## Variants and common pitfalls

The letter **Î** always represents `/ɨ/` with no alternate pronunciations in standard speech. Learners commonly mistake it for `/i/` (like in *see*) or confuse it with `/ə/` (spelled **Ă**). Be careful not to round your lips as if pronouncing German *ü*, and ensure the tongue is centered rather than fronted.
