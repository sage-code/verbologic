## The group ough

The letters ough make the sound /ɔː/ in (bought) /bɔːt/. This word means you paid money for something. The group can make four different sounds.

## How to say it

Open your mouth wide and make a circle with your lips for the first sound. For the next sound, begin with a round mouth and pull your lips together. For the third sound, push your lips forward into a small tight ring. For the last sound, keep your mouth open, then touch your lower lip to your upper teeth with blowing air. Place your fingers on your throat to feel voice for the vowels.

## Reading the spelling

Before t in past words, the group makes the sound /ɔː/ as in (thought) /θɔːt/. At the end of some words, it makes the sound /oʊ/ as in (though) /ðoʊ/. It makes the long sound /uː/ in (through) /θruː/. In other words, it sounds like /ʌf/ as in (rough) /rʌf/.

## Common mistakes

Do not speak every letter in this group. The letters g and h are often completely silent. Remember that the vowel changes between words, so you must learn each word alone.
