## The Russian letter kha

The letter (ха) /xa/ makes the sound /x/, that is a hard rasping breath, heard in the word (хлеб) /xlʲep/, which means bread. This letter has two different sounds.

## Pronouncing the letter kha

Lift the back of your tongue toward the roof of your mouth. Blow air steadily through the narrow gap without stopping the airflow. Keep your vocal cords completely still. For the soft version, raise the middle of your tongue closer to the roof as you breathe out. Put a hand on your throat to make sure it does not buzz.

## Reading the letter kha

Before consonants or non front vowels, you say the sound /x/, that is a hard rasping breath, as in (хорошо) /xərəˈʂo/. Before front vowels and the soft sign, you say the sound /xʲ/, that is a soft rasping breath, as in (химия) /ˈxʲimʲɪjə/.

## Avoiding common English errors

Do not replace this sound with the sound in the English word cat. Keep the airflow continuous and never let your tongue touch the roof of your mouth. Do not use the sound in the English word hat. Always make the breath scrape firmly at the back of your mouth.
