## Sound of eme

The letter (eme) /ˈeme/ makes the sound /m/. You can hear it in (mano) /ˈma.no/, which means hand. This letter always makes only one sound in Spanish.

## How to say eme

Close both lips firmly together. Let your voice hum through your throat. Send the stream of air out through your nose. Place your hand on your neck to feel the steady buzz.

## Reading eme

This letter always makes the sound /m/ in all positions. Say this sound at the beginning of a syllable or at the end of a syllable, as in (campo) /ˈkam.po/.

## Pitfalls with eme

This sound is identical to the English sound in mother. You do not need to change anything about your mouth or your tongue. There are no special traps or pitfalls.
