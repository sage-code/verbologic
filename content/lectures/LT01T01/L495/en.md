## Sound of the letter gé

The letter (gé) /ɡeː/ has two sounds. Its main sound is the sound /ɡ/, like in the word (gomb) /ˈɡomb/, which means button.

## How to say it

To make the first sound, press the back of your tongue against the soft roof of your mouth. Release the air suddenly and turn on your voice. For the second sound, make the exact same tongue movement, but do not use your voice. Touch your throat with your hand to feel the vibration stop.

## Spelling rules

In most places, this letter makes the sound /ɡ/, like in the word (ág) /ˈaːɡ/. It changes to the sound /k/ when it stands directly before a voiceless consonant. You hear this sound change in the word (megfog) /ˈmɛkfoɡ/.

## Common mistakes

You might soften this letter before front vowels into the sound in English gem. Always keep it hard, like the first sound in garden. Also, do not forget to turn off your voice before voiceless sounds. Say it like the sound in bake, not bag.
