## Letter emme sound

The French letter (emme) /ɛm/ makes the sound /m/. You hear this sound in the word (mer) /mɛʁ/, which means sea. This letter has two spoken variants.

## Saying the sound

Close both lips firmly together. Let your vocal cords vibrate with your voice. Let the air flow out freely through your nose. Keep your hand on your throat to feel the strong hum.

## Spelling rules

This letter makes the sound /m/ before any vowel or another identical letter, as in (pomme) /pɔm/. It is not pronounced in a few special words, such as (damner) /dɑ.ne/.

## Common mistakes

This sound matches the English sound in mother. You already pronounce this sound naturally, so there is no real pitfall to avoid.
