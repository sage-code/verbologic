## Sound of letter é

The name of this letter is (é) /eː/. It represents the sound /eː/, like in the word (ég) /eːɡ/, which means sky or burn. This letter has only one sound.

## How to say é

Spread your lips wide into a smile. Raise the front of your tongue close to the roof of your mouth. Hold your mouth steady and do not move your lips or tongue. Let your vocal cords vibrate steadily. Touch your throat with your hand to feel the strong and steady buzzing.

## Spelling of letter é

This letter always keeps its one sound in all regular positions. It represents the sound /eː/ in the word (kép) /keːp/.

## Comparing letter é

Learners often turn this sound into a gliding vowel, like the sound in cake. Do not move your tongue up at the end. Keep your tongue completely still and hold one pure sound.
