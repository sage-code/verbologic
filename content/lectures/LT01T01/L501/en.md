## The letter ká

The letter (ká) /kaː/ has two sounds. Its basic sound is the sound /k/. You hear this in the word (kék) /ˈkeːk/, which means blue.

## How to say it

Press the back of your tongue against the roof of your mouth. Release the air suddenly without any voice. Put your hand on your throat to feel that it does not vibrate. For the voiced sound, keep the same mouth shape. Add your voice so your throat vibrates.

## Reading this letter

This letter usually sounds like the sound /k/ before vowels and quiet consonants. You hear it in the word (kutya) /ˈkucɒ/. It becomes the sound /ɡ/ before voiced consonants like b, d or g. You hear this sound change in the word (lökdös) /ˈløɡdøʃ/.

## Common mistakes

In English, this sound comes out with a puff of air. Hold your hand before your mouth. Say key, and you feel air. In Hungarian, drop that puff completely. Make the sound crisp and dry, like the sound in skip. Never add a puff of air.
