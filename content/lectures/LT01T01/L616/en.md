## Sound of sh

The letters sh make the sound /ʃ/. You hear this main sound in the word (ship) /ʃɪp/, which means ship. These letters make only one sound in English.

## Mouth movements

Push your lips slightly forward into a soft circle. Raise the front of your tongue toward the roof of your mouth. Blow air smoothly over your tongue without using your voice. Place a hand on your throat to check that it does not buzz.

## Spelling rules

The letters sh always make the sound /ʃ/ in standard English words. You hear it at the end of the word (fish) /fɪʃ/.

## Common mistakes

Do not smile or place your tongue tip against your teeth. That mistake makes the sound /s/ as in see. Keep your lips pushed forward and keep your tongue away from your teeth.
