## Letter zède sound

The letter (zède) /zɛd/ makes the main sound /z/, as in the word (zèbre) /zɛbʁ/, which means zebra. This letter has one spoken sound in standard French, but it can also be silent.

## How to say zède

Bring your teeth close together and place your tongue tip near your upper ridge. Push air through the narrow gap while you buzz your vocal cords. Place your hand on your throat to feel the buzzing vibration clearly.

## Reading the letter zède

At the start of a word or between vowels, this letter sounds like /z/, as in (zone) /zon/. At the end of many everyday words, the letter is silent, as in (nez) /ne/ and (riz) /ʁi/.

## Sounds to watch for

This sound is just like the buzzing sound in the English word zip. Do not whisper the sound like the hiss in sip, because your throat must vibrate throughout. At the end of words, remember to leave the letter completely quiet.
