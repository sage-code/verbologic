## The letter Vau

The name of this letter is spoken as (Vau) /faʊ/. Its main sound is heard in the word (Vater) /ˈfaːtɐ/, which means father. This letter can make two different sounds in German words.

## Mouth shape and air

Place your top teeth lightly on your lower lip. Blow air through the narrow gap without any voice. For the other sound, keep the exact same position, but turn your voice on. Rest a hand on your throat to feel the strong buzz.

## Reading the letter

In native German words and at the end of words, this letter makes the sound /f/. You hear this in the word (Vogel) /ˈfoːɡl̩/. In borrowed words from other languages, it makes the sound /v/. You hear this in the word (Vase) /ˈvaːzə/.

## Avoiding common mistakes

Do not read this letter as the sound in very when it begins native words. Think of the starting sound in the English word father. If you use your voice by mistake, blow plain air instead.
