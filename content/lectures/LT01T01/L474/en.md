## The letter name

The letter name is (qu) /ky/. Its main sound is the sound /k/, heard in the word (cinq) /sɛ̃k/, which means five. This letter produces one single sound on its own.

## How to say it

Press the back of your tongue against the soft roof of your mouth. Stop the air for a moment. Then release the air suddenly without using your voice. Touch your throat to feel that your vocal cords do not vibrate.

## Spelling rules

This letter appears at the end of words or very rarely on its own. It always makes the sound /k/, as in the word (coq) /kɔk/.

## Common mistakes

In English words like quick, this letter has an added w sound. Do not add any extra sound in French. You should produce only the crisp k sound, just like the sound in the English word cook.
