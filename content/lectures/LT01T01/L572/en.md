## Sound of T

The letter (T) /tiː/ makes the sound /t/, that is a crisp burst of air. You hear this sound in the word (ten) /tɛn/, which means the number ten. This letter makes three sounds in English speech.

## How to say T

For the first sound, press your tongue tip behind your upper teeth. Release the air sharply without your vocal cords. For the second sound, tap your tongue tip very quickly against the roof of your mouth. Keep your voice on during this quick tap. You can feel a quick puff of air on your fingers during the first sound.

## Spelling of T

At the start of words and in stressed beats, you say the sound /t/, that is a crisp burst of air, as in (top) /tɒp/. Between two vowels before a light beat, you tap quickly with the sound /ɾ/, that is a quick tongue tap, as in (water) /wɔː.tər/. In words like (listen) /lɪs.ən/, the letter stays completely silent.

## Comparing T

Do not press your tongue flat against your front teeth. Touch the ridge just behind your top teeth instead. Do not hum with your throat on the first sound. Keep that sound sharp and voiceless. When you tap between vowels, do not stop the flow of sound entirely.
