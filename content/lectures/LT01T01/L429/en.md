## The letter ics

The letter (ics) /iks/ makes the sound /ks/. You hear it in the word (xilofono) /ksiˈlɔfono/, which means xylophone. This letter is not native to Italian and appears in borrowed words. It has two sounds.

## How to say ics

For the first sound, lift the back of your tongue to block the airflow. Release it sharply without voice, then hiss through your closed teeth. For the second sound, make that same block while vibrating your vocal cords. Then let air buzz between your tongue and upper teeth. Place your fingers on your throat to feel that buzz.

## Spelling of ics

In borrowed words and scientific terms, this letter makes the sound /ks/, as in (extra) /ˈɛkstra/ and (taxi) /ˈtaksi/. Between vowels in certain words taken from French, it makes the sound /ɡz/, as in (ex) /ˈɛɡz/.

## Comparing ics

Do not pronounce this letter at the start of a word as a single buzzing sound, like the z in English zebra. Always start with a crisp stop, as in the middle of English box.
