## The letters gn

The letters gn sound as the sound /n/. Their main sound is the sound /n/, heard in (gnat) /næt/, which means gnat. These letters make only one sound.

## How to say it

Touch the roof of your mouth just behind your upper teeth with your tongue tip. Let the air flow freely through your nose. Turn on your voice. Feel your throat vibrate while your tongue stays in place.

## When to use it

The letters gn appear at the beginning of words before a vowel. They sound as the sound /n/, for example in (gnaw) /nɔː/.

## Common mistakes

Never say the first letter as a separate sound before the nasal sound. Do not add a hard consonant sound before the sound /n/. Drop the first letter entirely and start directly with the sound /n/.
