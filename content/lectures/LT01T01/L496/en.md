## Letter gyé

The letter (gyé) /ɟeː/ makes the sound /ɟ/, as in the word (gyerek) /ˈɟɛrɛk/, which means child. This letter has only one sound in Hungarian. Always pronounce it with voice from your throat.

## Pronunciation of gyé

Press the flat top of your tongue against the hard roof of your mouth. Keep the tip of your tongue down behind your bottom front teeth. Release the air smoothly while using your voice. Touch your throat to feel the strong vibration of your vocal cords.

## Spelling of gyé

The letter (gyé) /ɟeː/ always represents the sound /ɟ/ wherever it appears. You hear it at the end of a word in (ágy) /aːɟ/. You also hear it when it stands in other standard positions.

## Common English mistakes

Do not replace this letter with the sound in jam. The English sound starts behind the teeth and releases with friction. Press the middle of your tongue firmly against the roof of your mouth instead. Do not add a separate y sound either.
