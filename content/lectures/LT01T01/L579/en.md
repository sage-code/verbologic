## Sound of A

You call this letter (A) /æ/. Its main sound is the sound /æ/, as in the word (apple) /æ.pəl/, which means apple. This letter makes four different sounds in English words.

## How to say it

For the first sound, drop your jaw wide and spread your lips flat. For the next sound, move your mouth from an open position toward a smile. For the third sound, relax your mouth completely and let out a quick, weak breath. For the last sound, drop your jaw deeply and keep your tongue low and flat. Feel your throat vibrate for all four sounds.

## Spelling rules

In a closed syllable with stress, say the short sound /æ/, as in (cat) /kæt/. In an open syllable or before a consonant with silent e, say the sound /eɪ/, as in (bake) /beɪk/. In an unstressed syllable, make the quick weak sound /ə/, as in (ago) /ə.ɡoʊ/. Before consonants like lm or in broad words, say the long sound /ɑː/, as in (father) /fɑː.ðər/.

## Common mistakes

Do not replace the wide sound with a pure ah sound. Open your mouth wide and stretch your lips sideways, like in the word hat. Do not make the weak sound strong or clear. Keep that weak vowel very short, gentle, and quiet.
