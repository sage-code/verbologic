## Sound of the letter

The letter (я) /ja/ is called (я) /ja/. Its main sound is the sound /ja/, as in the word (яблоко) /ˈja.blə.kə/, which means apple. This letter can make four different sounds.

## How to say it

To make the first sound, lift the middle of your tongue high. Then open wide and let your voice ring out. For the next sound, drop your jaw and keep your tongue low and flat. For the third sound, raise your tongue high, then drop it just slightly. For the last sound, keep your tongue relaxed near the roof of your mouth. Place your hand on your throat to feel your voice buzz.

## Reading rules

At the start of a word or after another vowel, this stressed letter makes the sound /ja/, as in (маяк) /mɐˈjak/. After a soft consonant under stress, it makes the open sound /a/, as in (мясо) /ˈmʲa.sə/. Without stress at the start of a word or after a vowel, it makes the sound /jɪ/, as in (яйцо) /jɪjˈt͡so/. After a soft consonant without stress before the accent, it makes the sound /ɪ/, as in (мясной) /mʲɪsˈnoj/.

## Common mistakes

Do not turn the sound /jɪ/ into a long English sound like the vowel in eel. Keep the sound short and weak, like the vowel in the English word bit. When you say the sound after a consonant, do not insert a strong English consonant sound like the first sound in yes. Soften the consonant itself, then glide straight into the vowel.
