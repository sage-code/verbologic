## Sound of the letter cu

The letter (cu) /ku/ has only one sound. You pronounce it as the sound /k/. A good example is (quando) /ˈkwan.do/, which means when.

## How to say it

Press the back of your tongue against the soft roof of your mouth. Stop the airflow for a brief moment. Then drop your tongue quickly to release a small burst of air. Do not vibrate your vocal cords. You can touch your throat with your hand to make sure it does not buzz.

## Reading the letter cu

This letter always appears before the vowel u and another vowel. In every single word, it makes the sound /k/. You can see this regular pattern in the word (questo) /ˈkwes.to/. Another clear example is the word (quadro) /ˈkwa.dro/.

## Mistakes to avoid

Do not release a puff of breath with this sound. In English words like quick, you breathe out sharply. Keep this Italian sound dry, crisp and clean.
