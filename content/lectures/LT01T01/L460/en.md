## The letter cé

The letter (cé) /se/ usually sounds like the sound /k/. You hear it in the word (café) /ka.fe/, which means coffee. This letter makes two different sounds across French words, and it can also stay silent.

## Mouth movements

For the first sound, raise the back of your tongue to touch the roof of your mouth. Release a burst of air without using your voice. For the second sound, place your tongue tip near your upper teeth. Blow air smoothly through the narrow gap. You can place a hand on your throat to feel that your vocal cords do not vibrate for either sound.

## Spelling and sounds

This letter makes the sound /k/ before the vowels a, o, or u, or before consonants, as in (carte) /kaʁt/. It makes the sound /s/ before e, i, or y, as in (cerise) /sə.ʁiz/. With a cedilla mark underneath, it makes the sound /s/ before a, o, or u, as in (garçon) /gaʁ.sɔ̃/. At the end of some words, it is not pronounced, as in (tabac) /ta.ba/ and (porc) /pɔʁ/.

## Common mistakes

In English, the sound of k in words like cat often comes with a puff of air. In French, release the air gently with no puff. Keep the sound clear and crisp.
