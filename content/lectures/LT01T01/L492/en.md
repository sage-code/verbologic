## Sound of letter e

The letter name is (e) /ɛ/. It makes the sound /ɛ/, like in the word (este) /ˈɛʃtɛ/, which means evening. This letter always makes only one sound in Hungarian. It sounds very open and clear.

## How to say letter e

Open your mouth fairly wide. Keep your tongue low and forward, touching your bottom teeth. Spread your lips slightly. Let the air flow freely while you vibrate your vocal cords. Place your fingers on your throat to feel that strong buzz.

## Spelling of letter e

This letter always produces the same open sound /ɛ/ everywhere. You can hear it in the word (ember) /ˈɛmbɛr/. No accents or neighbouring consonants change this vowel.

## Mistakes to avoid with e

Do not make this vowel too long. Do not glide into an extra sound at the end, as in the English word say. Keep it short and steady, very similar to the vowel in bet.
