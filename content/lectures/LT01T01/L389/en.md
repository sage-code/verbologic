## Sound of the letter

The letter (эр) /ɛr/ makes the rolled sound /r/, that is a hard rolled r. You hear it in (рука) /rʊˈka/, which means hand or arm. This letter has two different sounds.

## How to say it

For the first sound, let the tip of your tongue vibrate against the roof of your mouth. Send out strong air and let your vocal cords buzz. For the second sound, raise the middle of your tongue toward the roof of your mouth while rolling the tip. Touch your throat to feel the strong buzz.

## Reading the letter

You say the hard sound /r/, that is a hard rolled r, before hard vowels or at the end of a word. You hear it in (рыба) /ˈrɨbə/ and (брат) /brat/. You say the soft sound /rʲ/, that is a soft rolled r, before softening letters. You hear it in (река) /rʲɪˈka/ and (дверь) /dvʲerʲ/.

## Common mistakes

Do not make the smooth sound of the English word red. Keep your tongue tip forward and roll it sharply. Do not drop the roll at the end of a word like door. Keep the vibration clear every time.
