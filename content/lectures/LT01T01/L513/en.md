## Sound of letter err

The letter is named (err) /ɛr/. Its main sound is the sound /r/, heard in the word (róka) /ˈroːkɒ/, which means fox. This letter makes only one sound in Hungarian.

## How to say it

Put the tip of your tongue against the ridge behind your upper teeth. Blow air quickly so the tip vibrates and taps against the gum. Turn on your voice to make a rolled sound. Touch your neck to feel your throat buzz.

## Reading this letter

This letter always makes the sound /r/ in every position. You hear it at the start, in the middle, and at the end of words. For example, listen to the word (piros) /ˈpiroʃ/.

## Common mistakes

Do not curl your tongue back as in the English word red. Keep your tongue forward. Tap the tip against the roof behind your teeth. Make sure you do not swallow the sound at the end of a word.
