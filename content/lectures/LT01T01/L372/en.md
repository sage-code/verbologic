## The letter a

The letter (а) /a/ has the name (а) /a/. Its main sound is heard in the word (арбуз) /ɐrˈbus/, which means watermelon. This letter makes three different sounds.

## Pronouncing the letter

For the first sound, open your mouth wide. Keep your tongue low and flat, with your lips relaxed. For the next sound, close your jaw slightly and keep your tongue relaxed. For the last sound, keep your mouth barely open and your tongue centered. Place your hand on your throat to feel your voice box vibrate.

## Reading rules

When the vowel carries the stress, pronounce the sound /a/, as in the word (брат) /brat/. In the syllable right before the stress, pronounce the sound /ɐ/, that is a clearer short uh sound, as in (вода) /vɐˈda/. In all other unstressed positions, pronounce the sound /ə/, that is a weak muffled uh sound, as in (карандаш) /kərɐnˈdaʂ/.

## Common mistakes

Do not make the stressed vowel glide like the vowel sound in father. Keep the sound steady from start to finish. When the vowel has no stress, do not say a wide open vowel sound. Let your mouth relax completely instead.
