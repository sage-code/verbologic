## Sound of the letter ka

The letter (ka) /ka/ makes the sound /k/. You hear it in the word (kilo) /ˈki.lo/, which means kilo. This letter has only one sound in Spanish. It is an uncommon letter mostly used in borrowed words.

## How to say it

Press the back of your tongue against the roof of your mouth. Stop the air completely for a moment. Then release the air suddenly without puffing out a breath. Your vocal cords do not vibrate. Put a hand on your throat to feel that it stays still.

## When it sounds like this

This letter always makes the sound /k/. You see it at the end of borrowed words like (anorak) /a.noˈrak/. It keeps this same clean sound in any position.

## Common mistakes

In English you often release this sound with a puff of air, as in kite. In Spanish you must not puff out any air. Hold a hand before your lips to make sure you feel no breeze. Keep the stop crisp and dry.
