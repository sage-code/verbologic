## Letter Y sound

The letter (Y) /waɪ/ mainly sounds like /j/, as in (yes) /jɛs/, which means yes. This letter makes four sounds in English words.

## How to say Y

For the first sound, lift your tongue high near the roof of your mouth. Push voiced air smoothly through the narrow gap. For the second sound, smile slightly and hold your tongue high and forward. For the third sound, open wide, then glide smoothly up to a high front smile. For the fourth sound, relax your tongue slightly lower. Touch your throat to feel each sound vibrate.

## Spelling rules for Y

At the start of a syllable before a vowel, this letter sounds like /j/, as in (yellow) /jɛl.oʊ/. Without stress at the end of a word, it sounds like /i/, as in (happy) /hæp.i/. With stress at the end of an open syllable, it sounds like /aɪ/, as in (fly) /flaɪ/. In a closed syllable between consonants, it sounds like /ɪ/, as in (gym) /dʒɪm/.

## Common mistakes with Y

Do not pronounce the consonant sound with a hard buzz like a soft j sound. Keep your tongue from touching the roof of your mouth so air glides freely. Do not confuse the short vowel with the long sound in words like (myth) /mɪθ/. Keep your mouth more relaxed than in the word (city) /sɪt.i/.
