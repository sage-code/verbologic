## The group ough

The letters ough do not have an official name. The main sound of the letters ough is heard in the word (through) /θruː/, which means moving from one side to the other. This spelling pattern has four different sounds.

## Making the sounds

For the first sound, push your lips into a tight circle and make a long tone. For the second sound, round your lips loosely and glide them tighter. For the third sound, open your mouth wide and make a long, open tone. For the last sound, keep your mouth relaxed, and touch your upper teeth with your lower lip to blow air.

## Reading the letters

In some base words, the letters ough sound like /uː/. In words like (though) /ðoʊ/, the letters ough sound like /oʊ/. When the letter t follows, the letters ough make the open sound /ɔː/, as in (thought) /θɔːt/. In words like (rough) /rʌf/, the letters ough sound like /ʌf/.

## Common mistakes

Do not read these letters the same way in every word. In words with the /f/ sound, do not drop the blowing air. Remember to check whether the letters make a vowel or end with an f sound.
