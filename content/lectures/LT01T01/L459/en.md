## Sound of the letter

The letter (bé) /be/ makes the sound /b/ in the word (balle) /bal/, which means ball. Across French words, this letter can make two different sounds, or stay completely silent.

## Mouth shape and sound

Press your lips tightly together, build up gentle air behind them, and release your lips. Let your voice box buzz at the exact moment your lips open. To make the other sound, bring your lips together in the same way, but do not use your voice box. Place a hand flat against your throat. You should feel a clear buzz for the first sound, but no vibration for the second.

## Reading rules and spelling

In most positions, the letter gives the sound /b/, as in the word (robe) /ʁɔb/. It changes to the sound /p/ right before an unvoiced consonant, as in the word (obtenir) /ɔp.tə.niʁ/. At the end of a few words, this letter is silent and makes no sound, as in the word (plomb) /plɔ̃/.

## Tips for English learners

In English words like bat, the sound starts with a tiny puff of air. In French, you must buzz your vocal cords earlier, before the lips part. Do not push out a strong puff of breath. Keep the sound light and clean.
