## The letter ipsilon

The letter (ipsilon) /ˈipsilon/ makes the sound /j/. You hear it in the loanword (yogurt) /ˈjɔ.ɡurt/, which means yoghurt. This foreign letter has two sounds in Italian.

## How to say ipsilon

Raise the middle of your tongue close to the roof of your mouth. Push voice through the small gap and glide quickly into the next vowel. For the vowel sound, keep your tongue high and forward. Spread your lips wide and let your vocal cords buzz continuously. Touch your throat to feel the steady vibration.

## Spelling and sounds

This letter appears in borrowed words. Before another vowel, it makes the sound /j/, as in (yacht) /jɔt/. At the end of a word or as a full vowel, it makes the sound /i/. You hear this vowel sound in (hobby) /ˈɔb.bi/ and in (baby) /ˈbɛi̯.bi/.

## Tips for pronunciation

You might want to make this letter into a vowel at the start of words. Do not say it like the vowel in the English word eat. Keep the start quick and tight, exactly like the beginning of yes.
