## Sound of letter J

The letter (J) /dʒeɪ/ usually makes the sound /dʒ/. You hear it in the word (jam) /dʒæm/, which means sweet fruit spread. This letter has four sounds in English words. Most everyday words use only the first sound. Borrowed words can use the other three sounds.

## How to say letter J

For the main sound, press the tip of your tongue behind your top front teeth. Push air through while your voice box vibrates. For the second sound, breathe air gently out through an open throat. For the third sound, raise the middle of your tongue near the roof of your mouth. For the fourth sound, push air smoothly across your tongue. Touch your throat to feel the voice buzzing.

## Spelling of letter J

In regular English words, this letter makes the sound /dʒ/, as in (job) /dʒɒb/. In words borrowed from Spanish, it makes the sound /h/, as in (jalapeño) /hæ.lə.peɪ.njoʊ/. In words from German or northern languages, it makes the sound /j/, as in (hallelujah) /hæ.lə.luː.jə/. In words taken from French, it makes the sound /ʒ/, as in (bijou) /biː.ʒuː/.

## Common mistakes with letter J

Many learners use the sound /j/ from yes instead of the main sound /dʒ/. Do not slide softly into the vowel. Block the air behind your top teeth first, then release it with a strong burst. Other learners make the soft sound /ʒ/ without stopping the air. Make sure your tongue presses firmly against the roof of your mouth before you let the air out.
