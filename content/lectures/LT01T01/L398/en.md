## Sound of the letter

The letter (ща) /ɕːa/ makes the sound /ɕː/. You hear it in the word (щи) /ɕːi/, which means cabbage soup. This letter makes only one sound.

## How to say it

Put the middle of your tongue high against your roof. Place the tip of your tongue behind your lower front teeth. Push a continuous stream of air through this narrow gap. Do not use your voice. Keep your lips slightly spread. Hold the hiss longer than a normal shushing noise. Touch your throat to feel no vibration.

## Reading this letter

The letter always makes the sound /ɕː/ in every position. You can hear this sound clearly at the end of the word (борщ) /borɕː/.

## Common mistakes

Do not say the sound like the start of ship. The sound in ship is deeper and your lips stick out. For this Russian sound, spread your lips and raise your tongue higher. Do not add a hard stop like the start of chip. Keep the airflow smooth, soft and long from start to finish.
