## The letter ee kratkoye

The letter (и краткое) /i ˈkratkəjə/ sounds like the sound /j/. You hear it in the Russian word (йогурт) /ˈjoɡʊrt/, which means yogurt. This letter makes only one sound.

## How to say it

Raise the middle of your tongue high toward the roof of your mouth. Keep the tip down behind your lower front teeth. Spread your lips slightly. Push air through and vibrate your vocal cords. Touch your neck to feel the buzzing vibration.

## Reading this letter

This letter always makes the sound /j/. It appears after vowels and before consonants or at word ends, as in (май) /maj/.

## Mistakes to avoid

Do not turn this letter into a long vowel like the sound in see. Keep it very quick and glide immediately away, just like the first sound in yes.
