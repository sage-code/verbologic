## The letter Ef

The German letter (Ef) /ɛf/ makes the sound /f/. You hear it in the word (Fisch) /fɪʃ/, which means fish. This letter makes only one sound in German.

## Making the sound

Rest your top teeth lightly on your lower lip. Blow air gently through the gap. Do not use your voice. Touch your throat to feel that it does not buzz.

## Reading this letter

This letter always makes the sound /f/ in every position. You hear it at the start of (finden) /ˈfɪndn̩/. You also hear it when written double, as in (Schiff) /ʃɪf/.

## Tips for English

This letter sounds just like the regular sound in the English word fall. There are no special traps with this sound in German.
