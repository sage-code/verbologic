## Letter sound

The letter (a) /ɒ/ is called (a) /ɒ/. Its main sound is heard in (alma) /ˈɒlmɒ/, which means apple. This letter has only one sound in Hungarian. It never changes its sound.

## Mouth shape

Open your mouth fairly wide and drop your jaw. Pull your tongue back and keep it low. Shape your lips into an open ring. Let your vocal cords vibrate freely. You can feel your throat vibrate when you make this voiced vowel.

## Reading rules

This letter always makes the sound /ɒ/. In every position, it keeps the same quality. For instance, see the word (ablak) /ˈɒblɒk/.

## Common pitfalls

Do not say the flat vowel in cat. That sound keeps the lips wide and flat. Instead, pull your tongue back and make an open ring with your lips. It is very close to the vowel in the British word lot.
