## Letter de sound

The letter (de) /de/ makes the sound /d/ in the word (día) /ˈdi.a/, which means day. This Spanish letter has three sounds.

## How to say de

For the first sound, press your tongue tip against the back of your top front teeth. Block the air, voice the sound, and release it gently. For the second sound, place your tongue tip between your teeth without closing them, letting air flow smoothly. For the third sound, blow soft air between your teeth without voicing. Feel your throat vibrate for the first two sounds.

## Spelling rules for de

After a pause, or after the letters m, n, or l, you pronounce the sound /d/ as in (donde) /ˈdon.de/. Between vowels and in most other places, you pronounce the sound /ð/ as in (cada) /ˈka.ða/ or (madre) /ˈma.ðɾe/. At the end of a word in central and northern Spain, you pronounce the soft sound /θ/ as in (madrid) /maˈðɾið/ or (verdad) /beɾˈðað/.

## Mistakes with de

Do not press your tongue behind your teeth as you do in dog. Touch the back of your top front teeth instead. Do not say a hard stop between vowels like the d in ladder. Keep your tongue slightly open so the air flows softly, just like the th sound in either.
