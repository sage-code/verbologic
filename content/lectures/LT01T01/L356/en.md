## Sound of the letter

The letter (O) /oː/ has two sounds. Its main sound is heard in (Obst) /oːpst/, which means fruit.

## How to say it

To make the first sound, pull your tongue back. Make a tight circle with your lips. Hold the sound steady and let your vocal cords vibrate. For the second sound, open your mouth wider and form a looser circle. Put a hand on your neck to feel the steady buzz.

## Spelling rules

The letter makes the long sound /oː/ in open syllables, like in (Sohn) /zoːn/. It makes the short open sound /ɔ/ in closed syllables, like in (offen) /ˈɔfn̩/.

## Common mistakes

You might glide the long vowel like the vowel sound in boat. Keep your lips in one circle without moving them at the end. For the short sound, do not spread your lips flat like in cot. Form an open circle with your lips instead.
