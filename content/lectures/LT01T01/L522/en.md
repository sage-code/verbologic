## Letter vé

The letter name is (vé) /veː/. This letter makes the sound /v/, as in (víz) /ˈviːz/, which means water. It has only one sound in standard Hungarian.

## How to say it

Rest your upper teeth lightly on your lower lip. Push air through the small gap while using your voice. Touch your throat to feel a strong buzz. Keep the airflow smooth and continuous.

## Reading it

This letter always makes the sound /v/ in every position. For example, you hear it in the word (tavasz) /ˈtɒvɒs/.

## Common mistakes

This sound matches the English sound in voice or vine. Do not swap it with the sound in wine. Keep your top teeth touching your bottom lip so your lips do not form a circle.
