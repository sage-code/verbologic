## Letter dê

The letter (dê) /de/ makes the sound /d/. You can hear it in (dia) /ˈdi.ɐ/, which means day. This letter has only one sound in European Portuguese.

## Making the sound

Press the tip of your tongue against the back of your top teeth. Build up a little air behind your tongue. Release the air suddenly while you make a sound with your voice. You can feel your vocal cords vibrate if you touch your throat.

## Reading this letter

This letter always makes the sound /d/. It keeps this sound at the beginning of a word and after vowels or consonants. For example, it sounds like this in (onde) /ˈõ.dɨ/.

## Avoiding mistakes

In English, you often touch the gum ridge behind your teeth to say door. In Portuguese, touch the back of your upper teeth instead. Keep your tongue further forward to avoid a heavy English sound.
