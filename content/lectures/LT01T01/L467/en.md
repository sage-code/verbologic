## The sound

The letter (ji) /ʒi/ makes the sound /ʒ/. You hear this sound in the word (jupe) /ʒyp/, which means skirt. This letter makes only one sound in standard French words.

## How to say it

Push your lips forward into a loose ring. Raise the front of your tongue toward the roof of your mouth. Do not touch the roof. Blow air smoothly through the narrow gap. Make your vocal cords vibrate at the same time. Place your fingers on your throat to feel the steady buzz.

## Spelling rules

In almost all regular French words, this letter makes the sound /ʒ/. You hear it at the start of (joli) /ʒɔ.li/. You also hear it in (jeu) /ʒø/. It keeps this same sound before every vowel.

## Common mistakes

Do not start this sound with a hard tap. English words like jam or jump begin with a firm press against the roof. The French sound must start smoothly with no tap. Keep the air flowing smoothly like the sound in the middle of treasure or measure.
