## Letter sound e

This letter is called (e) /e/. Its main sound is the sound /ɛ/, heard in (essere) /ˈɛs.se.re/, which means to be. In Italian, this letter makes two different sounds.

## Pronouncing the letter e

For the first sound, keep your lips spread wide. Raise your tongue high in your mouth, near the roof. For the second sound, drop your jaw slightly lower. Keep your tongue a bit lower in your mouth, with relaxed lips. Touch your throat to feel your voice box vibrate for both vowels.

## Spelling rules for e

This letter sounds closed in all unstressed positions, as in (vedere) /veˈde.re/. It also has this closed sound /e/ in some stressed syllables, like in (mela) /ˈme.la/. In other stressed syllables, it has the open sound /ɛ/, that is an open sound, as in (bello) /ˈbɛl.lo/. The single stressed word (è) /ɛ/ also takes this open sound.

## Common mistakes with e

Do not turn the sound into a glide like the vowel in day. Keep your tongue still until the vowel ends. Do not weaken the unstressed vowel to the weak sound in sofa. Always pronounce the vowel clearly and brightly.
