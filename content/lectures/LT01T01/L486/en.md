## Sound of bé

The letter name is (bé) /beː/. Its main sound is heard in the word (baba) /ˈbɒbɒ/, which means baby or doll. This letter makes two sounds.

## How to say bé

Close both lips firmly to block the air. Open your lips quickly and let your voice vibrate. For the other sound, keep the same mouth shape and release the air without your voice. Touch your throat to feel the vibration turn on or off.

## Reading bé

Normally, you pronounce the sound /b/, as in the word (jobb) /jobː/. Before voiceless consonants, you pronounce the sound /p/ instead, as in the word (rabkocsi) /ˈrɒpkot͡ʃi/.

## Mistakes to avoid

Do not release a puff of breath with the sound /p/. Keep it tight, like the letter in spin rather than pin.
