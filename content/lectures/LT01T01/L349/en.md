## The letter Ha

The name of this letter is spoken as (Ha) /haː/. Its main sound is the sound /h/, as heard in the word (Haus) /haʊ̯s/, which means house. Across German words, this letter has two different pronunciations.

## Mouth and voice

To make the first sound, keep your mouth open and relax your tongue. Breathe out gently through your throat without using your vocal cords. Hold a hand before your lips to feel the warm puff of air. For the other sound, do not move your mouth at all. Keep your throat silent.

## Spelling rules

At the start of a word or a stressed syllable, it makes the sound /h/, as in (Hand) /hant/. After a vowel, you do not pronounce it, as in (sehen) /ˈzeːən/. There it only shows that the vowel is long.

## Tips for English

You already know the sound in the word hat. The main mistake is trying to pronounce it after a vowel. Do not make a breathing sound inside words like (fahren) /ˈfaːʁən/. Just stretch the vowel before it.
