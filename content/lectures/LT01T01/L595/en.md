## Sound of T

The letter (T) /tʃ/ has two spoken sounds. Its main sound is heard in the word (top) /tɑp/, which means top. Sometimes this letter makes a quick tap instead. In other words, you do not pronounce it at all.

## How to say T

Touch the ridge behind your upper teeth with your tongue tip. Release the air suddenly without using your voice. For the quick tap, strike that same ridge very fast once with your tongue tip. Put your hand on your throat. You feel no buzzing for the first sound, but a quick vibration for the tap.

## Spelling of T

At the start of a word, it makes the sound /t/, that is a crisp unvoiced burst, as in (ten) /tɛn/. Between two vowels before a light beat, it makes the sound /ɾ/, that is a fast tap, as in (water) /wɑ.tɚ/. In some words after the letter s, it is not pronounced, as in (listen) /lɪ.sən/.

## Common mistakes

Do not press your tongue flat against your upper teeth. That mistake creates an unnatural, thick sound. Touch only the gum ridge behind your teeth, and let the air escape cleanly. Also, remember to stay quiet when this letter follows s in words like (castle) /kæ.səl/.
