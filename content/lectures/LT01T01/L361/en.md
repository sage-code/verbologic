## Sound of letter Te

The name of this letter is (Te) /teː/. Its main sound is heard in (Tisch) /tɪʃ/, which means table. This letter makes two sounds.

## How to say it

Press the tip of your tongue against the ridge behind your upper teeth. Release it sharply with a small puff of air without using your voice. For the second sound, release your tongue cleanly with no breath. Hold your hand before your mouth to feel the breath.

## Spelling rules

Before a stressed vowel, you say the sound /tʰ/, that is a breathed t, as in (Tag) /tʰaːk/. After an s sound, in unstressed spots, or in consonant clusters, you say the sound /t/, that is an unbreathed t. You hear this in (Stein) /ʃtaɪ̯n/.

## Common mistakes

Do not turn this letter into an English flap between vowels as in water. Keep the release crisp and firm every time.
