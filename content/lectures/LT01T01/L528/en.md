## Letter sound

This letter is called (bê) /be/. Its main sound is the sound /b/, heard in (bom) /bõ/, which means good. The letter makes two sounds in European Portuguese.

## Mouth shape

For the first sound, close both lips tightly to stop the air. Release your lips with voice. For the second sound, bring both lips close together without touching. Blow air gently through the narrow gap with voice. Put your hand on your throat to feel the buzzing voice.

## Reading rules

You make the sound /b/ at the start of a word or after another consonant. You hear this sound in (ombro) /ˈõ.bɾu/. You make the softer sound /β/ between vowels in normal speech. You hear this softer sound in (cabo) /ˈka.βu/ and (saber) /sɐˈβeɾ/.

## Common mistakes

Do not confuse the soft variant with the English sound in vine. For the English sound, your top teeth touch your lower lip. For this Portuguese sound, your teeth never touch your lip. Keep both lips close together instead.
