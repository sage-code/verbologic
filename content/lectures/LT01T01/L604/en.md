## The spelling kn

The letters kn make the sound /n/. You hear this sound in (knee) /ni/, which means the joint in the middle of your leg. This letter group makes only one sound in English words.

## How to say it

Press the tip of your tongue against the roof of your mouth, just behind your front teeth. Keep your lips slightly open. Push the air out through your nose while your voice hums. Touch your throat to feel the strong buzz. Do not let any air pop through your lips.

## When to use it

The letters kn appear at the beginning of words. The first letter stays completely silent, so you only say the sound /n/. You hear this in the word (knot) /nɒt/.

## Common mistakes

Learners often try to pronounce the first letter. Never make a hard clicking sound before the sound /n/. Start straight with your tongue against the roof of your mouth. Smoothly say the sound without any pause or extra push of air.
