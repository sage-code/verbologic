## Sound of Ix

The letter (Ix) /ɪks/ makes the sound /ks/ in the word (Xylophon) /ksyloˈfoːn/, which means xylophone. This letter has two different sounds in spoken German.

## Pronouncing the letter

For the first sound, lift the back of your tongue to block the air. Release it sharply without voice, then hiss air through your front teeth. For the second sound, make that same back click with your voice on. Then push air through your teeth while vibrating your vocal cords. Place two fingers on your throat to feel the buzz.

## Reading rules

In almost all native German words and Greek loanwords, read this letter as the sound /ks/. You hear this in the word (Hexe) /ˈhɛksə/. In a few words borrowed from French or English, pronounce the voiced sound /gz/. You hear this pattern in the word (Examen) /ɛkˈzaːmən/.

## Common mistakes

At the start of words, do not turn this letter into a simple z sound. English words like xylophone use a z sound, but German keeps both parts sharp and clear. Say a crisp k sound first, then slide into the s sound.
