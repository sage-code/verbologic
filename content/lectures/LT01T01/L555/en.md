## The letter C

The letter (C) /siː/ makes the sound /k/, as in (cat) /kæt/. A (cat) /kæt/ is a small furry pet. This letter has two different sounds.

## Making the sounds

Lift the back of your tongue against the roof of your mouth. Release a quick puff of quiet air. For the next sound, touch your upper side teeth with your tongue. Push air smoothly across the tongue tip without using your voice. Hold a finger before your lips to feel the steady breeze.

## Spelling rules

Before the letters a, o, u or consonants, this letter sounds like /k/, as in (cup) /kʌp/. Before the letters e, i, or y, it makes the sound /s/, as in (city) /sɪt.i/.

## Common mistakes

Do not add voice to the sound /k/. A hand on your throat should feel no vibration at all. Also, do not stop the airflow when you make the sound /s/. Let the air hiss continuously.
