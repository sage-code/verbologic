## The letter sha

The name of this letter sounds like (ша) /ʃa/. It mainly makes the sound /ʂ/, as heard in the word (школа) /ˈʂko.ɫə/, meaning school. Overall, this single letter makes two distinct sounds in speech.

## How to say it

Curl the tip of your tongue slightly back behind your top teeth. Push air through the small gap without your vocal cords vibrating. For the second sound, keep the same tongue position and vibrate your vocal cords. Touch your throat to feel that vibration.

## Reading rules

In most words, this letter makes the voiceless sound /ʂ/. You hear this sound in (шар) /ʂar/. Before voiced consonants, it changes to the voiced sound /ʐ/. You hear this voiced sound in the phrase (наш дом) /naʐ dom/.

## Common mistakes

Do not make the soft sound heard in ship. Pull your tongue tip further back and keep the sound hard and dry.
