## The letter name

The letter name is (El) /ɛl/. Its main sound is the sound /l/ in the word (Lampe) /ˈlampə/, which means lamp. This letter makes only one sound in German.

## Making the sound

Press the tip of your tongue firmly against your upper gum ridge. Keep the back of your tongue low and flat. Let the air flow smoothly around the sides of your tongue. Your vocal cords vibrate. Put a hand on your throat to feel the gentle buzz.

## Reading the letter

This letter always makes the sound /l/ in German. You hear this sound at the beginning, in the middle, and at the end of words. Double letters also make this exact same sound, as in the word (Ball) /bal/.

## Common mistakes

At the end of an English word like ball, you raise the back of your tongue. In German, never raise the back of your tongue. Always keep your tongue flat and clear, exactly like the first sound in light.
