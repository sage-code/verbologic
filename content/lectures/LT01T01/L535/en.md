## The letter i

This letter is called (i) /i/. It sounds like the sound /i/ in the word (ilha) /ˈi.ʎɐ/, which means island. In European Portuguese, this letter has two sounds.

## Mouth movements

To make the first sound, spread your lips wide into a smile. Raise the front of your tongue high toward the roof of your mouth. Your vocal cords vibrate continuously. To make the second sound, push the tongue up even faster and glide instantly away. Place two fingers on your throat to feel the strong vibration.

## Reading rules

You hear the vowel sound /i/ when the letter forms a full syllable, as in (amigo) /ɐ.ˈmi.ɡu/. You make the quick gliding sound /j/ next to another vowel, as in (pai) /ˈpaj/ and in (viagem) /ˈvja.ʒɐ̃j̃/.

## Common mistakes

Do not shorten this vowel to the relaxed sound in the English word sit. Keep your tongue high and tense, like the sound in the English word see. When gliding, make a quick flick of the tongue.
