## Sound of u

You call this letter (u) /u/. Its main sound is heard in the word (uno) /ˈu.no/, which means one. It makes two different sounds in Italian.

## How to say u

For the vowel sound, pull your tongue back and high. Round your lips tightly into a small circle. Let your vocal cords vibrate steadily. For the gliding sound, keep the same mouth shape but move quickly into the next vowel. Touch your throat to feel continuous vibration.

## Spelling of u

This letter makes the vowel sound /u/ on its own or between consonants, as in (luna) /ˈlu.na/. It makes the quick gliding sound /w/ right before or after another vowel, as in (uomo) /ˈwɔ.mo/.

## Pitfalls with u

Do not let this sound slide into two vowels like in the English word boot. Keep your tongue steady and your lips firm. Do not add a y sound before it like in music. Make a pure, steady Italian sound from start to finish.
