## Letter X sound

The letter (X) /ks/ is called /ks/. Its main sound is heard in the word (box) /bɒks/, which means box. This letter makes three different sounds in English words.

## How to say X

For the first sound, push the back of your tongue up, release air, and then hiss. For the second sound, make that same back sound with voice, and then buzz. For the third sound, put your tongue tip near your upper teeth and buzz with your voice. Touch your throat to feel the voice buzz.

## Spelling rules for X

Most often, especially in the middle or at the end of a word, it sounds like the sound /ks/, as in (fox) /fɒks/. Before a stressed vowel, it sounds like the sound /ɡz/, as in (exam) /ɪɡ.zæm/. At the beginning of a word, it sounds like the sound /z/, as in (xenon) /zɛn.ɒn/.

## Common mistakes with X

Do not pronounce the beginning of a word with the sound /ks/. Say only the buzzing sound /z/, with your voice on. At the end of a word, do not drop the hissing sound /s/. Make sure you pronounce both sounds clearly, without adding an extra vowel at the end.
