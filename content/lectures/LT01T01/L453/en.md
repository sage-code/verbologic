## Letter uve sound

This letter is called (uve) /ˈube/. Its main sound is the sound /b/, heard in the word (verde) /ˈbeɾ.de/, which means green. In Spanish, this letter makes two different sounds.

## How to say uve

For the first sound, close both lips firmly. Stop the air, then let it burst open while your voice sounds. For the other sound, bring both lips close together without touching. Let the air squeeze gently through the narrow gap with your voice. Put a hand on your throat to feel the strong buzz.

## Spelling rules for uve

At the start of a sentence or after an m or n, it makes the sound /b/, as in (enviar) /emˈbjaɾ/. Between vowels or after any other letter, it makes the sound /β/, as in (uva) /ˈu.βa/. You also hear that softer sound in (curva) /ˈkuɾ.βa/.

## Mistakes to avoid with uve

Do not touch your bottom lip to your top teeth like in the English word vine. Spanish never uses that English sound. Keep your top teeth entirely out of the way. Bring only your two lips together, as if blowing gently on hot soup.
