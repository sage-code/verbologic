## The letter Pe

The German name of this letter is (Pe) /peː/. Its main sound is heard in (Papa) /ˈpaːpa/, which means dad. This letter produces two sounds in German spoken words.

## Pronouncing Pe

Press your lips tightly together and block the air. Then release the air with a clear puff. Your vocal cords do not vibrate. For the other sound, press your lips together and pop them open without that puff of air. Hold your hand before your mouth to feel the breath.

## Spelling rules for Pe

At the start of a word, it makes the sound /pʰ/, that is an aspirated p. You hear this sound in (Post) /pʰɔst/. After the sound /s/ or in unstressed syllables, it makes the sound /p/, that is an unaspirated p. You hear this variant in (Sport) /ʃpɔʁt/.

## Common mistakes with Pe

Do not voice this letter like a b sound in boy. Keep your vocal cords completely still. At the start of words, remember to add a strong puff of air, just like in pet. In clusters after s, drop that puff of air, exactly as in spin.
