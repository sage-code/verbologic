## Sound of eme

The letter (eme) /ˈɛmi/ makes its main sound in the word (mão) /ˈmɐ̃w̃/, which means hand. This letter has two different sounds.

## How to say it

For the first sound, close both lips together. Send voiced air out through your nose. For the other sound, do not close your lips. Instead, lower the soft back of your mouth. Voiced air flows out through your nose and mouth together. Put two fingers against your nose to feel it buzz.

## Reading rules

Before a vowel or at syllable starts, it makes the sound /m/, as in (mar) /ˈmaɾ/. At the end of a syllable, it does not close your lips. It only makes the vowel nasal, like the sound /̃/ in (fim) /ˈfĩ/.

## Common mistakes

At the end of a syllable, do not close your lips. In English, you press your lips shut for the last sound in him. In Portuguese, keep your lips open and let the vowel ring through your nose.
