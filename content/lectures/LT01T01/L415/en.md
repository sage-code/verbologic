## Sound of emme

The letter (emme) /ˈɛmme/ makes the sound /m/. You hear this in the word (mare) /ˈma.re/, which means sea. This letter has only one sound in standard Italian.

## How to say it

Close both lips gently together. Send the air out through your nose. Turn on your voice. You can feel your throat vibrate, and your lips buzz softly.

## Spelling rules

The letter makes the sound /m/ in all positions. For instance, you hear it clearly in (mamma) /ˈmam.ma/.

## Common mistakes

This sound is identical to the sound in the English word moon. Italian speakers never drop or alter this sound. You do not need to fix anything.
