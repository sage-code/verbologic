## The letter ee

The name of this letter is (и) /i/. Its main sound is heard in the word (игра) /ɪˈɡra/, which means game or play. This letter produces three different sounds in Russian.

## Making the sounds

For the first sound, push your tongue high and forward toward the roof of your mouth. Spread your lips wide and vibrate your vocal cords. For the second sound, relax your tongue slightly lower. For the third sound, pull your tongue further back into the middle of your mouth. Touch your throat to feel the steady vibration.

## Reading the variants

Under stress, it makes the sound /i/, as in (мир) /mʲir/. Without stress, it makes the sound /ɪ/, that is a softer short ih sound, as in (зима) /zʲɪˈma/. After certain hard consonants, it makes the sound /ɨ/, that is a deeper ih sound from the back of the mouth, as in (жить) /ʐɨtʲ/.

## Common mistakes

Do not replace the third sound with the vowel in the English word sit. The English sound is made near the front of your mouth. To fix this, pull your tongue back toward your throat and keep your lips flat.
