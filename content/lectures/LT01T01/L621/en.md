## Sound of ng

The letters ng are called the sound /ŋ/. Their main sound is the sound /ŋ/, as in (sing) /sɪŋ/. To (sing) /sɪŋ/ means to make music with your voice. This letter group makes two sounds in English words.

## How to say ng

To make the first sound, lift the back of your tongue against the soft roof of your mouth. Let air flow out through your nose while your voice hums. Touch your throat to feel the strong vibration. For the second sound, make that same humming nose sound, then finish with a quick puff of voice from your throat.

## Spelling patterns of ng

At the end of a word, you say the sound /ŋ/, like in (ring) /rɪŋ/. Inside the middle of a root word before a vowel, you pronounce the sound /ŋɡ/, like in (finger) /fɪŋ.ɡər/.

## Comparing ng

Learners often add a hard click at the end of simple words. Do not let your tongue drop suddenly. Keep the back of your tongue touching the roof until your voice stops completely. Also, do not replace this sound with a simple sound made at your front teeth.
