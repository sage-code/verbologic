## Sound of letter ű

The letter (ű) /yː/ is called (ű) /yː/. Its main sound is the sound /yː/, heard in the word (űr) /ˈyːr/, which means space, cosmos. This letter makes only one sound.

## How to say it

Push your tongue high and forward in your mouth. Shape your lips into a tight small circle. Hold this position steady while your vocal cords vibrate. Keep the sound long and even. Place your fingers on your throat to feel the steady buzz.

## Spelling rules

This letter always keeps its one sound in every standard position. You hear the long sound /yː/ in words like (tűz) /ˈtyːz/.

## Mistakes to avoid

Do not replace this vowel with the sound in the English word spoon. Keep your tongue pushed forward instead of pulling it back. Do not glide into a second sound at the end. Hold the vowel pure and long.
