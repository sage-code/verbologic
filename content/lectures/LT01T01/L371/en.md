## The letter Eszett

The German letter (Eszett) /ɛsˈtsɛt/ makes a sharp hissing sound. You hear it clearly in the word (Straße) /ˈʃtʁaːsə/, which means street. This letter always makes only one sound in standard German.

## Mouth shape

Bring your teeth close together and spread your lips slightly. Put your tongue tip near the bump behind your top teeth. Push air through the narrow gap. Do not use your voice. You can feel cool air on your hand in front of your lips.

## Spelling rules

This letter always stands for the sound /s/. It appears only after long vowels, as in (groß) /ɡʁoːs/. It also appears after two vowels said together, as in (fleißig) /ˈflaɪ̯sɪç/. It never stands at the start of a word.

## Common mistakes

Do not buzz this sound like the letter in zoo. Keep your vocal cords completely still. It sounds just like the double letter in grass. It also looks like a capital letter B, but you never pronounce it like bad.
