## Sound of zeta

The letter name is (zeta) /ˈdzɛta/. Its main sound is the sound /dz/, heard in the word (zaino) /ˈdzai̯.no/, which means backpack. This letter makes two sounds in Italian.

## How to say zeta

For the first sound, place your tongue behind your top front teeth. Block the air, then release it sharply with a hissing sound. Do not vibrate your vocal cords. For the second sound, make the exact same mouth shape and release the air. This time, vibrate your vocal cords. Put a hand on your throat to feel the buzzing.

## Spelling of zeta

This letter makes the voiceless sound /ts/ after consonants or in common endings, as in (stazione) /statˈtsjoːne/ and (marzo) /ˈmartso/. It makes the voiced sound /dz/ at the start of many words and between vowels, as in (zero) /ˈdzɛːro/.

## Mistakes to avoid

Do not pronounce this letter like an English z in the word zoo. That English sound is a simple buzz without a stop. Always start with your tongue pressed tight against your teeth. First make a quick t sound or d sound, then let the hiss follow.
