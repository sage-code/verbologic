## Sound of pé

The letter name is (pé) /peː/. It usually makes the sound /p/, as in (piros) /ˈpiroʃ/, which means red. This letter can make two sounds.

## How to say pé

To make the first sound, press both lips firmly together. Stop the air completely, then release it quickly without voicing. To make the second sound, press both lips together in the same way, but vibrate your vocal cords as the air breaks through. Put your fingers on your throat to feel the buzz.

## Spelling rules for pé

In most words, this letter makes the sound /p/, as in (apa) /ˈɒpɒ/. Before a voiced consonant, it changes into the sound /b/, as in (népdal) /ˈneːbdɒl/.

## Comparing pé

In English, the sound of p often has a strong puff of air, as in pen. In Hungarian, do not puff out extra air. Release the sound cleanly, like the p in spin.
