## Sound of the letter pe

The letter (pe) /pe/ makes the sound /p/. You hear it in (pan) /ˈpan/, which means bread. This letter has only one sound in Spanish.

## How to say pe

Close both lips firmly to block the air. Then open your lips quickly to release the air. Your vocal cords do not vibrate. Hold your hand in front of your lips to feel almost no breath.

## Spelling of pe

The letter (pe) /pe/ always makes the sound /p/. It sounds the same in every position, as in the word (copa) /ˈko.pa/.

## Mistakes to avoid with pe

Do not blow out a puff of air. In English words like pin, a burst of breath follows the lips opening. Release your lips gently so no puff comes out.
