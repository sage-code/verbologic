## The letter E

The letter (E) /eː/ is called (E) /eː/. Its main sound is heard in (Elefant) /eleˈfant/, which means elephant. This letter makes four distinct vowel sounds in German words.

## Pronouncing the letter

For the first sound, spread your lips wide and keep your tongue high and tense. For the second sound, drop your lower jaw slightly and relax your tongue. For the third sound, relax your entire mouth completely and make a very quick, weak vowel. For the fourth sound, open your mouth a bit wider and lower your tongue. Place your hand on your throat to feel your voice box vibrate for all four sounds.

## Spelling and sounds

In long stressed syllables or when doubled, it makes the sound /eː/ as in (Tee) /teː/. In short stressed syllables before several consonants, it makes the sound /ɛ/ as in (Bett) /bɛt/. In unstressed prefixes or word endings, it makes the sound /ə/, that is a weak central vowel, as in (bitte) /ˈbɪtə/. In unstressed endings before the letter r, it makes the sound /ɐ/, that is a short open vowel, as in (Mutter) /ˈmʊtɐ/.

## Avoiding common mistakes

Do not glide into an English sound like the vowel in day. Keep your tongue completely still from start to finish. Also, do not pronounce the unstressed ending like the strong vowel in pet. Keep it short and relaxed.
