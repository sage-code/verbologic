## The Sound of K

The letter **K** represents a voiceless velar stop `/k/`, identical to the 'k' sound in the English word **kite**. In Romanian, it is primarily found in borrowed international vocabulary and scientific units. Unlike English, Romanian `/k/` is typically unaspirated, meaning there is no noticeable puff of air following the release.

## How to Pronounce K

Raise the back of your tongue until it firmly presses against your soft palate to block the airflow entirely. Build a slight amount of breath pressure behind this closure, then release the tongue briskly to produce a clean burst. Keep your vocal cords still to ensure the sound remains entirely voiceless.

## Spelling and Usage

**K** is relatively rare in Romanian, appearing almost exclusively in foreign loanwords, proper names, and metric abbreviations like **kg** or **km**. Native Romanian words use the letters **C**, **Ch**, or **Q** for the `/k/` sound instead. When writing in lowercase or uppercase, it preserves standard Latin typography without any diacritics.

## Nuances and Common Mistakes

English speakers must avoid adding heavy aspiration when pronouncing **K** before vowels, as Romanian stops are crisp and clean. Additionally, when followed by front vowels like **e** or **i**, the sound shifts slightly forward in the mouth to a palatalized `[kʲ]`, similar to the 'k' in **key**. Take care not to pronounce it with the throaty friction heard in German or Dutch.
