## Sound of the letter vee

The letter (V) /viː/ makes the sound /v/. You hear it in the word (van) /væn/, which means a van. This letter makes only one sound in English.

## How to say it

Rest your upper teeth gently on your lower lip. Push air out steadily while using your voice. You can feel your throat buzz with your fingers.

## Reading the letter

This letter always makes the sound /v/. It appears in words like (river) /rɪv.ər/.

## Common mistakes

Do not press your two lips together as in win. Make sure your top teeth touch your lower lip. Do not whisper the sound as in fan. Keep your voice buzzing continuously.
