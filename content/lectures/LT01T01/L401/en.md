## The soft sign

The letter is called (мягкий знак) /ˈmʲaxkʲɪj ˈznak/. It makes no sound by itself. Instead, it changes the consonant before it, as in (день) /dʲenʲ/, which means day. This sign has two jobs in speech.

## Pronouncing the sounds

For the first effect, raise the middle of your tongue toward the roof of your mouth. Keep the tip touching your lower teeth. For the second effect, lift your tongue quickly toward the roof of your mouth, like the first sound in yes. Touch your throat to feel your voice buzz.

## Reading the sign

At the end of a word or before another consonant, it softens the consonant before it. You hear this change in (мальчик) /ˈmalʲt͡ɕɪk/. When it stands before a vowel letter, it adds a quick glide right before that vowel. You hear this glide in (семья) /sʲɪmʲˈja/.

## Common pitfalls

Do not add an extra vowel sound like the ending of city. Do not insert a separate vowel between consonants. Keep the consonant soft without dragging it out. To check this, keep your tongue high against the roof of your mouth as you finish the consonant.
