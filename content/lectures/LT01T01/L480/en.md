## The letter double vé

The letter is called (double vé) /dublə ve/. Its main sound is heard in the word (wagon) /va.ɡɔ̃/, which means railway carriage. This letter can make two different sounds.

## Making the sounds

For the first sound, place your upper front teeth against your lower lip. Push air out while your vocal cords vibrate. For the next sound, make a tight small circle with your lips. Pull the back of your tongue upward. Let your vocal cords vibrate as air flows outward. Touch your neck to feel the steady buzz.

## Reading the letter

In older borrowings mostly from Germanic languages, this letter sounds like /v/, as in (warrant) /va.ʁɑ̃/. In modern loanwords mainly taken from English, it sounds like /w/, as in (web) /wɛb/.

## Mistakes to avoid

Do not use the sound in vine for every loanword. Some modern words require the beginning sound of water instead. Never pause awkwardly before this letter. Simply pronounce it as one smooth glide.
