## Letter sound

You call this letter (Y) /j/. Its main sound is heard in the word (yes) /jɛs/, which means yes. This letter can make four different sounds.

## How to say it

For the first sound, lift your tongue close to the roof of your mouth. Smoothly glide into the next vowel with voice. For the second sound, open wide and then glide up to a smile. For the third sound, spread your lips and make a clear high voice. For the fourth sound, relax your tongue and mouth a little. Put a hand on your throat to feel each sound vibrate.

## Reading rules

Before a vowel at the start of a syllable, it makes the sound /j/ as in (yam) /jæm/. At the end of a short one-syllable word, it makes the sound /aɪ/ as in (my) /maɪ/. At the end of a longer word without stress, it makes the sound /i/ as in (happy) /hæ.pi/. Inside a closed syllable of Greek origin, it makes the sound /ɪ/ as in (gym) /dʒɪm/.

## Common mistakes

Do not turn the first sound into a hard friction. Keep the air flowing smoothly without any buzzing. At the end of short words, do not make a flat vowel. Remember to open your mouth wide first and then move your lips into a smile.
