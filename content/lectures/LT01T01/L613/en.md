## Sound of -sland

The letter ending -sland is called /lənd/. It makes the sound /lənd/, as in the word (island) /aɪ.lənd/, which means a piece of land surrounded by water. This letter group makes only one sound in standard English words.

## How to say -sland

Touch the tip of your tongue to the roof of your mouth for the first sound. Drop your tongue slightly into the center of your mouth for a brief vowel. Touch the tip to the roof again, and finish by closing both lips to stop the air. Your vocal cords vibrate all the way through.

## Spelling of -sland

This spelling pattern appears at the end of unstressed words and geographic names. The letter s is silent before the ending. It sounds like /lənd/ in every standard noun of this type.

## Comparing -sland

Do not pronounce the letter s in this ending. Saying an s sound is a common mistake. Keep the sound silent just like in the everyday word aisle. Touch your throat to ensure your voice hums continuously through the rest of the ending.
