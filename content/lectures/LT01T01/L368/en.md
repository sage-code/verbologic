## The letter Ä

The letter (Ä) /ɛː/ is called (Ä) /ɛː/. Its main sound is heard in the word (Äpfel) /ˈɛpfəl/, which means apples. This letter makes two sounds.

## How to say Ä

For the first sound, drop your jaw slightly and spread your lips. Keep your tongue relaxed and low in the mouth. For the second sound, open your mouth wider and hold the vowel longer. Place your hand on your throat to feel continuous voice.

## Spelling of Ä

In a short, closed stressed syllable, it makes the sound /ɛ/, that is a short open vowel, as in (Hände) /ˈhɛndə/. In a long syllable with a single consonant, it makes the sound /ɛː/, that is a long open vowel, as in (Käse) /ˈkɛːzə/.

## Common mistakes with Ä

Do not turn the long sound into the vowel of day. Keep your tongue perfectly still without gliding into another sound. Do not replace the short sound with the vowel in cat.
