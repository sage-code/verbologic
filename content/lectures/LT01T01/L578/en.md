## Sound of Z

The letter (Z) /ziː/ sounds like the sound /z/. You hear this sound in the word (zebra) /zi.brə/, an animal with black and white stripes. This letter makes three sounds in English words.

## How to say Z

For the first sound, place your tongue tip near your upper gum. Push air out smoothly and vibrate your vocal cords. For the second sound, pull your tongue back slightly, push the air, and keep your voice humming. For the third sound, block the air briefly behind your teeth, then release it sharply with no voice. Touch your throat to feel the buzz.

## Spelling patterns

In most places, this letter makes the sound /z/, as in (zone) /zoʊn/. Before an unstressed vowel or u, it makes the sound /ʒ/, as in (azure) /æ.ʒər/. In words borrowed from other languages, it makes the sound /ts/, as in (pizza) /pit.sə/.

## Common mistakes

Do not turn the sound /z/ into an s. Always keep your vocal cords buzzing. Do not let the sound /ʒ/ become the sound /z/. Keep your tongue pulled back slightly and push the air smoothly.
