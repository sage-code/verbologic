## Sound of emm

The name of this letter is (emm) /ɛm/. It mainly sounds like the sound /m/, as heard in (ma) /ˈmɒ/, which means today. This letter makes only one sound.

## How to say it

Close both lips firmly together. Let your vocal cords vibrate to make voice. Direct the air out through your nose. Rest a finger on your nose to feel the buzz.

## Spelling rules

This letter always produces the sound /m/ in all positions. An example is the word (alma) /ˈɒlmɒ/.

## Common mistakes

This sound matches the sound in the ordinary English word mother. There are no pitfalls for you with this consonant.
