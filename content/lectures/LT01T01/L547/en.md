## The letter u

You call this letter (u) /u/. Its main sound is the sound /ˈũ/, heard in (um) /ˈũ/, which means one, a. This letter has three different sounds.

## How to say u

For the first sound, pull your tongue back and push your lips forward into a small circle. For the second sound, make that same shape very quickly and glide into the next vowel. For the third sound, keep the same mouth shape, but let air escape through your nose. Feel your throat buzz with each sound.

## Spelling rules for u

This letter makes the sound /u/ when stressed or before vowels, as in (uva) /ˈu.vɐ/. It makes the gliding sound /w/ when joined with another vowel, as in (mau) /ˈmaw/. Before nasal consonants in the same syllable, it makes the nasal sound /ˈũ/, as in (junto) /ˈʒũ.tu/.

## Common mistakes with u

Do not add a y sound before the vowel, like in the English word music. Make a pure vowel instead, like in goose. Do not make the vowel too long, like in moon. Push your lips firmly into a small circle, and keep the vowel steady and short.
