## Letter sound

The letter (eff) /ɛf/ makes the sound /f/. You hear it in the word (fa) /ˈfɒ/, which means tree or wood. This letter makes only one sound in Hungarian.

## How to say it

Rest your upper teeth gently on your lower lip. Blow air smoothly through the narrow gap. Keep your vocal cords completely still. Touch your throat to feel that it does not buzz.

## Reading guide

This letter always makes the sound /f/ in all regular positions. You can hear it clearly in the word (fül) /ˈfyl/.

## Common mistakes

This sound is identical to the first sound in the English word fish. There are no tricky pitfalls with this letter in Hungarian.
