## Letter sound vê

The name of this letter is (vê) /ve/. It makes the sound /v/, as in (verde) /ˈveɾ.dɨ/, which means green. This letter has only one sound in European Portuguese.

## How to say it

Rest your upper teeth gently on your lower lip. Blow air through the gap while your voice box buzzes. Put your fingers on your throat to feel the strong vibration.

## Spelling rules

This letter always makes the sound /v/ in every position. You hear it at the start of a word in (vida) /ˈvi.dɐ/.

## Common mistakes

Do not confuse this sound with the sound /b/. Never press your two lips together. Keep your upper teeth on your bottom lip, exactly like the sound in the English word vine.
