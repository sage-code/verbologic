## Letter sound

The letter (ache) /aʃ/ has no sound of its own. It is completely silent in the word (habiter) /a.bi.te/, which means to live. This letter never makes any sound in French words.

## How to say it

You make no movement with your mouth. Your tongue and lips stay ready for the next letter. No air puffs out from your throat. Hold a hand before your lips to feel no puff of breath.

## Spelling rules

This letter is always silent. It makes no sound in (homme) /ɔm/. It also makes no sound in (héros) /e.ʁo/.

## Common mistakes

Do not breathe out like the first sound in hat. Keep your throat quiet. Go straight to the next vowel with no breath.
