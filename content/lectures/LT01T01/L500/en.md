## Letter sound

The letter (jé) /jɛ/ has only one sound. Its main sound is /j/, as heard in the word (jó) /ˈjoː/, which means good.

## Pronunciation guide

Raise the middle of your tongue toward the roof of your mouth. Keep your tongue close without touching the roof. Let your vocal cords vibrate as air flows smoothly out. Place a hand on your throat to feel the steady buzz.

## Reading rules

The letter always makes the sound /j/. You hear it clearly in the word (hajas) /ˈhɒjɒʃ/.

## Common pitfalls

Do not pronounce this letter like the sound in joy or the sound in jar. It sounds exactly like the first sound in yellow or yes. Keep the airflow smooth without stopping it.
