## The group lf

The letters lf sound like the sound /f/. You hear this in the word (calf) /kɑːf/, which means a young cow. This letter group makes two different sounds.

## Mouth movements

For the first sound, place your top teeth gently on your bottom lip. Blow air through the small gap without using your voice. For the second sound, touch the roof of your mouth with your tongue tip. Let voice flow, then move quickly to your bottom lip and blow air. Place your fingers on your throat to feel when voice starts.

## Reading the letters

In some everyday words, the first letter stays silent. The letters then make only the sound /f/, as in (half) /hɑːf/. In other words, you pronounce both letters together. The group then makes the sound /lf/, as in (gulf) /ɡʌlf/.

## Common mistakes

Do not force an l sound into every word you see. Saying an l sound in words where it is silent is an easy mistake. Check which words keep both sounds and keep your tongue low when the letter is silent.
