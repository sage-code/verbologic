## The letter ess

The letter (ess) /ɛʃ/ makes the sound /ʃ/. You hear it in the word (sárga) /ˈʃaːrɡɒ/, which means yellow. This letter always makes only one sound in Hungarian.

## Pronouncing ess

Push your lips forward a little. Raise the front of your tongue toward the roof of your mouth. Blow air gently through the narrow gap. Do not use your voice. Touch your throat with your hand to make sure it does not buzz.

## Spelling rules for ess

The letter (ess) /ɛʃ/ always makes the sound /ʃ/ in all standard positions. You can hear this sound clearly in the word (most) /moʃt/.

## Common mistakes

Do not pronounce this letter like the soft sound in the English word sun. It always sounds like the quiet rushing sound in the English word ship. Round your lips slightly and push the air through.
