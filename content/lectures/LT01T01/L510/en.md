## Letter ő sound

The letter (ő) /øː/ is called (ő) /øː/. Its main sound is the sound /øː/, heard in the word (ősz) /øːs/, which means autumn. This letter makes only one sound.

## Saying the letter ő

To make this vowel, place your tongue forward as if saying the vowel in see. Purse your lips forward into a small circle, as if saying the vowel in too. Keep your tongue steady and vibrate your vocal cords to make a long sound. Place your fingers on your throat to feel the steady buzz.

## Spelling of letter ő

This letter always produces the same long vowel sound everywhere. You will hear the sound /øː/ at the start, middle, or end of words. For example, it appears at the end of the word (erdő) /ˈɛrdøː/.

## Mistakes with letter ő

Do not replace this vowel with the sound in bird. Do not glide into another sound like the vowel in go. Shape your lips first, and hold the vowel steady without moving your mouth.
