## The letter acca

The letter (acca) /ˈakka/ has no sound of its own in Italian. You see it at the start of (hotel) /oˈtɛl/, which means hotel. This letter has only one silent form.

## Making the sound

Your mouth makes no sound at all for this letter. Keep your throat quiet and let no extra air push out. Place your hand on your throat. You feel nothing move until you speak the vowel right after it.

## Where it appears

This letter is always silent in every Italian word. You see it at the beginning of words like (hanno) /ˈan.no/.

## Common mistakes

Never blow air like the first sound in hat or house. Start straight on the vowel as in the English word hour or honest.
