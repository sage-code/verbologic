## Sound of ph

The letters ph make the sound /f/. You hear this sound in the word (photo) /foʊ.toʊ/, which means a picture taken with a camera. This letter combination makes two different sounds in English words. Most words use only the first sound.

## How to say ph

To make the first sound, place your upper teeth on your lower lip. Blow air gently through the gap without using your voice. To make the second sound, keep your mouth in the same position. Blow air through the gap and turn your voice on. Put a hand on your throat to feel the buzz.

## Spelling of ph

The letters ph usually make the sound /f/ in words from Greek, such as (phone) /foʊn/ and (dolphin) /dɑl.fɪn/. In the name (Stephen) /sti.vən/, these letters make the sound /v/ instead.

## Common mistakes

Do not pronounce the letters separately as two sounds. Never say the sound in pet followed by the sound in hat. Keep your upper teeth on your lower lip, and push the air smoothly through your mouth.
