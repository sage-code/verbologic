## Sound of ó

The letter (ó) /oː/ is named (ó) /oː/. Its main sound is the sound /oː/, heard in the word (óra) /ˈoː.rɒ/, which means clock or hour. This letter has only one sound in Hungarian.

## How to say ó

Pull your tongue back and raise it halfway. Form a firm circle with your lips, pushing them forward. Let your voice vibrate steadily. Hold your mouth still so the sound stays steady. You can feel your lips stay firm and tense as you make the sound.

## Spelling of ó

This letter always makes the sound /oː/ in all positions. An example is the word (só) /ʃoː/.

## Comparing ó with English

In English words like boat or go, your lips move toward a w sound. Do not move your lips while you say this vowel. Keep your lips in a steady circle until you finish speaking.
