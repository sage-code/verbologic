## Sound of the letter ka

The letter (ka) /ka/ makes the sound /k/. You hear it in the word (kilo) /ki.lo/, which means kilo. This letter always makes just one sound in French.

## How to say it

Press the back of your tongue firmly against the roof of your mouth. Stop the air completely for a moment. Then lower your tongue quickly to release a sharp burst of air. Do not vibrate your vocal cords. Hold a hand before your lips to feel a sudden puff of air.

## Spelling rules

This letter always produces the same sound in all positions. At the end of a word, it sounds like /k/ in (anorak) /a.nɔ.ʁak/.

## Common mistakes

In English, this sound comes with a strong puff of breath at the start of words like keep. In French, you must release much less air. Keep the burst very crisp and short. Hold a hand before your mouth and make sure you feel only a tiny breath.
