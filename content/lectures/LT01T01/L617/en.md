## The group ch

The letter group ch makes the sound /tʃ/. You hear it in the word (chin) /tʃɪn/, which means the chin on your face. This group has three different sounds across English words.

## How to say it

Touch the roof of your mouth with your tongue, then release the air quickly. Keep your vocal cords quiet for this sound. To make the next sound, touch the back of your mouth with your tongue and release the air. For the last sound, round your lips and push continuous air smoothly over your tongue.

## Spelling rules

In most native English words, the group ch sounds like /tʃ/, as in (rich) /rɪtʃ/. In words from Greek, the group sounds like /k/, as in (echo) /ɛk.oʊ/. In words borrowed from modern French, it sounds like /ʃ/, as in (chef) /ʃɛf/.

## Common mistakes

Do not soften the primary sound into a continuous hiss. Stop the air completely before releasing it with a quick pop. Since this is your own language, you usually pick the correct sound naturally without difficulty.
