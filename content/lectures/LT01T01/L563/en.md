## Letter sound

You call this letter (K) /keɪ/. It mainly sounds like the sound /k/, as heard in (kite) /kaɪt/. This word means a light toy that flies in the wind. This letter has one sound, and it can also stay silent.

## Pronunciation guide

Press the back of your tongue against the roof of your mouth. Stop the air for a moment. Then let the air burst out quickly without using your voice. Place your fingers on your throat. You will feel no buzz at all.

## Spelling patterns

This letter usually gives the sound /k/ at the start, middle, or end of a word, as in (milk) /mɪlk/. When it stands before the letter n at the start of a word, it stays silent, as in (knee) /niː/.

## Common mistakes

Learners often try to pronounce the silent letter before n. In words like (knot) /nɒt/, do not say any sound before the sound /n/. Let your tongue begin straight on the roof of your mouth.
