## The letter yu

The letter (ю) /ju/ is called (ю) /ju/. Its main sound is heard in the word (юг) /juk/, which means south. This letter has four sounds in total.

## Pronouncing the sounds

For the first sound, push your tongue forward and then round your lips tightly. For the second sound, pull your tongue back and push your lips forward into a circle. For the third sound, start with a quick glide and relax your tongue and lips. For the fourth sound, loosen your lips into an open ring. Touch your throat to feel the strong vibration of your voice.

## Reading this letter

At the start of a stressed syllable, say the sound /ju/ as in (каюта) /kɐˈjutə/. After a consonant under stress, make the consonant soft and say the sound /u/ as in (люди) /ˈlʲʉdʲɪ/. At the beginning of an unstressed syllable, say the sound /jʊ/ as in (юрист) /jʊˈrʲist/. After a consonant without stress, soften the consonant and say the sound /ʊ/ as in (люблю) /lʲʊˈblʲu/.

## Avoiding mistakes

Do not drop the quick glide when this letter starts a word. Connect it cleanly, exactly like the start of the English word you. After consonants, do not say a separate sound like the start of yes. Soften the consonant itself and glide directly into the vowel, avoiding the extra step you hear in beauty.
