## Sound of the letter

The letter (o) /o/ is called (o) /o/. It makes the sound /o/, like in the word (ojo) /ˈo.xo/, which means eye. This letter makes only one sound in Spanish.

## How to say it

To make the sound /o/, open your mouth halfway. Pull your tongue back slightly. Round your lips into a circle. Keep your lips and tongue still so the sound stays steady. Your vocal cords vibrate.

## Reading and spelling

The letter (o) /o/ always makes the sound /o/. It makes this sound in every position, as in the word (sol) /sol/.

## Common mistakes

Do not let this vowel slide into a double sound like in the English word boat. Keep your mouth firmly in place from start to finish. Also, never relax it into a weak sound like the second vowel in carrot.
