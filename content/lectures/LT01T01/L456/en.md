## The letter ye

The letter (ye) /ˈje/ sounds like (yo) /ˈɟʝo/, which means I. This letter has three different sounds in spoken Spanish.

## Pronouncing the letter

Raise the middle of your tongue close to the roof of your mouth. Push voiced air through the gap to make friction. For the stronger sound, press your tongue firmly against the roof first. Release the air with a slight burst and friction. For the vowel sound, spread your lips wide and keep your tongue high. Touch your throat to feel the steady vibration of your vocal cords.

## Reading the letter

Between vowels or after most consonants, the letter sounds like /ʝ/, as in (mayo) /ˈma.ʝo/. At the start of a word or after n or l, it sounds like /ɟʝ/, as in (cónyuge) /ˈkon.ɟʝu.xe/. At the end of a word, it sounds like /i/, as in (rey) /rei̯/. The single word (y) /i/ also uses this vowel sound.

## Common pronunciation errors

Do not turn the strong sound into the English j in judge. Keep the tip of your tongue down behind your bottom teeth. Do not replace the friction sound with a weak English y in yes. Make sure you squeeze the air through a narrow space. Do not make the vowel too long like the ee in see. Keep the sound short and tense.
