## The letter efe

The name of this letter is (efe) /ˈɛfi/. Its main sound is heard in the word (fogo) /ˈfo.ɣu/, which means fire. This letter makes only one sound in European Portuguese.

## How to say it

Rest your upper teeth gently on your lower lip. Blow air smoothly through the narrow gap. Do not use your voice. You can feel cool air on your hand, but no buzzing in your throat.

## When it sounds like this

The letter always has the same sound in all positions. You can hear it in the word (café) /kɐˈfɛ/.

## Common mistakes

This sound is identical to the English sound in fish. You will not face any real pitfall with this letter.
