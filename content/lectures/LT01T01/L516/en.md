## Letter sound

The letter (té) /teː/ makes the sound /t/. You hear this sound in the word (tél) /ˈteːl/, which means winter. This letter can make two sounds.

## Mouth shape

For the first sound, press your tongue tip firmly against your upper gum. Build up breath behind your tongue, then release the air suddenly without vibrating your vocal cords. For the second sound, make the exact same mouth movement, but vibrate your vocal cords. Put your fingers on your throat to feel the buzz.

## Spelling rules

In most words, this letter makes the voiceless sound /t/, as in (torta) /ˈtortɒ/. It changes to the voiced sound /d/ when it stands directly before voiced consonants, as in (hatvan) /ˈhɒdvɒn/.

## Common mistakes

In English words like table, you release a small puff of air. In Hungarian, do not puff out extra breath when you release your tongue. Keep the release sharp, dry, and tight.
