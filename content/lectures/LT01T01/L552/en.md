## The letter zê

The letter is named (zê) /ze/. Its primary value is the sound /z/, heard in (zebra) /ˈze.βɾɐ/, which means zebra. This letter has three distinct sounds in European Portuguese.

## Making the sounds

For the first sound, place your tongue close to your upper gum. Push air out while vibrating your vocal cords. For the second sound, pull your tongue slightly back and round your lips softly. Blow air gently without using your voice. For the third sound, keep the same mouth shape, but turn your voice on. You can feel your throat vibrate with your hand.

## Reading the letter

At the beginning of a word or between vowels, it makes the sound /z/, as in (fazer) /fɐˈzeɾ/. At the end of a word before a pause, it makes the sound /ʃ/, as in (paz) /paʃ/. Before an unvoiced consonant, it also makes the sound /ʃ/, as in (luz clara) /luʃ ˈkla.ɾɐ/. Finally, before a voiced consonant, it makes the sound /ʒ/, as in (luz divina) /luʒ diˈvi.nɐ/.

## Avoiding mistakes

Do not pronounce word endings with the buzzing sound in buzz. When a word ends before a pause, use the quiet sound in shoe instead. Before a voiced consonant, switch smoothly to the softer sound in vision.
