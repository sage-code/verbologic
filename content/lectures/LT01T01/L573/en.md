## The letter U

The letter (U) /juː/ usually makes the sound /ʌ/, as in the word (up) /ʌp/. This word means toward a higher place. This letter makes four sounds in English words.

## How to say it

For the first sound, keep your mouth open and let your tongue rest flat. For the next sound, lift your tongue and circle your lips tight. For the third sound, push your lips into a tight ring. For the last sound, keep your lips loose and slightly curved. Touch your throat to feel your voice buzz for all four sounds.

## Spelling rules

In closed syllables, you say the sound /ʌ/, as in the word (cup) /kʌp/. In open syllables or before one consonant and silent e, you say /juː/, as in (music) /mjuː.zɪk/ or (cube) /kjuːb/. After r, l, or j, or before silent e, you say /uː/, as in (rule) /ruːl/ or (flute) /fluːt/. In special closed syllables, you say /ʊ/, as in (put) /pʊt/ or (full) /fʊl/.

## Common mistakes

Do not make the mouth too wide for the short vowel, or it sounds wrong. Keep your jaw relaxed. Do not forget to slide from a quick y sound into the long vowel when required. Do not push your tongue forward on the deeper sounds. Keep your tongue relaxed inside your mouth.
