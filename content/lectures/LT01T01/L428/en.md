## Sound of letter doppia vu

The letter (doppia vu) /ˈdoppja vu/ is a foreign letter in Italian. Its main sound is the sound /w/, heard in (web) /ˈwɛb/, meaning web, internet. This foreign letter makes two sounds in Italian.

## How to say doppia vu

To make the first sound, place your top teeth gently on your bottom lip. Blow air through while your vocal cords vibrate. For the second sound, make a tight small circle with your lips. Pull the back of your tongue up toward the roof of your mouth and voice the sound. Place your hand on your throat to feel the buzz.

## Reading doppia vu

This letter appears only in borrowed words. In words from German and in older loanwords, it makes the sound /v/, as in (wurstel) /ˈvurstel/. In words from English, it makes the sound /w/, as in (weekend) /wiˈkɛnd/.

## Common mistakes with doppia vu

Do not mix up the two sounds in borrowed words. If you see a word from German, do not use the English sound in win. Place your top teeth on your bottom lip, as in vine. If the word comes from English, make a small circle with your lips instead.
