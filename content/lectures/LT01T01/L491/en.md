## Letter name dzsé

The letter is called (dzsé) /dʒeː/. It makes the sound /d͡ʒ/, as in the word (dzsem) /ˈd͡ʒɛm/, which means jam. This letter has only one sound.

## Pronouncing the letter

Press the tip of your tongue against the roof of your mouth, just behind your upper teeth. Push air gently against this seal, then release it while your vocal cords vibrate. Put a hand on your throat. You will feel a strong buzz when you make the sound.

## Letter spelling rules

This letter always produces the same sound wherever it appears in a word. You pronounce it as the sound /d͡ʒ/ in every position, for instance in the word (tiniédzser) /ˈtini.eːd͡ʒɛr/.

## Comparing similar sounds

This sound is identical to the first sound in the English word judge. Make sure your vocal cords vibrate, so you do not say the softer sound in church.
