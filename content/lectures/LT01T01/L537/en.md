## The sound capa

The name of this letter is (capa) /ˈkapɐ/. It has only one sound, heard in the word (kilo) /ˈki.lu/, which means kilo or kilogram.

## Making the sound

Raise the back of your tongue to touch the roof of your mouth. Block the air briefly, then release it sharply without using your voice. Place your fingers on your throat to feel that your vocal cords do not vibrate.

## Reading the letter

This letter appears only in foreign loanwords and international units of measurement. In these borrowed words, it always makes the sound /k/, as in (kit) /ˈkit/ and (karting) /ˈkaɾ.tĩŋ/.

## Sound pitfalls

This sound is just like the sound in the English word kite, but you must release less breath. Do not breathe out strongly after releasing the air from the back of your mouth.
