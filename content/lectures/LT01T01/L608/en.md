## Sounds of lk

The spelling -lk sounds like the sound /k/. You hear it in (walk) /wɔːk/, which means to walk. This letter group makes two different sounds in English words.

## Making the sounds

For the first sound, lift the back of your tongue to touch the roof. Stop the air, then release it with a sharp burst. Your vocal cords stay still. For the second sound, first touch the roof with your tongue tip. Let air flow around the sides, then make that sharp burst.

## Reading the spelling

At the end of words where the first letter is silent, you say the sound /k/. You hear this pattern in (talk) /tɔːk/. After vowels where both letters are sounded, you say the sound /lk/. You hear this in (milk) /mɪlk/ and in (silk) /sɪlk/.

## Common mistakes

Do not pronounce the first letter in words where it stays quiet. Keep the sound simple and clean. Make the sharp burst at the very end.
