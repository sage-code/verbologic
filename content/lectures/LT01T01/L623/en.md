## Sound of -eigh

The letters eigh sound like /eɪ/. Their main sound is heard in the word (weigh) /weɪ/, which means to measure how heavy something is. This spelling has two different sounds in English. You never pronounce the individual letters separately.

## How to say -eigh

To make the first sound, spread your lips and raise the front of your tongue toward the roof of your mouth. Glide smoothly upward toward a narrower vowel. For the second sound, open your jaw wider, then move your tongue upward. Put a hand on your throat to feel your voice buzz throughout both sounds.

## Spelling of -eigh

Most words with this spelling use the sound /eɪ/. You hear this pattern in (sleigh) /sleɪ/. In a few words, this spelling makes the sound /aɪ/. You hear this pattern in (height) /haɪt/.

## Mistakes with -eigh

Do not try to speak the letters g and h. They are silent here. Do not stop after the first part of the glide. Make sure your tongue moves upward until the vowel finishes cleanly.
