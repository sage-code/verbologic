## The letter ve

The letter (вэ) /vɛ/ mainly makes the sound /v/, that is a hard v sound. You hear this sound in the word (вода) /vɐˈda/, which means water. Overall, this letter makes four different sounds in Russian.

## How to say it

Touch your upper front teeth lightly with your lower lip and vibrate your vocal cords. For the softer version, raise the middle of your tongue toward the roof of your mouth. For the next sound, keep the same mouth shape but turn your voice off completely. For the final sound, keep your voice off while raising your tongue toward the roof. Put your hand on your throat to feel when your voice turns on and off.

## Spelling rules

Before hard vowels and voiced consonants, it makes the sound /v/, that is a hard v sound, as in (волк) /volk/. Before soft vowels or a soft sign, it makes the sound /vʲ/, that is a soft v sound, as in (ветер) /ˈvʲetʲɪr/. At the end of a word or before voiceless consonants, it makes the sound /f/, that is a hard f sound, as in (вход) /fxot/. At the end of a word before a soft sign, it makes the sound /fʲ/, that is a soft f sound, as in (кровь) /krofʲ/.

## Common mistakes

Do not turn the sound into a w sound, like the first sound in water. Always touch your lower lip against your upper teeth. For the soft sounds, do not add a separate y sound like in view. Keep the lip contact and tongue lift together in one smooth sound.
