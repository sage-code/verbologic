## Sound of the letter

The letter (o) /o/ is called (o) /o/. It usually makes the sound /ɔ/, that is an open o, like in (olive) /ɔ.liv/, which means olive. This letter has two different sounds in French. Both sounds are clear vowels.

## How to say it

For the first sound, drop your jaw slightly. Round your lips gently and pull your tongue back. For the second sound, push your lips forward into a small tight circle. Keep your tongue pulled back and raise the back of your tongue higher. Touch your throat to feel your vocal cords vibrate for both.

## When to use each

This letter makes the sound /ɔ/, that is an open o, in most closed syllables. You can hear it in (porte) /pɔʁt/. It makes the sound /o/, that is a closed o, at the end of a word, as in (vélo) /ve.lo/. It also makes this sound before an s that sounds like a z, as in (rose) /ʁoz/.

## Common mistakes

Do not let your lips glide into a w sound at the end, like in the English word boat. English vowels often move, but French vowels stay completely still. Keep your lips frozen in a circle until the sound finishes.
