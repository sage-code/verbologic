## Sound of quê

The letter (quê) /ke/ mainly makes the sound /kw/, like in the word (quatro) /ˈkwa.tɾu/, which means four. This letter has two different sounds in European Portuguese.

## How to say it

For the first sound, press the back of your tongue against the soft roof of your mouth. Release the air sharply while you round your lips into a tight circle. For the second sound, release the air without rounding your lips. Place a hand on your throat to feel that your vocal cords stay still.

## Reading and writing

This letter always appears with the vowel u. Before the letters a or o, it sounds like /kw/, as in (quase) /ˈkwa.zɨ/. Before the letters e or i, the following vowel u stays silent, so the letter makes the sound /k/, as in (que) /kɨ/ and (aqui) /ɐˈki/.

## Common mistakes

Do not add a w sound when the letter stands before e or i. English words like quick always keep that sound. In Portuguese, keep the second letter silent so it sounds like the k in keep.
