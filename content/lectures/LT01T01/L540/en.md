## The letter ene

The letter (ene) /ˈɛni/ makes the sound /n/ as in (noite) /ˈnoj.tɨ/, which means night. This letter has two different sounds in European Portuguese.

## Making the sounds

For the first sound, press the tip of your tongue behind your top front teeth. Let the air flow through your nose and vibrate your vocal cords. For the other sound, do not block the air with your tongue. Drop the soft back of your mouth so air flows out through your nose. Place two fingers on your nose to feel it buzz.

## Reading the letter

Before a vowel, the letter sounds like the sound /n/ in (ano) /ˈɐ.nu/. At the end of a syllable, the letter is not pronounced on its own. Instead, it turns the vowel before it into a nasal sound, as in (canto) /ˈkɐ̃.tu/ and (ponte) /ˈpõ.tɨ/.

## Avoiding mistakes

At the end of a syllable, do not tap your tongue tip like in the English word tent. Keep your tongue low and let the air escape freely through your nose.
