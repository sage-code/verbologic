## The letter name

The letter (Ü) /yː/ is called (Ü) /yː/, that is a long close front vowel with circle lips. Its main sound is heard in the word (Übung) /ˈyːbʊŋ/, which means exercise, practice. This German letter makes two different sounds.

## How to say it

To make the first sound, place your tongue high and forward as for see. Push your lips tightly forward into a small circle, then hold the sound steady. For the second sound, pull your tongue slightly back and lower your jaw a little bit. Form a looser circle with your lips. Put a finger before your lips to check the push.

## When to use each sound

You make the sound /yː/, that is a long close front vowel with circle lips, in open or stressed syllables. The word (Tür) /tyːɐ̯/ shows this sound. You make the sound /ʏ/, that is a short close front vowel with circle lips, before double consonants or consonant clusters. The word (fünf) /fʏnf/ shows this second sound.

## Common mistakes to avoid

Do not replace the sound with the vowel of goose. For that English word your tongue pulls back, but German needs your tongue forward. Do not replace it with the vowel of see. Keep your lips pushed forward into a circle instead of spreading them wide.
