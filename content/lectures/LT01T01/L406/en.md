## Sound of the letter bi

The letter (bi) /bi/ makes the sound /b/, as in the word (bene) /ˈbɛ.ne/, which means well. This letter always has one single sound in Italian.

## How to say bi

Press your lips firmly together to stop the air. Release your lips suddenly so the air bursts out. Vibrate your vocal cords at the same time. Place your hand on your throat to feel the gentle buzz.

## Spelling of bi

The letter (bi) /bi/ always makes the sound /b/ everywhere it appears. You hear it at the start of (bello) /ˈbɛl.lo/. You also hear it inside (albero) /ˈal.be.ro/.

## Compare bi with English

This letter sounds just like the English sound in the word boat. Make sure your lips close fully. Do not puff out too much air when you open them.
