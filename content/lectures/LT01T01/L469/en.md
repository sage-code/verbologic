## Letter sound elle

The name of this letter is (elle) /ɛl/. Its main sound is the sound /l/, like in the word (lune) /lyn/, which means moon. This letter has one spoken sound in standard speech, but it can also stay completely silent.

## How to say elle

Press the tip of your tongue against the ridge behind your upper front teeth. Keep the sides of your tongue down so air passes freely around them. Turn on your voice. You can feel your throat vibrate with your fingers. Keep the back of your tongue flat and relaxed throughout the whole sound.

## Spelling rules elle

In most positions, you pronounce this letter as the sound /l/, like in (bal) /bal/. At the end of a few specific words, the letter is not pronounced. For example, it stays silent in the word (fusil) /fy.zi/ and in the word (outil) /u.ti/.

## Mistakes to avoid elle

Do not raise the back of your tongue at the end of a word. Many learners make a dark sound, like the end of bell. Keep your tongue flat and clear instead. Never drop the sound when it sits at the end of most normal words.
