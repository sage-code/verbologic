## Sound of es

The letter (эс) /ɛs/ makes four different sounds in Russian words. Its primary sound is heard in the word (солнце) /ˈsont͡sə/, which means sun.

## How to say es

Put your tongue tip behind your lower teeth. Blow air through the narrow gap without voice for the hard hiss. For the soft hiss, raise the middle of your tongue toward the roof. Keep your vocal cords vibrating for the two voiced versions. Place a hand on your throat to feel the buzz.

## Reading the letter es

Before hard vowels or at the end of a word, it sounds as /s/, that is a plain s, like in (сом) /som/. Before soft vowels or a soft sign, it sounds as /sʲ/, that is a softened s, in (семь) /sʲemʲ/. Before voiced consonants, it turns into /z/, that is a plain z, in (сделать) /ˈzdʲeɫətʲ/. Before voiced soft consonants, it becomes /zʲ/, that is a softened z, in (сбить) /zʲbʲitʲ/.

## Common mistakes with es

You might forget to soften the hissing sound before soft vowels. Do not say the start of simple words like sit. Lift the middle of your tongue high to make a crisp, light hiss. You might also forget to voice the letter before voiced consonants. Let your throat buzz strongly so it does not sound like sea.
