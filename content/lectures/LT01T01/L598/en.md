## Letter sound

You call this letter (X) /gz/. Its main sound is heard in the word (box) /bɒks/, which means a square container. This letter has three different sounds.

## Mouth movements

For the first sound, stop air with the back of your tongue, release it without voice, and push air through your teeth. For the second sound, make that same stop and hiss, but keep your voice buzzing. For the third sound, blow air smoothly over the tongue tip with voice. Feel your throat buzz on the voiced sounds.

## Reading rules

At the end of a word or before quiet consonants, pronounce the sound /ks/ as in (tax) /tæks/. Between vowels right before a stressed syllable, say the sound /ɡz/ as in (exam) /ɪɡ.zæm/ or (exist) /ɪɡ.zɪst/. At the beginning of a word, pronounce the sound /z/ as in (xylophone) /zaɪ.lə.fəʊn/ or (xenon) /zɛn.ɒn/.

## Common mistakes

Do not add an extra vowel sound before this letter at the start of a word. Many learners try to pronounce a stop sound first. Start smoothly with a buzzing hiss, exactly like the first sound in zoo. Keep the air flowing without stopping it.
