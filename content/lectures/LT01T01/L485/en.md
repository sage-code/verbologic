## Sound of the letter á

The letter (á) /aː/ is called (á) /aː/. It makes the sound /aː/, as in the word (ár) /ˈaːr/, which means price or flood. This letter always makes only one sound in Hungarian.

## How to say it

Open your mouth wide and keep your tongue low and flat. Do not round your lips. Let your voice flow out steadily to make a long clear vowel. Place your hand on your throat to feel the strong steady vibration.

## Spelling rules

This letter always makes the long sound /aː/ in every position. You hear this sound in the word (ház) /haːz/.

## Common mistakes

You might make this vowel too short. It sounds very close to the vowel in the English word father. Keep your mouth wide open and hold the sound twice as long as a short vowel.
