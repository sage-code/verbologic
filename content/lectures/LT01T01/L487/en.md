## The letter cé

The letter (cé) /tseː/ is the fifth letter of the Hungarian alphabet. Its primary sound is the sound /ts/, heard in (cica) /ˈtsi.tsɒ/, which means kitten. This letter produces two sounds in total.

## Pronouncing cé

For the first sound, place the tip of your tongue behind your upper front teeth. Build air pressure, then release it quickly with a sharp hiss. Keep your vocal cords quiet. For the second sound, make the exact same movement, but add your voice so your throat vibrates. Put two fingers on your throat to feel the buzz.

## Spelling rules for cé

In most positions, this letter makes the sound /ts/. You hear this at the end of (arc) /ɒrts/. Before a voiced consonant, the letter changes its sound to /dz/. You hear this in the word (lécből) /ˈleːdzbøːl/.

## Common mistakes

Do not pronounce this letter as a hard sound like the c in cat. Never pronounce it as a soft sound like the c in city. Instead, think of the sound at the end of cats. Unlike in English, this crisp sound easily starts a word.
