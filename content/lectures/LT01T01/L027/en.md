## The Sound of W

In Romanian, **W** is an imported letter found strictly in foreign borrowings, names, and international terms. Depending on the origin of the word, it is pronounced either as the labiodental fricative `/v/` (like *v* in English *vase*) or as the labiovelar glide `/w/` (like *w* in English *water*). Older or Germanic borrowings tend to use `/v/`, while modern English loans retain `/w/`.

## How to Pronounce W

When pronouncing **W** as `/v/`, lightly press your upper front teeth against your lower lip and vibrate your vocal cords. For the English-style `/w/` pronunciation, round your lips tightly into an *oo* shape while raising the back of your tongue toward the soft palate. Release the air smoothly into the following vowel without letting your teeth touch your lips.

## Spelling and Usage

**W** is one of the newest additions to the official 31-letter Romanian alphabet, introduced primarily to accommodate international terminology. It appears in abbreviations, international units of measurement such as *watt*, and common lifestyle loanwords like *weekend* or *whisky*. Native Romanian words never contain this letter.

## Pronunciation Variations and Traps

A frequent dilemma for learners is deciding whether to pronounce **W** as `/v/` or `/w/`. For example, scientific terms like *watt* are universally read as `/vat/`, whereas digital terms like *web* are read as `/web/`. Avoid substituting a full vowel `/u/` where a crisp consonantal glide `/w/` is expected in modern loanwords.
