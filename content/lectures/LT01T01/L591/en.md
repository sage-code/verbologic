## Letter sound

The letter (S) /ʃ/ mainly makes the sound /s/. You hear this sound in the word (sun) /sʌn/, which means sun. This letter has three different sounds in English words.

## Mouth movements

For the first sound, place your tongue close to the tooth ridge behind your top teeth. Push air through the small gap without your voice. Your throat stays still. For the second sound, make the exact same mouth shape with your tongue. Now turn your voice on. You can feel your throat buzz with a finger on your neck.

## Spelling rules

This letter makes the sound /s/ at the beginning of words, as in (stop) /stɒp/. It also makes the sound /s/ before sounds that use no voice. Between vowels or after voiced sounds, it makes the sound /z/, as in (rose) /roʊz/ or (busy) /bɪ.zi/. The letter is silent in a few special words, such as (island) /aɪ.lənd/.

## Common mistakes

Do not confuse the quiet hissing sound with the buzzing sound. When you make the sound /z/, your vocal cords must vibrate. Check this by pressing two fingers gently to your throat while speaking.
