## The letter vu

The letter (vu) /vu/ makes the sound /v/. You hear it in the word (vita) /ˈvi.ta/, which means life. This letter has only one sound in Italian.

## Making the sound

Rest your upper teeth gently on your bottom lip. Push air through the small gap while you use your voice. Touch your throat with your hand to feel the strong buzz.

## When it appears

The letter (vu) /vu/ always makes the sound /v/. You hear it anywhere in a word, as in (avanti) /aˈvan.ti/.

## Mistakes to avoid

Do not confuse this sound with the sound /b/ or the sound /w/. Keep your top teeth lightly touching your lower lip, as in the English word vine. Never round both lips as you would for wine.
