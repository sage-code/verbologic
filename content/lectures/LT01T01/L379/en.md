## Sound of zhe

Russian speakers call this letter (жэ) /ʒɛ/. It usually sounds like the sound /ʐ/, as in the word (жук) /ʐuk/, which means beetle. This letter makes two different sounds.

## How to say it

Curl your tongue tip slightly back behind your top teeth. Push air through the narrow gap with your voice turned on. For the unvoiced sound, make the exact same mouth shape, but whisper without using your voice. Place your hand on your throat to feel the strong vibration stop.

## Reading rules

This letter usually sounds like the voiced sound /ʐ/. You hear it before vowels and voiced consonants, like in (жар) /ʐar/. It changes into the unvoiced sound /ʂ/ at the end of a word, as in (муж) /muʂ/. It also makes the unvoiced sound /ʂ/ before voiceless consonants, as in (ложка) /ˈloʂkə/.

## Common mistakes

Do not make the soft sound in the English word measure. That sound keeps your tongue flat. Pull your tongue tip further back and curl it slightly up. Do not soften the unvoiced sound like the start of ship. Keep your tongue firm and drawn back.
