## Letter sound

The letter (е) /je/ makes the main sound in (ель) /jelʲ/, which means spruce. This letter has four sounds in total.

## How to say it

For the first sound, glide your tongue from the roof down and unround your lips. For the second sound, spread your lips and keep your tongue high. For the third sound, start with a quick glide and relax your tongue. For the fourth sound, lift your tongue close to the roof. Feel your vocal cords vibrate on your throat for all four sounds.

## Reading rules

Stressed after a vowel, it sounds like the sound /je/ in (поезд) /ˈpo.jist/. Stressed after a consonant, it makes the sound /e/ in (лес) /lʲes/. Unstressed at the beginning of a word, it gives the sound /jɪ/ in (еда) /jɪˈda/. Unstressed after a consonant, it makes the sound /ɪ/ in (весна) /vʲɪsˈna/.

## Common mistakes

Do not turn the sound into the long sound in English day. Keep your tongue steady and do not slide into another vowel. When it follows a consonant, do not insert a separate y sound like in yes. Instead, soften the consonant itself before the vowel.
