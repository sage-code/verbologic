## The letter ele

This letter is called (ele) /ˈele/. Its main sound is heard in the word (luna) /ˈlu.na/, which means moon. The letter makes three slightly different sounds in Spanish.

## Making the sounds

For the first sound, place your tongue tip against the ridge behind your upper teeth. Let the air flow past the sides of your tongue. For the second sound, press your tongue tip directly against the back of your top teeth. For the third sound, raise the middle of your tongue toward the roof of your mouth while touching the ridge. Your voice vibrates, and you can feel this buzz on your throat.

## Reading this letter

Most of the time, the letter makes the sound /l/, that is an l on the ridge, as in (sol) /sol/. Before the consonants t and d, it becomes the sound /l̪/, that is an l against the upper teeth, as in (alto) /ˈal̪.to/. Before ch or y, it makes the sound /lʲ/, that is a softened l, as in (el chico) /elʲ ˈt͡ʃi.ko/.

## Sounds to avoid

Do not swallow this consonant at the end of a syllable. In English words like ball, the back of your tongue pulls down. Keep your tongue high and light in Spanish, like the first sound in leaf.
