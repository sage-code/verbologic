## Sound of the letter pê

The letter (pê) /pe/ makes the sound /p/. You hear it in the word (pão) /ˈpɐ̃w̃/, which means bread. This letter makes two sounds.

## How to say pê

Press your lips tightly together and block the air. Release your lips cleanly without adding a breath. Keep your vocal cords still for this first sound. For the second sound, press your lips together in the same spot. Make your vocal cords vibrate as you let the air escape. Put your hand on your throat to feel that vibration.

## Spelling of pê

In regular positions, this letter gives the sound /p/. You hear it in (pai) /ˈpaj/ and in (copo) /ˈkɔ.pu/. In quick speech before a voiced consonant, it shifts to the sound /b/. That happens in the word (subepático) /su.bɛ.ˈpa.ti.ku/.

## Comparing pê with English

In English words like pen, you release a small puff of air. In Portuguese, you must avoid that puff completely. Hold a finger before your lips. The air must not move your finger when you release the sound.
