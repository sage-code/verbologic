## Sound of esse

The name of this Italian letter is spoken as (esse) /ˈɛsse/. Its main sound is the sound /s/, heard in (sera) /ˈse.ra/, which means evening. This letter can make two different sounds in Italian.

## How to say esse

For the first sound, place your tongue tip close behind your upper teeth. Blow air out without using your voice. For the second sound, keep your mouth in the exact same shape. Blow air out while using your voice. You can feel your throat buzz with a hand on your neck.

## Spelling rules for esse

At the start of a word before a vowel, it makes the sound /s/, as in (sole) /ˈso.le/. Next to a voiceless consonant, it also makes the sound /s/, like in (testa) /ˈtɛ.sta/. Before a voiced consonant, it becomes the sound /z/, heard in (sbaglio) /ˈzbaʎ.ʎo/. Between two vowels, it usually makes the sound /z/, as in (rosa) /ˈrɔ.za/.

## Common mistakes with esse

Do not turn the sound /s/ into a sound /z/ when a word begins with a vowel. In English, words like please end with a buzzing sound. Keep your voice quiet when you say the sound /s/ in Italian.
