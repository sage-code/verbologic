## The letter pe

The name of this letter sounds like (пэ) /pɛ/. Its main sound appears in (папа) /ˈpa.pə/, which means dad. The letter makes three different sounds.

## How to say pe

To say the plain sound, press your lips firmly together. Release them without letting out a puff of air. Keep your vocal cords still. To make the soft sound, arch your tongue high toward the roof of your mouth while closing your lips. Release the lips cleanly. To make the voiced sound, close your lips and vibrate your vocal cords. Touch your throat to feel that vibration.

## Spelling rules for pe

Before consonants, at word ends, and before hard vowels, the letter makes the sound /p/, that is a plain voiceless sound, as in (суп) /sup/. Before soft vowels and the soft sign, it makes the sound /pʲ/, that is a softened voiceless sound, as in (песня) /ˈpʲe.sʲnʲə/. Before voiced consonants, it changes to the sound /b/, as in (сбербанк) /zbʲɪrˈbank/.

## Common mistakes with pe

Do not puff out air like in the English word pet. Stop the breath burst completely. When making the soft sound, do not add an extra consonant sound like the first sound in yes. Simply keep the middle of your tongue high against the roof of your mouth as your lips part.
