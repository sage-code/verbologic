## Sound of the letter E

The letter (E) /iː/ sounds like /ɛ/ in the word (egg) /ɛɡ/. This word means egg. The letter has four different sounds across English words.

## Mouth shape

For the first sound, open your mouth slightly and keep your tongue forward. For the second sound, smile widely and raise your tongue high near the roof. For the third sound, relax your tongue and lips completely in the middle. The fourth variant makes no sound at all. Touch your throat to feel your voice buzz on all three spoken sounds.

## Spelling rules

In closed syllables with a consonant after, you make the sound /ɛ/ as in (bed) /bɛd/. In open stressed syllables, you make the long sound /iː/ as in (me) /miː/. In unstressed syllables, you use the weak sound /ə/ as in (problem) /prɒb.ləm/. Finally, the letter is not pronounced at the end of a word like (take) /teɪk/.

## Common mistakes

Do not confuse the short sound with the sound in cat. Keep your jaw closer to your teeth and do not drop it too low. Also, never say a sound at the end of words when the letter sits silent. Keep your lips still at the finish.
