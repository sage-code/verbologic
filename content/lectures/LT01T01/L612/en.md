## The spelling bt

The letter group -bt sounds like the sound /t/. You hear this in the word (debt) /dɛt/, which means money you owe. This group has only one spoken sound.

## Producing the sound

Press the tip of your tongue against the ridge behind your upper teeth. Stop the air for a moment. Then drop your tongue to release a small puff of air. Keep your vocal cords still. Touch your throat to feel that it does not buzz.

## Reading the letters

At the end of words, the letter b remains completely silent. The following letter sounds like the sound /t/. You can hear this clearly in the word (doubt) /daʊt/.

## Avoiding mistakes

The only common mistake is trying to pronounce both letters together. Do not close your lips to make a b sound. Move straight to the t sound, exactly like in the normal English word cat.
