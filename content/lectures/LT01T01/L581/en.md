## Letter sound

The letter (A) /ɑː/ sounds like the short vowel in (apple) /æp.əl/, meaning apple. This letter has four different sounds in English.

## Mouth shape

For the first sound, open your mouth wide and flatten your tongue low. For the second sound, glide from an open mouth to spread lips. For the third sound, keep your mouth relaxed and make a short, weak sound. For the fourth sound, open your mouth fully and pull your tongue back. Feel your throat vibrate for all four sounds.

## Reading rules

In closed syllables, read the short sound /æ/ as in (cat) /kæt/. Before a silent e or in open syllables, make the long sound /eɪ/ as in (cake) /keɪk/. In weak syllables without stress, make the soft sound /ə/ as in (about) /ə.baʊt/. Before the letter r, make the deep sound /ɑː/ as in (father) /fɑː.ðər/.

## Common mistakes

Do not mix up the short sound /æ/ with a simple open sound. Drop your jaw lower than you would for bed. Do not say the long sound /eɪ/ as one flat vowel. Make sure your tongue moves up at the end. In weak syllables, do not say a clear vowel. Keep the sound very short and quiet.
