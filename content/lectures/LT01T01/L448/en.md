## Letter sound

The letter (cu) /ku/ makes the sound /k/. You hear it in the word (queso) /ˈke.so/, which means cheese. This letter makes only one sound in Spanish. It is always followed by another letter to write this sound before vowels.

## Pronunciation guide

Press the back of your tongue against the soft roof of your mouth. Stop the breath completely for a moment. Then release the air sharply without using your vocal cords. Hold a hand before your mouth to feel a light burst of air.

## Spelling rules

The letter (cu) /ku/ always appears before the letter u. Together with that letter, it makes the sound /k/ before the letters e or i. For example, you hear this sound in the word (química) /ˈki.mi.ka/.

## Common mistakes

Do not pronounce the following vowel letter as a separate sound like in the English word quick. The sound is just a clean consonant, exactly like the first sound in the English word keep. Release the sound cleanly without a strong puff of air.
