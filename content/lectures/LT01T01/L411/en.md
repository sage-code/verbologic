## The letter gi

The letter (gi) /dʒi/ makes its main sound in the word (gatto) /ˈɡat.to/, which means cat. This letter has two different sounds in Italian.

## How to say gi

Press the back of your tongue against the roof of your mouth. Release the air suddenly while you buzz your voice. For the other sound, press the tip of your tongue behind your top front teeth. Push air through while your voice buzzes. Place your fingers on your throat to feel that vibration.

## Spelling of gi

This letter sounds like /ɡ/ before the vowels a, o, u, or before a consonant, as in (grande) /ˈɡran.de/. It sounds like /dʒ/ before the letters e or i, as in (gelato) /dʒeˈla.to/ and (giorno) /ˈdʒor.no/.

## Mistakes with gi

Do not soften this letter before the vowel sound in get. The first sound must match the hard sound in goat, not the soft start of gentle.
