## The letter effe

The letter (effe) /ˈɛffe/ makes the sound /f/. You hear it in the word (fare) /ˈfa.re/, which means to do or to make. This letter has only one sound in Italian.

## Saying the letter

Rest your upper teeth lightly on your lower lip. Push air out gently through the small gap between them. Keep your vocal cords completely still. You can place your hand on your throat to feel that it does not buzz.

## Reading the letter

This letter always makes the sound /f/ in every position. You hear it as a double letter in (caffè) /kafˈfɛ/. Hold the sound longer when you see two of them together.

## Comparing with English

This sound is identical to the sound in the English word find. You do not need to change anything about how you say it. Just hold the sound longer when the letter is doubled.
