## The letters kn

The letters kn sound like the sound /n/. In the word (knee) /ni/, they mean a knee. These two letters make only one single sound.

## Mouth shape

Press the tip of your tongue against the roof of your mouth. Send the air smoothly through your nose. Turn on your voice. You can feel your throat buzz with your fingers.

## Reading the letters

The letters kn appear at the beginning of a word before a vowel. You never pronounce the first letter. You say only the sound /n/, as in (knot) /nɒt/.

## Common mistakes

Do not pronounce the first letter. A common mistake is saying an extra tapping sound at the start. Begin cleanly with the nose sound, exactly like the start of net.
