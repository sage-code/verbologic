## The letter Ce

The German letter (Ce) /tseː/ usually sounds like the sound /k/. You hear it in the word (Computer) /kɔmˈpjuːtɐ/, which means an electronic machine. This letter produces three different sounds across borrowed words.

## Making the sounds

For the first sound, lift the back of your tongue against the roof. Release the trapped air sharply without your voice. For the second sound, touch the tip of your tongue behind your top front teeth. Release the burst of air into a sharp hiss. For the third sound, keep your tongue close to your upper gum. Blow air forward with no voice. Put a hand on your throat to feel that it does not buzz.

## Spelling rules

You say the sound /k/ before the vowels a, o, or u, or before consonants in borrowed words, as in (Café) /kaˈfeː/. You make the sound /ts/ before the front vowels e, i, ä, or ö in words from Latin, as in (Cent) /tsɛnt/. You use the soft sound /s/ in words from French before front vowels, as in (City) /ˈsɪti/.

## Avoiding mistakes

Do not pronounce the sound /ts/ as the simple English sound in see. Keep the sharp burst of air heard at the end of cats. Do not add a heavy puff of air when you say the sound /k/ after another consonant.
