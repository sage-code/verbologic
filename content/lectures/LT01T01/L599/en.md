## Sound of letter X

You call this letter (X) /kʃ/. Its most common sound is heard in (fox) /fɒks/, which means a wild animal with red fur. The letter makes three different sounds in English words.

## How to say it

For the first sound, lift the back of your tongue to stop the air. Release it with a hissing stream behind your upper teeth without voice. For the second sound, make the same movements, but keep your voice buzzing. Touch your throat to feel that buzz. For the third sound, bring your teeth close together and blow air while humming.

## Spelling rules

At the end of a word or before quiet consonants, it sounds like the sound /ks/ in (six) /sɪks/. Before a stressed vowel, you say the sound /ɡz/ as in (exam) /ɪɡ.zæm/, or in (exist) /ɪɡ.zɪst/. At the beginning of a word, it takes the buzzing sound /z/ in (xylophone) /zaɪ.lə.fəʊn/, and in (xenon) /zɛ.nɒn/.

## Common mistakes

Do not drop the first stop sound when you say the ending combinations. Make sure your tongue touches the roof of your mouth before you hiss. At the start of a word, never add a stop sound before the buzz. Start smoothly with the sound /z/.
