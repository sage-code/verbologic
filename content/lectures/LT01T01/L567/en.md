## Letter sound O

The name of the letter is (O) /oʊ/. Its main sound is the vowel /ɑ/, heard in the word (ox) /ɑks/, which means an ox. This letter has four sounds in total.

## How to say O

For the first sound, drop your jaw low, keep your tongue flat, and make a voiced sound. For the second sound, start with your lips open, then round them into a small circle. For the third sound, relax your tongue and lips completely and make a quick weak voice sound. For the fourth sound, push your lips forward tightly and pull your tongue back. Feel your vocal cords vibrate for each sound.

## Spelling rules for O

In a closed syllable with stress, this letter makes the sound /ɑ/, as in (hot) /hɑt/. At the end of an open syllable or before a silent e, it makes the sound /oʊ/, as in (go) /ɡoʊ/ or (home) /hoʊm/. In an unstressed syllable, it becomes the weak sound /ə/, as in (lemon) /lɛ.mən/ and (atom) /æ.təm/. In several common words, it sounds like /uː/, as in (do) /duː/ and (move) /muːv/.

## Common mistakes with O

Do not keep your lips completely still when you make the sound /oʊ/. Move your lips into a tight circle at the end. For the sound /ɑ/, open your mouth wider and drop your jaw lower than you might expect. For the weak sound /ə/, make the vowel very short and quiet.
