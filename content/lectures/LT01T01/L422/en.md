## Letter sound ti

The letter (ti) /ti/ makes the sound /t/. You hear this sound in the word (tempo) /ˈtɛm.po/, which means time or weather. This letter always makes this one sound in Italian.

## Saying the sound

Press the tip of your tongue against the back of your upper front teeth. Stop the air completely for a brief moment. Release the air cleanly with no puff of breath. Keep your vocal cords silent. Hold a hand before your lips to check that no warm breath escapes.

## Reading the sound

The letter always sounds as the clean sound /t/ in all positions. You find it at the start, inside or doubled in words. For example, listen to the sound /t/ inside the word (notte) /ˈnɔt.te/.

## Avoiding mistakes

In English words like top, you blow out a puff of air. In Italian, you must hold that breath back. Also place your tongue against your upper teeth, not further back on the gum ridge like in the English word table.
