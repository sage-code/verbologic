## Sound of stle

The ending -stle is pronounced /səl/. Its main sound appears in (castle) /kɑː.səl/, which means a large stone stronghold. This spelling pattern always makes one single sound combination.

## How to say it

Blow air smoothly over your tongue tip near the roof of your mouth. Then touch the tip of your tongue behind your top front teeth. Let the voiced air pass through the sides of your mouth. Put a hand on your neck to feel your throat vibrate at the very end.

## Spelling rules

At the end of a word, the group -stle sounds like /səl/. The letter t stays completely silent, as heard in (rustle) /rʌ.səl/.

## Common mistakes

Do not pronounce the letter t in this ending. Skip the t completely so it sounds like the middle of muscle.
