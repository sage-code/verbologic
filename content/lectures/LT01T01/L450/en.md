## Letter ese

The letter (ese) /ˈese/ makes the sound /s̺/, as in (sol) /ˈsol/, which means sun. This letter has two sounds in European Spanish.

## How to say ese

For the first sound, lift the tip of your tongue toward the ridge behind your upper teeth. Blow air out without using your voice. For the second sound, make the exact same mouth shape, but turn your voice on. You can feel your throat vibrate with your fingers during the second sound.

## Spelling of ese

The letter (ese) /ˈese/ usually makes the sound /s̺/, like in the word (casa) /ˈkasa/. Before voiced consonants, it can sound like /z̺/, as in (mismo) /ˈmizmo/ or (desde) /ˈdezðe/.

## Mistakes to avoid

Do not flatten the front of your tongue like in the English word sea. Use the very tip of your tongue raised slightly higher instead. Also, never make a buzzing sound between two vowels like in the English word rise.
