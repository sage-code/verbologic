## Sound of letter i

The name of this letter is (i) /i/. Its main sound is the sound /i/, heard in the word (itt) /ˈitː/, which means here. This letter always represents one single sound.

## How to say it

Spread your lips wide into a smile. Raise the front of your tongue high towards the roof of your mouth. Keep your jaw almost closed. Let your vocal cords vibrate freely. You can touch your throat to feel the strong buzzing.

## Reading this letter

This letter always makes the sound /i/ in all positions. You can hear it at the start of the word (ing) /ˈiŋɡ/.

## Common mistakes

Do not pronounce this letter like the sound in the English word sit. Make your tongue higher and spread your lips wider, closer to the vowel in machine, but keep it very short.
