## Sound of letter ü

The name of this letter is (ü) /y/. Its main sound is the sound /y/, heard in the word (üveg) /ˈyvɛɡ/, which means glass. This letter always has one single sound.

## Saying letter ü

Push your tongue high and forward, as if making an ee sound. Now round your lips tightly into a small circle, as if whistling. Keep your tongue forward while your lips stay round. Let your voice hum steadily. You can feel your lips pushing forward firmly with your finger.

## Spelling of letter ü

This letter always makes the sound /y/ in all positions. You hear this sound clearly in the word (tűz) /tyːz/.

## Comparing letter ü

You may be tempted to use the sound in English boot. Do not pull your tongue back into your throat. Keep your tongue forward, exactly like the vowel in see, while rounding your lips.
