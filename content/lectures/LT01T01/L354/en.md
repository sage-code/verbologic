## Sound of the letter Em

You call this letter (Em) /ɛm/. Its sound is the sound /m/, as in the word (Mutter) /ˈmʊtɐ/, which means mother. This letter makes only one sound in German.

## How to say the sound

Press your lips gently together and breathe out through your nose. Turn on your voice so your vocal cords buzz. You can put your fingers on your throat to feel the strong vibration.

## Spelling and variants

This letter always makes the sound /m/. You hear it at the start, middle, or end of a word. A single letter or a double letter makes this exact sound, as in (Sommer) /ˈzɔmɐ/.

## Mistakes to avoid

This sound is identical to the sound in the English word man. There is no special trap or pitfall to avoid.
