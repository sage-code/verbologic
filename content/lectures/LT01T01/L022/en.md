## Letter sound

This letter is called /ʃ/. It makes the sound /ʃ/, as in the word (școală) /ʃoˈsapte/, which means six. This letter has only one sound in Romanian.

## Mouth shape

Push your lips slightly forward. Raise the front of your tongue toward the roof of your mouth. Blow air gently over your tongue. Your vocal cords stay still. Hold your hand on your neck to feel that it does not vibrate.

## Spelling rules

This letter always makes the sound /ʃ/ everywhere it appears. At the beginning of a word, hear it in (șapte) /ˈʃap.te/. At the end of a word, hear it in (oraș) /oˈraʃ/.

## Common mistakes

Do not confuse this letter with a regular s. It sounds like the English letters sh in the word ship, never like the sound in sun. Keep your lips pushed forward and make a steady rushing sound.
