## Letter sound dáblio

The letter (dáblio) /ˈdabliu/ sounds like the sound /w/ in (web) /ˈwɛb/, meaning web. This letter only appears in foreign loanwords. It has two sounds in European Portuguese.

## Mouth movements

To make the first sound, purse your lips into a tight circle. Raise the back of your tongue and let your voice hum. For the second sound, touch your upper teeth to your lower lip. Blow air gently through the gap while your voice hums. You can feel your throat vibrate for both sounds.

## Spelling rules

In borrowed words from English, the letter makes the sound /w/, as in (whisky) /ˈwiʃki/. In borrowed words from German or older traditional loanwords, the letter makes the sound /v/, as in (watt) /ˈvat/.

## Common mistakes

Do not swap the two sounds in borrowed words. In English words, shape your lips into a circle instead of touching your teeth, like in water. In German words, place your top front teeth against your lower lip, like in vine. Check the origin of the word to choose the right sound.
