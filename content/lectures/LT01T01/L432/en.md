## The letter be

The name of this letter is (be) /be/. Its main sound is heard in the word (bueno) /ˈbwe.no/, which means good. This letter makes two different sounds in Spanish.

## Pronouncing the sounds

For the first sound, close both lips firmly to stop the air. Then open them with voice. For the second sound, bring both lips close together without touching. Let the voice flow through the narrow gap. Put a hand on your throat to feel the voice buzz for both sounds.

## Reading the variants

After a pause or after the letters m or n, you say the sound /b/, as in (también) /tamˈbjen/. Between vowels and after most other consonants, you say the softer sound /β/. You hear this softer sound in (lobo) /ˈlo.βo/ and in (árbol) /ˈaɾ.βol/.

## Avoiding common mistakes

Do not press your lips together when the sound is between vowels. That sounds like the b in baby. Do not touch your upper teeth with your lower lip either, like the v in vine. Keep only your lips close together, and let the air pass gently.
