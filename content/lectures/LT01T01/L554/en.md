## Sound of letter B

The letter (B) /biː/ makes the sound /b/. You hear it in the word (bed) /bɛd/, which means bed. This letter has one main spoken sound, but sometimes it makes no sound at all.

## How to say it

Close both lips firmly to stop the air. Release your lips quickly and use your voice. You can feel your throat vibrate when you touch your neck with two fingers.

## Reading and spelling rules

In most words, this letter makes the sound /b/. For example, you hear it at the end of (cab) /kæb/. The letter is silent when it sits right before the letter t, as in (doubt) /daʊt/. It is also silent after the letter m in the same syllable, as in (climb) /klaɪm/.

## Common mistakes to avoid

Do not confuse the voiced sound /b/ with the quiet sound /p/. For the sound /b/, always turn on your voice. If your throat does not buzz, you are making the wrong sound.
