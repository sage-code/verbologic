## Sound of letter ge

The name of this letter is (ge) /xe/. Its main sound is the sound /ɡ/, heard in (gato) /ˈɡa.to/, which means cat. This letter makes three different sounds in Spanish.

## How to say ge

For the first sound, press the back of your tongue against the soft roof. Block the air briefly, then release it while your voice buzzes. For the second sound, lift the back of your tongue near the soft roof. Let the air squeeze through smoothly without stopping, keeping your vocal cords vibrating. For the third sound, raise your back tongue high and blow rough air without voice. Touch your throat to feel the buzz stop.

## Spelling of letter ge

After a pause or after a nasal letter before a, o, or u, you hear the hard sound /ɡ/, as in (tengo) /ˈteŋ.ɡo/. Between vowels or after any other letter before a, o, or u, it softens to the sound /ɣ/, like in (amigo) /aˈmi.ɣo/. Before the vowels e or i, it makes the raspy sound /x/, as heard in (gente) /ˈxen.te/.

## Mistakes with ge

Do not say the sound /x/ softly like the letter h in hat. Tighten the back of your throat so the air scrapes loudly. Avoid stopping the air completely when saying the sound /ɣ/ between vowels, as in the English word dagger. Keep the airflow open so it never clicks shut.
