## The letter elly

The letter (elly) /ɛj/ stands for the sound /j/. You hear it in the word (lyuk) /ˈjuk/, which means hole. This letter always represents one single sound.

## Pronouncing the letter

Raise the middle of your tongue toward the roof of your mouth. Keep the tip down behind your lower front teeth. Let the air flow smoothly over your tongue with your voice on. You can feel your throat vibrate.

## Reading the letter

In modern Hungarian, this letter always makes the sound /j/. You see it in words like (gólya) /ˈɡoːjɒ/.

## Common mistakes

Do not pronounce an l sound when you see this letter. Say it exactly like the first sound in the English word yellow. Never make a rolling or sliding sound.
