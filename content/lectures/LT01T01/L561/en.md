## Sound of the letter

The letter (I) /aɪ/ makes several different vowel sounds. Its main sound is the short vowel /ɪ/, as in the word (ink) /ɪŋk/. In English, this letter has four common sounds.

## How to say it

For the first sound, open your mouth slightly and keep your tongue relaxed and high. For the second sound, open wide, then glide your tongue up toward your roof. For the third sound, smile widely and push your tongue forward and high. For the last sound, relax your whole mouth completely and make a quick, weak murmur. Put a hand on your throat to feel your voice buzz for all four.

## Reading rules

In a closed syllable with consonants after it, this letter makes the sound /ɪ/, as in (pin) /pɪn/. In an open syllable or before a silent e, you say the sound /aɪ/, as in (ice) /aɪs/ or (time) /taɪm/. In borrowed words or special endings, it makes the long sound /iː/, as in (ski) /skiː/ or (police) /pə.liːs/. In weak, unstressed syllables, it turns into the relaxed sound /ə/, as in (pencil) /pen.səl/ or (possible) /pɒs.ə.bəl/.

## Common mistakes

Do not confuse the short sound /ɪ/ with the long sound in beat. Keep your mouth relaxed and short, not smiling and tense. Also, do not turn the quick, weak sound /ə/ into a clear vowel. Keep unstressed syllables short and soft.
