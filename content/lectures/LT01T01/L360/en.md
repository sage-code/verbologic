## The letter Es

The letter (Es) /ɛs/ makes three different sounds. Its main sound is the sound /z/, heard in the word (Sonne) /ˈzɔnə/, which means sun.

## Making the sounds

For the first sound, place your tongue tip near your upper teeth. Push air through and turn your voice on. For the next sound, keep the exact same tongue position, but switch your voice off. Feel your throat with your hand to check the buzz. For the third sound, push your lips forward and blow gently without voice.

## Reading the letter

Before a vowel at the start of a syllable, the letter makes the sound /z/, like in (Rose) /ˈʁoːzə/. At the end of a syllable or before a voiceless consonant, it sounds like the sound /s/, like in (Haus) /haʊ̯s/ or (Maske) /ˈmaskə/. Before the consonants p or t at the start of a syllable, it makes the sound /ʃ/, like in (Sport) /ʃpɔʁt/ or (Stadt) /ʃtat/.

## Common mistakes

Do not pronounce the letter at the start of words like the hissing sound in see. Use a voiced buzz like in zoo instead. When this letter stands before p or t at the beginning of words, remember to make the quiet sound in shoe.
