## The letter ell

The letter (ell) /ɛl/ makes the sound /l/. You hear it in the word (ló) /ˈloː/, which means horse. This letter has only one sound in Hungarian.

## Pronouncing ell

Touch the ridge behind your upper front teeth with the tip of your tongue. Let the air flow smoothly around both sides of your tongue. Vibrate your vocal cords as the air leaves your mouth. Put a hand on your throat to feel the gentle vibration.

## Spelling and variants

This letter always produces the sound /l/ in all positions. You can hear it clearly in the word (toll) /ˈtolː/.

## Avoiding mistakes

At the end of words, do not swallow this sound or lift the back of your tongue. Keep your tongue flat and forward, exactly like the first sound in the English word leaf. Never make the hollow sound heard at the end of the English word ball.
