## The letter enne

The letter (enne) /ɛn/ makes the sound /n/. You hear it in the word (nez) /ne/, which means nose. This letter has one main spoken sound. In certain combinations, it can also stay completely silent.

## Pronouncing the letter

Press the tip of your tongue against the gum ridge behind your top teeth. Keep your lips relaxed and parted. Send the air out smoothly through your nose while your vocal cords vibrate. Place two fingers on your throat to feel the steady buzzing.

## Reading the letter

This letter makes the sound /n/ before a vowel, as in (minute) /mi.nyt/. It stays completely silent at the end of words or after the letter m, as in (condamner) /kɔ̃.da.ne/ and (monsieur) /mə.sjø/.

## Avoiding common mistakes

The French sound is lighter and crisper than in English. Do not pull your tongue tip back or press too heavily like in net. Keep your tongue relaxed against the front gum ridge, and let the air flow smoothly.
