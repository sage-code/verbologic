## Sound of letter Qu

The name of this letter sounds like (Qu) /kuː/. Its main sound is heard in (Quelle) /ˈkvɛlə/, which means source or spring. This letter makes two sounds in German.

## Pronouncing the sounds

For the first sound, press the back of your tongue against the roof of your mouth. Release the air sharply, then touch your lower lip to your upper teeth and turn your voice on. For the second sound, make only the crisp release of air without moving to the lip. Put your hand on your throat to feel it vibrate only during the voiced part.

## Spelling patterns

In native German words, you see this letter before the letter u, where it makes the sound /kv/ as in (Bequem) /bəˈkveːm/. In French loanwords, it makes the sound /k/ as in (Quiche) /kiːʃ/.

## Common learner errors

You might want to pronounce it like quick in English with both lips forming a circle. Do not use that sound. Use your upper front teeth and your lower lip instead, like the first sound in very.
