## Letter sound

The letter (Q) /kjuː/ usually represents a single basic sound. It makes the sound /k/ as in the word (qat) /kæt/, which means a khat plant. On its own, this letter has one main sound.

## Mouth movements

Raise the back of your tongue to touch the roof of your mouth. Stop the air completely. Release the air suddenly without using your voice. Place a hand on your throat to feel that your vocal cords do not vibrate.

## Spelling rules

On its own without the letter u in loanwords, it makes the sound /k/. An example is the word (qi) /tʃiː/.

## Common mistakes

Speakers sometimes add an extra sound when this letter stands alone. Do not add a w sound after the burst of air. Keep the sound /k/ crisp and short, just like the sound of the letter k in the common English word kite.
