## Sound of S

The letter (S) /s/ is named (S) /s/. Its main sound is the sound /s/, as in the word (sun) /sʌn/, which means sun. This letter has four sounds in English words.

## Pronouncing S

To make the first sound, place your tongue tip near your upper gum. Push air out smoothly without using your voice. For the second sound, keep the same mouth position and add your voice. For the third sound, pull your tongue back slightly and voice the air. Put a hand on your throat to feel the buzzing vibration during voiced sounds.

## Spelling of S

The letter makes the sound /s/ at word starts, like in (sit) /sɪt/, or after quiet sounds. It makes the sound /z/ between vowels, as in (rose) /roʊz/. It makes the sound /ʒ/ before endings like ion or ure, as in (vision) /vɪ.ʒən/. Finally, the letter is not pronounced in some special words, like in (island) /aɪ.lənd/.

## Common Mistakes

Do not turn the sound /s/ into the sound /z/ too early. Many learners forget to add voice to plurals or between vowels. Touch your throat to check for vibration. When saying words like (dogs) /dɒɡz/, feel your vocal cords buzz at the end.
