## Sound of letter u

The letter (u) /y/ is named (u) /y/. Its main sound is heard in (usine) /y.zin/, which means factory. This letter makes two distinct sounds in spoken French.

## How to say u

For the first sound, place your tongue forward against your bottom teeth. Push your lips forward into a tight small circle and vibrate your voice. Touch your throat to feel the steady vibration. For the second sound, make this same shape and quickly glide into the next vowel.

## Spelling of u

In most words, this vowel gives the full sound /y/, as in (sûr) /syʁ/. When it stands directly before another spoken vowel, it becomes a brief glide /ɥ/. You hear this gliding sound in (huit) /ɥit/ and (nuit) /nɥi/.

## Mistakes to avoid

Do not replace this sound with the vowel of boot. Keep your tongue pushed forward, like saying beet, but push your lips into a tight circle. Do not add a glide sound like you hear in music.
