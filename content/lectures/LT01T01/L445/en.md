## The letter eñe

The letter (eñe) /ˈeɲe/ represents the sound /ɲ/. You can hear it in the word (niño) /ˈni.ɲo/, which means boy, child. This letter has only one sound in Spanish.

## Mouth movements

Press the broad middle of your tongue against the roof of your mouth. Keep the tip of your tongue down behind your bottom front teeth. Let the air pass out through your nose while your vocal cords vibrate. Place two fingers on the bridge of your nose to feel the vibration.

## Reading the letter

The letter (eñe) /ˈeɲe/ always makes the sound /ɲ/ in every position. You see this sound inside the word (año) /ˈa.ɲo/. You also hear this exact sound in the word (España) /esˈpa.ɲa/.

## Common mistakes

Do not separate this sound into two parts like the letters in onion or canyon. Press the flat part of your tongue flat in one single smooth movement. Do not touch your upper teeth with the tip of your tongue.
