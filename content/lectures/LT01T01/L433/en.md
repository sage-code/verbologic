## The letter ce

The name of this letter is (ce) /θe/. Its main sound is heard in the word (casa) /ˈka.sa/, which means house. This letter produces two different sounds in spoken Spanish.

## How to say it

For the first sound, lift the back of your tongue against the roof of your mouth. Release the air sharply without voice. For the second sound, put the tip of your tongue between your teeth. Blow air through the narrow opening softly. Hold your hand on your throat to check that your vocal cords stay still for both sounds.

## Reading and spelling

This letter makes the sound /k/ before the vowels a, o and u, as in the word (clase) /ˈkla.se/. It also makes the sound /k/ when placed directly before another consonant. This letter makes the sound /θ/ before the vowels e and i, as in the word (cena) /ˈθe.na/ and the word (cielo) /ˈθje.lo/.

## Mistakes to avoid

Do not release a puff of breath when saying the sound /k/. The English word cat has a burst of air, so keep your breath back. Do not pronounce the sound /θ/ like an s sound, as in the word sun. Push your tongue gently between your front teeth instead.
