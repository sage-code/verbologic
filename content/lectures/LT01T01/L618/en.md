## Sound of ch

The letters ch are named /k/. Their main sound is the sound /tʃ/, heard in the word (chin) /tʃɪn/, which means the bottom part of your face. These letters make three different sounds in English.

## How to say ch

For the first sound, press your tongue tip behind your upper teeth. Stop the breath, then release it with a quick puff. For the second sound, lift the back of your tongue against the roof of your mouth and release a clean burst of air. For the third sound, round your lips slightly and blow air steadily over your tongue. Your throat does not buzz for any of them. Put a hand on your throat to check.

## Spelling of ch

In native English words, these letters usually sound like the sound /tʃ/, as in (rich) /rɪtʃ/. In words borrowed from Greek, they make the sound /k/, as in (echo) /ɛk.oʊ/ or (ache) /eɪk/. In words borrowed from French, they make the sound /ʃ/, as in (chef) /ʃɛf/ and (chic) /ʃiːk/.

## Common mistakes with ch

Do not let your throat buzz when you make these sounds. A common error is blending the sounds into a smooth glide instead of stopping the air. Make sure you stop the air completely before releasing the puff.
