## Letter ef

The name of this letter sounds like (эф) /ɛf/. Its main sound appears in the word (фонарь) /fɐˈnarʲ/, which means lantern, streetlight. In Russian, this letter represents two distinct sounds.

## Making the sounds

For the first sound, place your upper teeth lightly against your lower lip. Blow air steadily through the gap without using your vocal cords. For the second sound, make the same contact and raise the middle of your tongue toward the roof of your mouth. Place a hand on your throat to feel that it does not buzz.

## Reading the variants

The letter makes the sound /f/, that is a hard f, before hard vowels, consonants, and word-finally, as in (факт) /fakt/. It makes the sound /fʲ/, that is a soft f, before softening vowels and the soft sign, as in (фильм) /fʲilʲm/ or (кофе) /ˈkofʲe/.

## Common mistakes

You may pronounce the soft sound like the ordinary f in fast. Prepare the y sound with your tongue at the same time to soften it. Do not let your vocal cords vibrate, or you will produce the sound of vine instead of fine.
