## Sound of ú

The letter (ú) /uː/ is called (ú) /uː/. It makes the sound /uː/, as heard in (út) /ˈuːt/, which means road, path. This letter makes only one sound in all words.

## How to say ú

Push your tongue back toward the roof of your mouth. Round your lips tightly into a small circle. Push your lips slightly forward. Make a steady voice sound without moving your tongue or your lips. Keep your hand on your throat to feel the strong vibration.

## Spelling of ú

The accent mark over the letter shows that the sound is always held long. It sounds as /uː/ in every position, as in (húsz) /ˈhuːs/.

## Common mistakes with ú

Do not let your lips glide toward a w sound as in the English word boot. Keep your lips firmly fixed in place from start to finish. Do not shorten this sound to the quick vowel of book. Hold the vowel long and steady without changing your mouth shape.
