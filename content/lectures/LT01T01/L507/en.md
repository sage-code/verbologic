## The letter o

The letter (o) /o/ is called (o) /o/. It makes the sound /o/, like in the word (okos) /ˈokoʃ/, which means smart. This letter has only one sound.

## How to say it

Pull your tongue back and keep it mid height. Round your lips into a circle. Push your lips slightly forward. Let your voice vibrate freely without moving your jaw. Touch your throat to feel the strong vibration of your vocal cords.

## When it appears

The letter (o) /o/ makes the sound /o/ in all standard positions. You hear it at the start of the word (orvos) /ˈorvoʃ/. Keep the vowel short and steady.

## Common mistakes

You might glide this vowel like in the English word go. Do not add a w sound at the end. Keep your lips still from start to finish. Make it a clean, short sound.
