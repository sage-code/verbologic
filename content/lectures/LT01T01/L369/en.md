## The letter Ö

The letter (Ö) /øː/ is called /øː/, that is a long closed vowel. Its main sound is the long vowel /øː/, that is a long closed vowel, as in (Öl) /ʔøːl/, which means oil. This letter makes two different sounds.

## How to say it

To make the first sound, say the sound in see. Keep your tongue high and forward, then round your lips tightly. To make the second sound, say the sound in bed. Keep your tongue in that spot, then round your lips into an open circle. Touch your throat to feel your voice buzz on both sounds.

## Spelling rules

This letter makes its long sound in open syllables or before a single consonant. An example is the word (schön) /ʃøːn/. It makes its short sound in closed syllables, especially before two consonants. An example is the word (öffnen) /ˈʔœfnən/.

## Common mistakes

Do not replace these sounds with the vowel in bird or burn. Do not flatten your lips or glide into an r sound. Shape your lips into a tight circle first, and keep them still until the sound ends.
