## Sound of the letter

The letter (L) /el/ makes the sound /l/ in the word (lamp) /læmp/, which means lamp. This letter makes one spoken sound, but sometimes stays completely quiet.

## How to say it

Press the tip of your tongue against the ridge behind your top teeth. Let the air flow smoothly around the sides of your tongue. Use your voice. Touch your throat to feel a strong buzz. When this sound comes after a vowel, raise the back of your tongue slightly toward the roof of your mouth.

## Spelling rules

This letter usually makes the sound /l/, like in the word (milk) /mɪlk/. It stays quiet before certain consonants in a few common words, like in the word (calm) /kɑːm/ and the word (salmon) /sæ.mən/.

## Common mistakes

Do not replace the sound at the end of a word with a vowel sound like in tool. Keep the tip of your tongue firmly against the roof of your mouth until the sound ends completely.
