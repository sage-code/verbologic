## The letter a

The letter (a) /a/ is called (a) /a/. Its main sound is the vowel /a/, heard in (agua) /ˈa.ɣwa/, which means water. This letter makes only one sound in standard Spanish.

## Pronouncing the letter

Open your mouth wide and keep your lips relaxed. Rest your tongue flat in the bottom of your mouth. Let your voice flow freely with no friction. Put a hand on your throat to feel the steady vibration of your vocal cords.

## Reading rules

The letter (a) /a/ always represents the sound /a/ in all standard positions. You hear this vowel in (casa) /ˈka.sa/ and in (madre) /ˈma.dɾe/.

## Common mistakes

Do not turn this vowel into the sound heard in English cat or father. Keep your mouth open wide and do not change the sound at the end. Never weaken this vowel to an unclear sound when it carries no stress.
