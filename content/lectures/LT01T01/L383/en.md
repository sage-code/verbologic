## Letter ka sound

The letter (ка) /ka/ makes its main sound in the word (кот) /kot/, which means cat. This letter produces three sounds in speech.

## How to say ka

Press the back of your tongue against the soft roof of your mouth. Release the air suddenly without any voice. For the softer version, raise the middle of your tongue at the same time. For the voiced sound, keep the same mouth position but vibrate your vocal cords. Place your fingers on your throat to feel the buzz.

## Reading the letter ka

Before consonants or hard vowels, pronounce the sound /k/, that is a hard k, as in (как) /kak/. This sound also appears at the end of words. Before soft vowels or a soft sign, pronounce the sound /kʲ/, that is a soft k, as in (кит) /kʲit/. Before certain voiced consonants, say the sound /ɡ/ as in (вокзал) /vaɡˈzal/.

## Mistakes to avoid with ka

You might release a puff of breath as in the English word kite. Keep the air burst much tighter. Do not add a separate y sound when saying the softer sound as in the English word cue. Raise the middle of your tongue smoothly instead.
