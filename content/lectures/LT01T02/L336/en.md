## Sound of cr

The letters cr make the sound /kr/. You hear this in the word (crab) /kræb/, which means crab. This letter pair makes only one sound.

## Mouth movements

Press the back of your tongue against the roof of your mouth. Release a quick puff of air without using your voice. Then pull your tongue tip back slightly into your mouth, curling it up without touching the roof. Turn your voice on for the second part. Feel your throat vibrate on the second sound.

## Spelling rules

The letters cr appear at the start of words before a vowel. They make the sound /kr/ in (cross) /krɒs/.

## Common mistakes

Do not tap or roll your tongue against your teeth. Keep your tongue pulled back inside your mouth so it touches nothing. Do not add an extra vowel sound between the two consonants.
