## Sound of bl

The letters bl sound as /bl/. Listen to this sound in (black) /blæk/, which means black. This letter pair makes only one sound combination in English.

## How to say bl

Close both lips firmly to stop the air. Release them gently while placing the tongue tip behind your upper front teeth. Let the air flow smoothly around the sides of your tongue. Turn on your voice for both parts. Feel your vocal cords vibrate in your throat.

## Spelling of bl

The letters bl always make the sound /bl/ at the beginning of a syllable. You hear this sound pattern in the word (blue) /blu/ and in (blind) /blaɪnd/.

## Common mistakes with bl

Do not insert a short vowel between the two letters. Move smoothly from your lips directly into the tongue position without a pause.
