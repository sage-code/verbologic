## Sound of oi

The letters oi usually make the sound /ɔɪ/. You hear this in the word (oil) /ɔɪl/, which means oil. This spelling has two different sounds in English.

## How to say oi

To make the first sound, round your lips and open your mouth. Then glide your lips into a smile and raise your tongue. To make the second sound, round your lips tight, then open your mouth wide. Rest your hand on your throat to feel your voice buzz throughout both sounds.

## Spelling of oi

In most English words, these letters make the sound /ɔɪ/. You hear this sound in (coin) /kɔɪn/. In words borrowed from French, the letters make the sound /wɑː/. You hear this sound in the word (memoir) /mɛm.wɑː/.

## Common mistakes with oi

Do not split the sound into two separate vowels. Glide smoothly from the open shape to the smile. In borrowed words, do not say the usual vowel sound. Remember to shape your lips like a w at the start.
