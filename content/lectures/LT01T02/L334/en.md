## The sound br

The letters br make the blended sound /br/. You hear this sound in the word (brown) /braʊn/, which means brown. These two letters always make this single combined sound.

## Mouth movements

Press your lips together to stop the air. Release them with your voice. At the same time, pull the tip of your tongue slightly back and curl it up. Touch a hand to your throat to feel the strong buzzing.

## Reading the letters

The letters br appear everywhere as the sound /br/. You see this sound at the start of the word (brick) /brɪk/.

## Common mistakes

Do not roll your tongue against your teeth. Keep your tongue tip back so the sound stays smooth. Do not add a vowel between the two letters.
