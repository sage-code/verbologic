## Sound of dr

The letters dr make the sound /dr/. You hear this sound in the word (drum) /drʌm/, which means drum. These two letters together have only one sound in English.

## Making the sound

Press the tip of your tongue behind your top front teeth. Release the air while pulling your tongue back into your mouth. Slightly round your lips as you do this. Your vocal cords vibrate for the whole sound. Place a hand on your throat to feel the steady vibration.

## Spelling patterns

The letters dr always make the sound /dr/. You find this cluster at the beginning of words like (drop) /drɒp/ and (dress) /drɛs/.

## Common mistakes

Do not add a vowel between the two letters. Blend the two sounds smoothly together. Do not roll your tongue tip. Pull your tongue back without tapping the roof of your mouth.
