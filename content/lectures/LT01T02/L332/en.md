## Sounds of ur

The letters ur are called /ɜːr/. Their main sound is the sound /ɜːr/, as in (fur) /fɜːr/, which means fur. These letters have three sounds.

## How to say ur

For the first sound, keep your tongue relaxed in the middle of your mouth. Round your lips slightly and use your voice. For the second sound, make a very short, weak vowel. Relax your jaw, lips, and tongue completely. For the third sound, start with your tongue high, then pull it back and round your lips. Feel your vocal cords vibrate on your throat.

## Spelling rules for ur

In a stressed syllable before a consonant or at the end of a word, ur makes the sound /ɜːr/, as in (burn) /bɜːrn/. In an unstressed syllable, ur makes the weak sound /ər/, as in (sulphur) /sʌl.fər/. When silent final e follows, ur makes the sound /jʊər/, as in (cure) /kjʊər/.

## Common mistakes

Do not roll or tap your tongue against your teeth. Keep your tongue back in your mouth. Do not open your mouth too wide for the sound /ɜːr/. Keep your jaw steady and your lips slightly relaxed. In an unstressed syllable, do not put heavy stress on the vowel. Keep the sound short and very light.
