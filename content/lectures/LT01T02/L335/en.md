## Sound of cl

The consonant cluster cl makes the sound /kl/. Hear it in the word (clap) /klæp/, which means clap. This letter group makes one combined sound.

## Mouth movement

Press the back of your tongue against the roof of your mouth. Release a burst of air without using your voice. Then quickly place the tip of your tongue behind your top front teeth. Let voiced air flow around the sides of your tongue. Feel your vocal cords vibrate on that second step.

## Spelling rules

The spelling cl represents the sound /kl/ in all positions. You hear it at the start of words like (club) /klʌb/.

## Common mistakes

Do not insert a short vowel sound between the two consonants. Move your tongue smoothly from the back to the front without stopping.
