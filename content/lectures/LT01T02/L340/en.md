## The sound st

This letter group sounds like /st/. You hear it in the word (stop) /stɒp/, which means to cease moving. The group has two different sounds.

## How to say it

First, hiss air softly over your tongue tip near your upper teeth. Next, tap your tongue against the roof of your mouth. For the other sound, just hiss the air softly without tapping your tongue. Hold a hand to your throat to feel that your voice stays quiet.

## Spelling rules

Most of the time, this pair sounds like /st/. You hear this at the end of (fast) /fɑːst/. In some words before letters like l e, the second letter stays silent. Then the group sounds like /s/, as in (castle) /kɑː.səl/. It also sounds like /s/ before e n, as in (listen) /lɪs.ən/.

## Common mistakes

Do not add an extra puff of air after the second letter. Also, do not pronounce the second letter when it should be quiet. There is no other common pitfall.
