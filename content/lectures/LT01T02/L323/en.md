## Sound of ow

The letters ow make two different sounds. Their main sound is the sound /aʊ/, as in the word (owl) /aʊl/. An owl is a night bird. Listen carefully to each word to learn which sound fits.

## How to say it

For the first sound, open your mouth wide. Then pull your tongue up and round your lips into a small circle. For the second sound, start with your mouth half open. Move your lips forward into a small circle as you finish. Put a hand on your throat to feel the strong vibration.

## Spelling rules

The letters ow most often make the sound /aʊ/ before consonants or at the end of words, as in (cow) /kaʊ/. They make the sound /oʊ/ as an alternative at the end of words, as in (snow) /snoʊ/. They also make the sound /oʊ/ before certain consonants, as in (bowl) /boʊl/.

## Common mistakes

Do not shorten the sounds into a single flat vowel. Always glide smoothly toward a small round lip shape. Do not mix up the two sounds in words with similar letters. Practice the change of lip shape slowly.
