## Sounds of oa

The letters oa sound like /oʊ/. You hear this in the word (oak) /oʊk/, a large forest tree. This pair makes two different sounds in English words.

## Mouth movements

For the first sound, open your mouth slightly and push your lips forward into a circle. Then glide into a smaller circle. For the second sound, pull your tongue back, drop your jaw, and make an open round shape. Hold the vowel steady. Touch your throat to feel your voice buzz throughout both sounds.

## Spelling rules

In most words, these letters make the sound /oʊ/. You hear it inside (boat) /boʊt/ and (road) /roʊd/. When the letter r follows right after, they make the sound /ɔː/. You hear this pattern inside (roar) /rɔːr/ and (board) /bɔːrd/.

## Common mistakes

Do not freeze your mouth on one flat vowel for the main sound. Glide smoothly from an open vowel to a small circle. Before r, do not say two separate vowel sounds. Blend them into one long, open sound.
