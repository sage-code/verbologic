## Sound of oy

The letters oy sound like /ɔɪ/. You hear this sound in the word (boy) /bɔɪ/, which means a young male person. These letters have only one sound in English.

## How to say oy

Start with your lips open and round. Your tongue sits low and pulled back. Then spread your lips wide into a smile. Your tongue glides up toward the front of your mouth. Keep your vocal cords vibrating all the time. Place two fingers on your throat to feel the steady buzz.

## Spelling of oy

The letters oy always make the sound /ɔɪ/. You find them at the end of words or before a vowel. A clear example is the word (toy) /tɔɪ/. They keep this exact pronunciation in every word.

## Comparing oy

Do not turn this into two separate sounds. Smooth the glide from open lips to a smile in one breath. Do not drop the final part, or it sounds like the vowel in law. Keep your voice smooth all the way through.
