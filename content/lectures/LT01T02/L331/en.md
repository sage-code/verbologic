## The letters or

The letters or make the main sound /ɔːr/, as in (fork) /fɔːrk/. This word means a tool with points for eating food. These letters make three different sounds.

## Making the sounds

For the first sound, pull your tongue back and make your lips into an open circle. For the second sound, relax your tongue and lips completely in the middle of your mouth. For the third sound, hold your tongue flat and slightly curled while keeping your lips relaxed. Feel your voice box shake in your neck for all three sounds.

## Spelling patterns

In stressed parts of a word, these letters make the sound /ɔːr/, like in (corn) /kɔːrn/. In unstressed parts of a word, they make the weak sound /ər/, like in (doctor) /dɑːk.tər/. After the letter w, they make the sound /ɜːr/, like in (word) /wɜːrd/.

## Common mistakes

Do not drop the curling tongue motion at the end. In a word like (storm) /stɔːrm/, do not make your lips into a tight circle like in boot. Keep the mouth wider and let the air flow steadily.
