## The letters au

The letters au sound like /ɔː/. You hear this main sound in (author) /ɔː.θər/, a person who writes books. Together, these two letters make three different sounds.

## Making the sounds

For the first sound, round your lips into an open circle. Pull your tongue back low, and let your voice hum. For the second sound, drop your jaw open wide without rounding your lips. For the third sound, keep your jaw open and round your lips just a little. Feel your throat vibrate with each vowel sound.

## Reading the letters

The letters au usually make the long sound /ɔː/ before consonants, as in (pause) /pɔːz/. In a few words from other languages, they sound like /ɑː/, as in (aunt) /ɑːnt/. They also make the short sound /ɒ/ in common words like (sausage) /sɒ.sɪdʒ/.

## Common mistakes

Do not pronounce both letters one after the other. Do not say the letters like two separate vowels. Always blend them into one steady sound. Keep your tongue steady until you finish speaking the vowel.
