## Sounds of er

The letters er make the sound /ɜːr/, as in the word (fern) /fɜːrn/. A fern is a green plant with feathery leaves. This letter combination makes two different sounds in English words.

## How to say er

For the first sound, keep your mouth slightly open. Pull your tongue back and curl the tip slightly up without touching anything. Make a long voice sound and feel your throat vibrate. For the second sound, relax your whole mouth completely. Make a very short, weak voice sound with your tongue resting in the middle.

## Spelling rules for er

In stressed syllables, the letters er sound like /ɜːr/, as in (term) /tɜːrm/. In unstressed syllables, usually at the end of a word, they sound like /ər/, as in (water) /wɔː.tər/ and (butter) /bʌ.tər/.

## Common mistakes

Do not roll your tongue against the roof of your mouth. Keep the tongue pulled back and still. In weak endings, do not make a strong vowel sound. Keep the ending very soft and quick.
