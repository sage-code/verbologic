## The sound fl

The letters fl sound like /fl/. You hear this sound at the start of (flag) /flæɡ/, which means a piece of cloth with a pattern. This letter combination makes one sound blend.

## How to say fl

Touch your lower lip gently with your top front teeth. Blow air through the gap without using your voice. Then place the tip of your tongue behind your top front teeth. Drop your tongue while turning on your voice. You can feel air on your hand first, then a vibration in your throat.

## Spelling rules

The letters fl appear in almost all positions. They always make the blended sound /fl/, as in the word (flat) /flæt/.

## Common mistakes

Do not insert an extra vowel sound between the two parts. Keep the flow smooth and quick. Do not stop the breath before moving your tongue. If you speak clearly, the transition feels natural.
