## Sounds of ar

The letter group ar is spoken as the sound /ɑːr/. Its main sound is the sound /ɑː/, heard in (arm) /ɑːm/, which means arm. This letter group makes four different sounds in English words.

## How to say ar

Open your mouth wide and keep your tongue low and flat for the first sound. Drop your jaw fully, then pull your tongue tip slightly back for the second sound. Relax your mouth and tongue completely into the middle for the third sound. Round your lips firmly and pull your tongue back for the fourth sound. Feel your throat vibrate with your voice.

## Spelling rules for ar

In stressed syllables before a consonant, ar makes the sound /ɑː/, as in (car) /kɑː/. Before a vowel, ar makes the sound /ɑːr/, as in (starry) /stɑː.ri/, or in (far) /fɑːr/. In unstressed syllables, it makes the sound /ə/, as in (sugar) /ʃʊɡ.ə/, or in (dollar) /dɒl.ə/. After a w sound, it often makes the sound /ɔː/, as in (war) /wɔː/, or in (warm) /wɔːm/.

## Common mistakes with ar

Speakers often use the wrong vowel after a w sound. Do not say the open vowel of car in those words. Round your lips into the sound of talk instead. In unstressed syllables, do not give this group a full vowel. Keep it completely weak and relaxed.
