## Sound of letter i

The French letter (i) /i/ is called (i) /i/. It most often represents the clear vowel sound /i/, as heard in (ici) /i.si/.

## How to say i

For the vowel sound /i/, spread your lips wide into a smile. Push your tongue high and forward against your lower teeth. For the glide sound /j/, start from that high position and glide quickly into the following vowel.

## Spelling rules for i

By default, or under accents, this character yields the vowel sound /i/, as in (île) /il/. Standing directly before another vowel, it turns into the semi-vowel sound /j/, as in (ciel) /sjɛl/ or (hier) /jɛʁ/.

## Comparing i with English

English speakers often relax this into the short vowel heard in sit or turn it into a diphthong like in see. Keep the French vowel sound /i/ tense, short, and pure, with no gliding.
