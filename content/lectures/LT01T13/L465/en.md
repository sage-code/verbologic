## Sound of ache

The letter (ache) /aʃ/ is always silent in French. You hear only the following vowel sound, as in (habiter) /a.bi.te/.

## Pronunciation of ache

Do not exhale or restrict your throat. Relax your vocal cords completely and make no sound at all for this letter.

## Spelling of ache

In every position, this letter is completely silent. It produces no sound in (homme) /ɔm/, and it remains silent in (héros) /e.ʁo/.

## Pitfalls for ache

English speakers often breathe an audible sound like the start of house. Never blow air out when you see this letter.
