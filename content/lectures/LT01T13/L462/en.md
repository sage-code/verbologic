## The letter e

The letter (e) /ə/ is called (e) /ə/. Its basic sound is heard in the word (menu) /mə.ny/.

## Pronouncing the letter

For the central sound, relax your tongue in the middle and round your lips slightly. For the closed sound, spread your lips and raise your tongue high. For the open sound, drop your jaw wider while keeping your lips unrounded.

## Reading the letter

In short monosyllables, it makes the sound /ə/ as in (le) /lə/. Written with an acute accent or before certain silent final consonants, it sounds like the sound /e/ in (été) /e.te/. With a grave or circumflex accent, it gives the sound /ɛ/ in (père) /pɛʁ/. Finally, it is silent at the end of words like (table) /tabl/.

## Common pitfalls

Do not replace the sound /e/ with the English diphthong in say or day. Keep the vowel pure and steady until the end, without gliding toward an ee sound.
