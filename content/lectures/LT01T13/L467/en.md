## The letter ji

The letter (ji) /ʒi/ makes the sound /ʒ/, heard in the word (jupe) /ʒyp/.

## Pronouncing the letter

Round your lips slightly and push them forward. Place the tip of your tongue near the roof of your mouth, just behind your upper teeth. Push air through while vibrating your vocal cords.

## Reading the letter

In almost all words, this character spells the sound /ʒ/, as heard in (joli) /ʒɔ.li/.

## Avoiding mistakes

Do not add an initial d sound before the consonant like in the English word judge. It is smooth and continuous, identical to the s sound heard in the English word measure.
