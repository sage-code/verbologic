## The sound
Listen to short words like (în) /ɨn/ or (la) /la/.
These tiny words sound very light.
Your voice does not punch them.
Instead, you save your breath for the next word.
Together, they sound like one single word.
One part stays quiet, and the other part rings out loud.

## How to say it
Keep your voice soft on the first word.
Do not stop your airflow between the words.
Say (în casă) /ɨn ˈka.sə/, which means at home.
Glide right from the first sound into the second sound.
Your mouth stays relaxed at first.
Then, you push more air on the main syllable.

## Reading rules
When you read short words, keep moving forward.
Do not pause after words like (la) /la/.
Read them as a quiet step before the main beat.
In the phrase (în casă) /ɨn ˈka.sə/, stress only the first part of the last word.
The rhythm stays smooth and natural.

## Common mistakes
Do not hit every word with equal force.
If you say (în) /ɨn/ too loudly, the rhythm breaks.
Do not pause between the small word and the main word.
Keep the breath flowing without any stop.
Connect them like syllables inside one single word.
