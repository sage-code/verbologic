## The sound
The standard word is (mulțumesc) /mult͡suˈmesk/. It means thank you. In everyday speech, people often shorten this word. You may hear the casual form (mulțam) /mulˈt͡sam/. Fast speech softens the sounds. The strong beat stays on the final syllable in both words.

## How to say it
Start with your lips relaxed. Touch your tongue to your upper teeth for the first sound. Then make a crisp sound like 'ts'. Push the stress to the end of the word. When you speak fast, do not stop between sounds. Keep the flow smooth and quick. Let your voice drop slightly at the end.

## Reading rules
The letters spell out every part of (mulțumesc) /mult͡suˈmesk/. The letter with a comma below makes a sharp 'ts' sound. The final group of consonants stays crisp in slow speech. In rapid conversation, speakers drop the middle sounds slightly. You can choose the full form or the short form (mulțam) /mulˈt͡sam/.

## Common mistakes
Do not stress the first syllable. Putting the beat too early sounds unnatural. Do not pause before the final consonants. Connect all vowels smoothly without adding extra breath. Also, do not force every single sound in casual talk. Relax your tongue so the word sounds natural and friendly.
