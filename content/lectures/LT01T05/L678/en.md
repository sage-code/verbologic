## The sound
Listen to (hai) /haj/ and (să) /sə/.
Together with an action, they form an invitation.
Listen to (hai, să mergem) /haj sə ˈmer.dʒem/, meaning come on, let's go.
The first two words sound light.
The main beat lands on the end.

## How to say it
Keep your jaw relaxed for the start.
Glide quickly through (hai) /haj/.
Make (să) /sə/ very brief with a relaxed tongue.
Do not pause between these words.
Push your voice forward to the verb.
The tone stays quick and friendly.

## Reading rules
The letters in (hai) /haj/ sound like 'eye'.
The letter ă in (să) /sə/ sounds like 'a' in 'about'.
In print, you often see a comma after (hai) /haj/.
In speech, you do not stop at this comma.
Read the phrase as one smooth line.

## Common mistakes
Many learners stress every single word equally.
Do not make (hai) /haj/ loud or long.
Do not drag out (să) /sə/.
If you pause after each word, you sound stiff.
Keep the start soft and fast.
