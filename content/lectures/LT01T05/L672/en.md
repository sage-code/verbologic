## The sound
In fast speech, two words can join together. You hear one single syllable. The words (ce) /tʃe/ and (ai) /aj/ blend into (ce-ai) /ˈtʃaj/. It sounds like the start of 'chair' joined with 'eye'. The voice glides smoothly across the sounds. You do not stop between them.

## How to say it
Start with your tongue behind your top front teeth. Release the air quickly to make the first sound. Then, open your mouth wide. Quickly glide your tongue up and forward. Keep your voice strong and steady on the main vowel sound. Say the whole unit in one smooth breath.

## Reading rules
Look for a hyphen between the two parts. The written mark shows that two words become one sound. When you see (ce-ai) /ˈtʃaj/, read it without any break. The hyphen tells you to drop the first vowel sound. You blend the remaining consonant directly into the next word.

## Common mistakes
Do not pause between the two parts. Some people try to say each part slowly. That creates two separate beats and breaks the rhythm. Do not add an extra sound in the middle. Glide smoothly from start to finish. Keep the pitch natural and relaxed.
