## The sound
Two small words often melt together in fast speech. You drop a vowel sound. The word (nu) /nu/ joins the word (a) /a/. They become one quick sound. Listen to (n-a venit) /na veˈnit/, which means he did not come. The two words fuse cleanly into one.

## How to say it
Touch the roof of your mouth with your tongue tip for the first sound. Drop your jaw quickly into an open central vowel. Do not pause between the two parts. Speak it like the start of the English word 'nap'. Keep your voice flowing smoothly into the next word.

## Reading rules
You write this short form with a hyphen. The dash shows that a letter is gone. When you see this dash, do not stop your voice. Read both parts together as a single syllable. The stress usually moves forward onto the action word that follows it.

## Common mistakes
Many learners pause at the hyphen. Do not stop your breath there. Another mistake is saying the full sound (nu) /nu/ by accident. Always blend the letters into one quick breath. Keep the sound light and move quickly to the main word.
