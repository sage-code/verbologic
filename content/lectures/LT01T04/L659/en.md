## The Sound of the Letter H
In Romanian, the letter H is never silent. It always makes a distinct sound when you read it. English speakers already know this friendly sound very well. It sounds just like the gentle puff of air in the English word house. In Romanian words, this letter sounds light and clear. You should never drop it or skip it when you read aloud. Every single written H must be heard in speech.

## How to Shape Your Mouth
Open your mouth naturally as if you are going to say a vowel. Keep your tongue relaxed and flat in the bottom of your mouth. Gently push warm air out from your throat without vibrating your vocal cords. It should feel like you are softly fogging up a cold mirror with your breath. The pitch stays steady and even during the breath. You then move your mouth smoothly right into the next vowel sound.

## Word Example and Practice
A wonderful word to practice this clear breathing sound is **hotel** /hoˈtel/. Notice that the stress lands strongly on the second syllable of the word. You start with that gentle puff of air on the first syllable. English speakers sometimes forget to pronounce the H when they see international words. In Romanian, always breathe that first sound out clearly. Keep your voice light and do not make a harsh scraping sound in your throat.
