## The sound
The letter h makes an open breath sound.
It sounds like the h in 'hat' or 'hotel'.
Listen to the word (hotel) /hoˈtel/, which means hotel.
You also hear it in (hartă) /ˈhar.tə/, which means map.
The sound is always clear and light.
Air flows freely through your throat.

## How to say it
Open your mouth slightly.
Keep your tongue relaxed and low.
Do not touch your teeth or palate.
Breathe out gently from your throat.
Do not use your vocal cords.
You only make the sound with passing air.
Practice saying (hotel) /hoˈtel/ smoothly.
Push the breath out right before the vowel.

## Reading rules
You always pronounce this letter in Romanian.
It is never silent.
You see it at the start of words like (hotel) /hoˈtel/.
You also see it in words like (haină) /ˈhaj.nə/, which means coat.
Say it every time you see it written.
Make the breath sound before you say the next vowel.

## Common mistakes
Do not drop this sound.
Never leave the letter h silent like in 'hour'.
If you say (hotel) /hoˈtel/ without the h, people notice.
Also, do not scrape your throat too hard.
Keep the air soft and smooth.
Just exhale quietly and move directly into the vowel.
