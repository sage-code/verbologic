## The sound
Look at the letters ș and ț.
They have a small comma below.
The letter ș sounds like 'sh' in 'shoe'.
Hear it in (școală) /ʃkoˈa.lə/, which means school.
The letter ț sounds like 'ts' in 'cats'.
Hear it in (țară) /ˈt͡sa.rə/, which means country.

## How to say it
Push your lips forward for ș.
Blow air softly over your tongue.
For ț, touch the roof of your mouth with your tongue tip.
Stop the air for a moment.
Then quickly release it with a sharp hiss.
Keep your voice quiet and smooth for both sounds.

## Reading rules
These two letters never change their sound.
You see ș, you always say 'sh'.
You see ț, you always say 'ts'.
Vowels after them do not alter their sound.
Even at the end of a word, keep the sound clear.
Do not confuse them with plain s or t.

## Common mistakes
Do not pronounce ș like a simple s.
Do not pronounce ț like a simple t.
Both letters need their full hiss.
Notice the little comma under the letter.
Without the comma, the letter makes a different sound.
Pay close attention to these marks when you read.
