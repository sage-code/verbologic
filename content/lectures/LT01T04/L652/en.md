## The sound
The letters c and h work as a team. Together, they make one clean, crisp sound. You hear this sound in the word (chiar) /kjar/, which means really. It sounds like the first sound in 'keep'. The sound is sharp and light. It never sounds like 'church'.

## How to say it
Lift the middle of your tongue toward the roof of your mouth. Press it flat and firm. Stop the air for a brief moment. Then, release the air quickly with a sharp puff. Keep your voice quiet and do not buzz your throat. Smile slightly to help your tongue find the right place.

## Reading rules
Look for the letter h right after the letter c. You only see this sound before the vowels e or i. Listen to the word (chem) /kem/, which means I call. Now listen to (chip) /kip/, which means face. The letter h stays silent. It only tells you to keep the c sound crisp and hard.

## Common mistakes
Do not make a rough sound in your throat. Do not make the sound from 'chat'. Many learners try to pronounce the letter h by itself. Never blow air for the letter h here. Treat the two letters as one single sound. Keep the sound forward in your mouth at all times.
