## The sound
The Romanian letter g can make a soft sound.
It sounds like the first sound in 'jump'.
You hear it in the word (gene) /ˈdʒe.ne/, which means eyelashes.
You also hear it in (gem) /dʒem/, which means jam.
It is smooth, voiced, and energetic.

## How to say it
Touch the roof of your mouth with your tongue tip.
Stop the air for a tiny moment.
Then, release the air gently with friction.
Turn on your voice box to make it buzz.
Push your lips forward just a little bit.
Keep your tongue relaxed after the release.

## Reading rules
Look at the letter right after g.
The letter g makes this soft sound before e and i.
See it in (gene) /ˈdʒe.ne/.
The vowel follows right after without a pause.
Before other letters, g sounds hard like in 'go'.
Always check the next letter first.

## Common mistakes
Do not say a hard sound like in 'get'.
Before e and i, the sound must stay soft.
Do not add an extra sound between letters.
Keep the syllable tight and clean.
Do not whisper this sound.
Your vocal cords must buzz every time.
