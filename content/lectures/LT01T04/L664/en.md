## The sound
Some letters change their voice in pairs. One sound is hard and deep. The other sound is soft and sharp. Listen to the hard start of (gară) /ˈɡa.rə/, station. Now hear the soft start of (ger) /ˈd͡ʒer/, frost. You train your ear to hear this shift.

## How to say it
Say the words out loud. For hard sounds, push air from the back of your throat. For soft sounds, lift your tongue toward your roof. Keep your jaw relaxed. Switch between the two sounds in a smooth rhythm. Feel the tongue move forward and back.

## Reading rules
Look closely at the vowel right after the letter. Letters like a, o, and u keep the sound hard. Letters like e and i make the sound soft. Read the pair slowly. Check the vowel first, then shape your mouth before you speak.

## Common mistakes
Do not rush through the words. Many learners make both sounds the same. That can change the meaning of your word. Also, do not add extra breath at the end. Keep every sound short and clean. Listen carefully as you switch from hard to soft.
