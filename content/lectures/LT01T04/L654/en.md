## The sound
The letter ț makes a sharp sound.
It sounds like the end of 'cats'.
When one thing becomes many, the sound changes.
A single brother is (frate) /ˈfra.te/.
Two or more are (frați) /frat͡sʲ/.
The flat t sound turns into a sharp ț sound.

## How to say it
Place the tip of your tongue behind your top front teeth.
Build up air pressure.
Then, quickly release the air through a narrow gap.
Make a quick 'ts' sound.
Do not use your voice box for this sound.
Keep the air stream short and crisp.
Push your lips slightly forward as you speak.

## Reading rules
Look at the letter ț with a small comma below it.
In many plurals, the last t changes into ț before the final letter i.
Listen to the word (băieți) /bəˈjet͡sʲ/, which means boys.
The final letter i is very soft and short.
You barely hear it after the ț sound.

## Common mistakes
Do not say a regular t in these plurals.
Saying (frați) /frat͡sʲ/ with a plain t sounds wrong.
Also, do not make a long vowel at the end.
Keep the final sound quick and sharp.
Do not separate the t and s into two steps.
Blend them into one single quick burst of air.
