## The sound
The letter c makes a crisp sound. It sounds like the 'k' in 'keep'. Listen to the word (casă) /ˈka.sə/, which means house. You hear a clear, hard sound at the start. Your breath stops completely for a moment. Then you release the air quickly.

## How to say it
Raise the back of your tongue. Press it firmly against the roof of your mouth. Do not use your voice yet. Build up air behind your tongue. Now, drop your tongue to let the air out. Listen to (copil) /koˈpil/, which means child. Keep the sound dry and short.

## Reading rules
Look at the letter right after c. Say this sound when c comes before a, o, or u. Hear it in (curat) /kuˈrat/, which means clean. The vowel changes, but the first sound stays firm. Do not change this sound before these three letters. It never softens here.

## Common mistakes
Do not add a heavy puff of air. Keep the release tight and neat. Also, do not make a 'ch' sound. That sound happens before other letters. Practice with (casă) /ˈka.sə/. Keep your tongue relaxed after the burst. Keep the sound clean and simple every time.
