## The sound
In Romanian, the letter group *gh* makes a hard sound.
It sounds like the 'g' in 'go' or 'get'.
You hear it in (ghem) /ɡem/, which means ball of yarn.
You also hear it in (ghici) /ˈɡi.tʃi/, which means to guess.
The sound is always firm and voiced.

## How to say it
Open your mouth a little bit.
Press the back of your tongue against the roof of your mouth.
Build up air behind your tongue.
Release the air quickly and vibrate your vocal cords.
Do not blow extra breath through your mouth.
Keep the sound short, crisp, and clean.

## Reading rules
The letter *h* is silent here.
It only protects the hard sound before *e* and *i*.
Write *ghe* to get a hard sound before *e*.
Write *ghi* to get a hard sound before *i*.
Listen to (ghid) /ɡid/, which means guide.
Without *h*, the letter *g* sounds soft.

## Common mistakes
Do not pronounce the letter *h*.
The *h* has no sound of its own here.
Do not say a soft sound like 'j' in 'jam'.
Always keep the sound hard like 'gift'.
Practice with the word (ghici) /ˈɡi.tʃi/.
Keep your tongue firm at the back for every word.
