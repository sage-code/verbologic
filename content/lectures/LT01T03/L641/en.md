## The sound
Notice the last sound in (casă) /ˈka.sə/ and (casa) /ˈka.sa/.
The letter ă sounds relaxed and soft like the sound in 'comma'.
The letter a sounds bright, clear, and open like the sound in 'father'.
These two endings change the meaning completely.
One means a house, and the other means the house.

## How to say it
For ă, keep your mouth half open.
Relax your tongue in the middle of your mouth.
Make a short, quiet sound.
For a, open your mouth wide.
Drop your lower jaw.
Push your voice out clearly and cleanly.
Keep your lips relaxed for both sounds.

## Reading rules
Look closely at the little mark above the letter.
The letter ă with a bowl on top always makes a muffled sound.
The plain letter a always sounds clear and wide.
In (casa) /ˈka.sa/, the stress stays on the first part.
Do not move the stress to the end.

## Common mistakes
Do not pronounce the ending ă like an open a.
If you do, you say (casa) /ˈka.sa/ instead of (casă) /ˈka.sə/.
Also, do not stress the final vowel.
Keep the ending light and short.
Listen carefully to the difference and keep your jaw loose.
