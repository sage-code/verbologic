## Two Different Endings
In Romanian, word endings are very important. The letter **ă** sounds soft and relaxed, while the letter **a** sounds open and bright. When you add **-a** to the end of a word, it often means "the". For example, **casă** means "a house", but **casa** means "the house". Changing the final sound changes the whole meaning!

## How Your Mouth Moves
To say **-ă**, keep your mouth relaxed and only slightly open. Your tongue rests flat in the middle, like saying "uh". To say **-a**, open your mouth wider and drop your jaw. Make the sound clear and bright, like saying "ah" at the doctor. In **casa** /ˈka.sa/, the stress is on the first syllable, but the final **-a** still stays open and clean.

## Listen and Compare
Listen carefully to pairs of words. Notice how the sound shifts at the very end.
- **casă** /ˈka.sə/ means "a house".
- **casa** /ˈka.sa/ means "the house".
- **fată** /ˈfa.tə/ means "a girl".
- **fata** /ˈfa.ta/ means "the girl".
Hear how **casa** ends with a wide, open vowel!

## Common Mistakes
English speakers often turn final vowels into a sleepy "uh" sound. In English, the end of "sofa" sounds like **-ă**. If you do that in Romanian, you might say "a house" when you mean "the house"! Always remember to open your mouth wide when you see the final **-a**.
