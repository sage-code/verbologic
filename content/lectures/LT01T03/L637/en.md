## A vowel that never hides
In Romanian, every vowel likes to be heard. The letter **ă** often sits right at the end of a word. In some other languages, you might swallow or drop the last sound when speaking quickly. But in Romanian, the final **ă** is always a full vowel. You must never skip it or turn it into silence. It stays in the word until the very end, keeping the word whole and easy to understand.

## How to use your mouth
To make the **ă** sound, keep your mouth slightly open. Do not stretch your lips wide like a smile, and do not round them into a circle. Relax your tongue flat in the middle of your mouth. It sounds like the "a" in "sofa". In words ending in **ă**, the stress usually lands on the syllable before it. Say that first part with more power, and then say the final **ă** softly, but keep your voice ringing clearly.

## Practicing with lampă
Let us practice with a simple word: **lampă** /ˈlam.pə/. This word means "lamp". Notice that it has two parts: **lam** and **pă**. When you say it, give strong energy to **lam**. Then, step down gently to pronounce **pă**. Do not cut the word short! Make sure your listener hears the sweet, clear **ă** at the finish line before you stop talking.

## Common mistakes to avoid
Many English speakers see a word like **lampă** and want to say "lamp". In English, we often stop talking after a hard consonant, or we turn ending vowels into tiny whispers. In Romanian, if you drop the **ă**, the word sounds broken or changes its meaning completely. Always finish the word completely. Keep your breath flowing until that final **ă** floats all the way out of your mouth.
