## The sound
Sometimes two vowels stand right next to each other. You do not slide them into one sound. Instead, you say each vowel cleanly in its own beat. Listen to (România) /ro.mɨˈni.a/, which means Romania. The last two sounds do not melt together. You make two clear vocal beats at the end.

## How to say it
Open your mouth for the first vowel. Keep your voice strong and steady. Then, quickly move your lips and tongue to the second vowel. Do not drop your breath between them. Listen to (aer) /ˈa.er/, which means air. Your tone stays steady as your mouth changes shape for each sound.

## Reading rules
When two written vowels split into two syllables, pronounce both fully. One vowel usually takes more stress than the other. In (România) /ro.mɨˈni.a/, the voice rises slightly on the first vowel of the pair. The final vowel is softer. Give each letter its own time and clear shape.

## Common mistakes
Do not crush the two vowels into one quick glide. People often blend them like the sound in 'boy'. In Romanian, keep the vowels separate. Do not insert an extra puff of air between them either. Move smoothly from one vowel directly into the next without stopping your voice.
