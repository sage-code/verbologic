## The sound
A closed syllable ends with a consonant sound. Listen to the word (pământ) /pəˈmɨnt/, which means earth. The last syllable ends with two consonants. Every ending consonant makes a crisp, clear sound. You must not swallow or drop the sound at the end.

## How to say it
Open your mouth for the vowel first. Then close your mouth firmly to finish the consonant. Listen to the word (om) /om/, which means man. Your lips touch completely for the final sound. Keep your breath steady until the syllable stops. Release the sound cleanly without adding an extra vowel.

## Reading rules
Look at the letters at the end of a syllable. When you see a consonant, read it with full energy. Listen to (lac) /lak/, which means lake. The letter sound does not fade away. In Romanian, you pronounce every written consonant firmly. Stress does not change this rule.

## Common mistakes
Do not swallow the final consonant. Do not soften it too much or let it disappear. Also, do not add an extra vowel at the end. Say (pământ) /pəˈmɨnt/ with a sharp final stop. Finish each sound cleanly before you move to the next word.
