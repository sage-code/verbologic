## The sound
Many Romanian words have a very regular beat. You often stress the second-last syllable. This beat sounds natural and rhythmic. Listen to the word (copilul) /koˈpi.lul/, which means the child. The middle beat is the loudest. Your voice rises slightly on that part.

## How to say it
Count the parts of the word from the end. Push more air from your lungs on the second-last part. Keep your mouth open longer on its vowel. Make that vowel louder and slightly higher in pitch. Say the last syllable softly and quickly. Let your voice relax at the very end.

## Reading rules
Two-syllable words often take stress on the first part. Listen to (masă) /ˈma.sə/, which means table. You also hear this pattern in (fată) /ˈfa.tə/, which means girl. When words grow longer with endings, the stress often stays on the second-last syllable. Look at word endings to find the rhythm easily.

## Common mistakes
Do not stress every syllable with equal power. That makes your speech sound flat and robotic. Also, do not always push stress onto the very last vowel. When you say (copilul) /koˈpi.lul/, do not stress the final part. Keep the last sound light and short.
