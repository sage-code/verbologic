## The Sound of «Э»

The letter **Э** represents an open-mid front unrounded vowel `[ɛ]`, identical to the 'e' in English **bed** or **pet**. Unlike Russian **Е**, it does not palatalize the preceding consonant and never includes an initial 'y' sound (`[j]`). It is a pure, steady vowel sound that stays open and crisp.

## How to Pronounce «Э»

Open your mouth moderately, keeping your lips relaxed and slightly spread without rounding them. Place the tip of your tongue gently behind your lower front teeth and raise the middle of your tongue slightly toward the palate. Release your voice cleanly, ensuring you do not glide into an 'ee' or add a 'y' onset at the beginning.

## Spelling Rules for «Э»

**Э** is relatively rare and appears mostly at the beginning of native words like **это** or in loanwords such as **мэр** and **дуэт**. It is specifically used after consonants to indicate that the preceding letter remains hard and non-palatalized. In modern Russian orthography, foreign borrowings often preserve the hard consonant sound even when spelled with **е**, but **э** guarantees a hard pronunciation.

## Sound Variations and Common Pitfalls

When stressed, **э** sounds clearly as `[ɛ]`, but in unstressed positions, it undergoes reduction toward a short `[ɪ]`, as heard in **этаж** (`[ɪˈtaʂ]`). The most common mistake for English speakers is pronouncing it with a 'y' glide like `[jɛ]` (confusing it with **е**). Always ensure the vowel starts cleanly without any palatal friction.
