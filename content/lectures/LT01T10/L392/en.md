## The Sound of У

The letter **У** represents the close back rounded vowel `/u/`. It sounds very similar to the 'oo' in English *moon*, but it is pronounced with tighter lip rounding and without gliding into a 'w' sound. Unlike English vowels, Russian `/u/` remains pure, steady, and energetic throughout its entire duration.

## How to Pronounce У

Pull your tongue body backward toward the soft palate while raising the back of the tongue high in the mouth. Round your lips firmly into a tight, funnel-like circle, pushing them slightly outward. Keep your vocal cords vibrating and avoid relaxing your lips until the vowel is completely finished.

## Spelling and Rules

Lowercase **у** looks identical to the Latin letter 'y', which frequently confuses beginners; always remember it is a pure vowel. When **у** follows a consonant, it indicates that the preceding consonant is hard (non-palatalized). After the hush consonants **ж**, **ш**, **ч**, and **щ**, the sound `/u/` is strictly written with **у**, never with **ю**.

## Variants and Pitfalls

Under stress, **У** is pronounced clearly as a full `[u]`, as in **ухо** (`/ˈu.xə/`). In unstressed positions, it retains its lip rounding but becomes slightly shorter and more relaxed, tending toward `[ʊ]` as in **рука** (`/ruˈka/`). A common mistake for English speakers is diphthongizing the sound into `[uw]` or failing to round the lips tightly enough.
