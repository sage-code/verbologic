## The Sound of Ц

The letter **Ц** represents the voiceless alveolar affricate `[ts]`, identical to the *ts* in English *cats* or *tsunami*. In Russian, this sound is strictly **hard** (unpalatalized) in standard pronunciation, never softening even when followed by vowels like **е** or **и**.

## How to Produce [ts]

Start by pressing the tip of your tongue firmly against the alveolar ridge just behind your upper front teeth, exactly as if preparing to say `[t]`. Immediately release the closure into a sharp, hissing `[s]` sound without pausing between the two phases. Keep your tongue body low and tense so the sound does not accidentally soften into `[tɕ]`.

## Spelling Rules for Ц

The letter **Ц** appears at the beginning, middle, and end of words. A key orthographic rule is that vowels **и** and **е** written after **Ц** are still pronounced as hard vowels: **ци** sounds like `[tsɨ]` and **це** sounds like `[tsɛ]`. In unstressed syllables, **це** reduces toward `[tsɨ]`.

## Avoiding Mistakes

English speakers often separate the elements into two clumsy consonants instead of a single, crisp affricate. The most frequent error, however, is softening **Ц** before **е** or **и** into a sound like English *ch* `[tʃ]`. Keep the sound completely hard in words like **центр** `[tsɛntr]` and **цирк** `[tsɨrk]`.
