## Sound of the Letter

The letter **Ш** represents the voiceless retroflex sibilant `[ʂ]`. Unlike the English 'sh' sound in *shoe*, which is palatalized and soft, the Russian **Ш** is inherently hard, deep, and 'hollow'. Even when written before soft-indicating vowels like **и** or **е**, it never softens.

## Pronunciation Guide

To pronounce **Ш**, curl the tip of your tongue slightly back toward the hard palate without letting it touch. Flatten the middle of your tongue instead of arching it upward as you would in English. Push the air out with slightly rounded lips to produce a low, rustling friction.

## Spelling and Rules

The letter **Ш** looks like a three-pronged fork resting on a baseline, written identically in lowercase except for size. Under Russian spelling conventions, the combination **ши** is historically written with **и** but is always pronounced `[ʂɨ]`. Likewise, combinations like **ше** sound like `[ʂɛ]`.

## Common Pitfalls

English speakers frequently confuse **Ш** (`[ʂ]`) with the softer letter **Щ** (`[ɕː]`). Producing a soft English-style 'sh' for **Ш** sounds unnatural and can lead to misunderstandings, such as confusing **чаша** (bowl) with **чаща** (thicket). Remember that **Ш** is strictly hard in standard Russian.
