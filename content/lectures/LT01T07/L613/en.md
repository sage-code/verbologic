## The Sound of '-sland'

In the common noun **island**, the letter group `-sland` is pronounced `/lənd/`, featuring a completely silent **s**. When `-land` combines with an existing word ending in an audible **s** (like *grassland*), it is pronounced `/slænd/` or `/slənd/`. Learners must be careful not to pronounce the **s** in *island*.

## How to Say It

For the silent-s pattern in *island*, glide directly from the preceding vowel into a light `/l/`, followed immediately by a weak schwa `/ə/` and the consonant cluster `/nd/`. Place the tip of your tongue against the upper alveolar ridge to form `/l/`, keep the vowel minimal, and finish with a firm `/nd/`. Do not let your tongue touch your teeth or hiss for the letter **s**.

## Spelling and Patterns

The spelling `-sland` with a silent **s** is famous in **island** due to an erroneous historical association with the French word *isle*. In standard geographic compounds like *grassland* or *disisland*, the **s** belongs to the first morpheme and remains fully voiced or unvoiced. It generally appears in the final unaccented syllable of these nouns.

## Comparison and Common Mistakes

Compare the silent **s** in *island* (`/ˈaɪ.lənd/`) with the audible `/s/` in compounds like *grassland* (`/ˈɡrɑːs.lænd/`). A very frequent error among non-native speakers is pronouncing the **s** in *island* as `/ˈaɪs.lənd/` or `/ˈɪz.lənd/`. Remember that *island* begins strictly with the diphthong `/aɪ/` and no sibilant sound at all.
