Rusty is a bright red rooster. He stands on a wooden fence. The warm sun shines on the farm.

Rusty opens his yellow beak. His morning voice is very quiet. Rusty is sad.

Daisy the cow walks to the fence. She brings a bowl of cool water. Rusty drinks the water.

Rusty feels happy and strong now. He sings a loud morning song. The farm animals smile at their friend.
