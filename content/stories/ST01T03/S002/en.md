Bella is a white sheep. She lives on a green hill. Today, the sun is bright.

A small bird visits Bella. The bird is named Blue. Blue sings a happy song.

A cold wind blows across the hill. Little Blue shivers in the wind. The small bird is very cold.

Bella sits near the cold bird. Bella has soft and warm wool. Blue sits in the thick wool.

Blue feels very safe and warm. The little bird sings again. Bella smiles at her sweet friend.
