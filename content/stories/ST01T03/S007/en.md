Milo is a small brown hamster. He lives in a sunny cage. He sees a big seed on a box.

Milo wants the big seed. The box is very tall. Milo cannot reach the seed.

Milo pushes a small wooden block. He moves the block near the box. Milo stands on the block.

Now Milo takes the big seed. He eats the tasty seed. Milo is proud and happy.
