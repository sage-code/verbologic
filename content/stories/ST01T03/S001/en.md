Pip is a happy white goose. Pip lives near a big pond. He finds a bright yellow flower.

Pip drops the yellow flower. The flower floats on the water. Pip cannot reach the flower.

Dot the little duck sees Pip. Dot swims fast across the pond. She brings the flower to Pip.

Pip is very glad now. He thanks his kind friend Dot. They play together near the water.
