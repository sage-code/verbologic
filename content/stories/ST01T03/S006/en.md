Barnaby is a small guinea pig. He lives in a green garden. He wants tasty food.

Barnaby sees a red apple. The apple sits on a big stone. Barnaby cannot reach the apple.

Barnaby finds a small wooden block. He pushes the block to the stone. Barnaby climbs the block.

Now Barnaby reaches the red apple. He eats the sweet fruit slowly. Barnaby feels proud and happy.
