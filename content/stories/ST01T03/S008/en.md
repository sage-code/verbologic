Finn is a little brown deer. He walks in a sunny forest. Finn looks at the tall trees.

Finn sees a small bird on the grass. The small bird is cold and sad. The bird cannot reach its high nest.

Finn lowers his head to the ground. The small bird sits on Finn's head.

Finn stands tall near the tree branch. The bird moves into the warm nest. Finn and the bird are happy friends.
