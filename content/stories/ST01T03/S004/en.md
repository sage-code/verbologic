Kiki is a bright green parrot. He lives in a sunny garden.

Kiki sees a sweet red berry. The berry is on the grass. Kiki flies to the berry.

A small gray mouse visits the garden. The little mouse is very hungry.

Kiki shares the sweet berry. The mouse eats with Kiki. The two friends are very happy.
