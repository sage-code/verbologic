Ollie is a little river otter. He plays in the sunny water. He has a favorite smooth rock.

Ollie tosses the rock high. The rock falls into deep water. It sinks to the river bottom.

Ollie swims to the river floor. He cannot see the smooth rock. Ollie feels very sad now.

A small fish sees sad Ollie. The friendly fish finds the rock. Ollie takes his favorite rock.

Ollie is happy with his rock. He thanks the kind little fish. They swim together in the river.
