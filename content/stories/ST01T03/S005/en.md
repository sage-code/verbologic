Toby is a small green turtle. He lives near a quiet pond. Toby wants a sweet yellow flower.

The yellow flower is on a tall stone. Toby has very short legs. The stone is too high for him.

Milo the frog sees his friend Toby. Milo rolls a small rock to the stone. The small rock is a good step.

Toby walks onto the small rock. He takes the sweet yellow flower. Toby shares the pretty petals with Milo.
