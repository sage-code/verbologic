Benny the beaver lives by a blue river. He gathers small brown sticks for his home. Benny loves his quiet river.

Benny finds a very long stick. The heavy stick does not move. Benny is sad and tired.

Mia the duck swims to Benny. She pushes the heavy stick with Benny. They work together with joy.

The big stick floats on the water. Benny builds a strong new home. Benny and Mia are happy friends.
