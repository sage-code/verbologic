Kiki the kangaroo is on the hill. Night comes and the air is still.

Kiki jumps in the soft light. The yellow stars are very bright. Kiki cannot stop her quick jumps.

Mother kangaroo makes a soft nest. She holds Kiki to her chest.

Kiki closes both of her eyes. Stars shine softly in the skies. Little Kiki sleeps in the nest.
