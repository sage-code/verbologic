Milo is a small penguin. He stands on the quiet ice. The dark sky has many stars.

Milo feels cold in the night. The ice is hard and white. He cannot sleep on the snow.

Mother Penguin calls little Milo. She opens her big warm wings. Milo walks quickly to his mother.

Mother Penguin hugs Milo very close. Her soft feathers are so warm. Milo closes his eyes and sleeps.
