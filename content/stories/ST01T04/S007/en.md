Benny the koala sits in a tall tree. The little stars shine for him to see.

Benny wants to rest his sleepy head. He has no warm or soft bed.

Mother Koala holds little Benny tight. She hugs him in the dark night.

Benny slowly closes his small eyes. The bright moon shines in the skies.
