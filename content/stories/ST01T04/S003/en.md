Pip the zebra stands in the grass. The warm sun sets in the west. It is time for a quiet rest.

The silver moon shines very bright. Pip blinks at the clear light. He wants to sleep tonight.

Pip rests his head on the ground. The dark meadow makes no sound. He breathes slow and deep.

The gentle stars shine and glow. Pip feels very calm and slow. The little zebra is asleep.
