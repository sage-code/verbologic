Toby the tiger sits on his bed. He rests his sleepy orange head.

The dark bedroom is very cold. He needs a warm blanket to hold.

Toby finds a big red sheet. The soft sheet covers his feet.

Toby smiles in the soft light. The happy tiger sleeps all night.
