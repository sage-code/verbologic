Sam is a small green snake. He lives by a calm lake.

The night wind is very cold. The grey stones are hard and old. Sam cannot sleep on the stones.

Sam moves to a soft nest. It is a good place to rest.

Sam closes his two tired eyes. The stars shine in the skies.
