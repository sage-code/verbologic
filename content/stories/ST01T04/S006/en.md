Danny the dolphin swims in the bay. The warm sun finishes the day.

Danny is tired and wants to sleep. The dark water is cold and deep.

His mother swims to a quiet cave. The cave stops every big cold wave.

Danny rests with his mother tonight. He closes his eyes in the moonlight.
