Eddie the eagle sits very high. The bright moon shines in the sky.

The night wind blows cold and deep. Eddie the eagle cannot sleep.

Mother Eagle sits by his side. She opens her wings soft and wide.

Eddie rests his head on her chest. Now Eddie sleeps in the nest.
