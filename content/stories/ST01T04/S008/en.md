The big silver moon shines bright. Bao the panda loves the night.

The hard stone gives no rest. Bao wants a soft, cozy nest.

Bao sees green grass on the ground. The forest makes no loud sound.

The fresh grass makes a bed. Bao rests his soft, sleepy head.
