Leo the crow flies across a dry garden. He is very thirsty. He wants fresh water.

Leo sees a tall bottle on a table. He puts his beak into the bottle. The water is too low.

Leo sees small grey stones on the grass. He carries the stones to the bottle. He drops the stones inside.

The water rises to the top. Leo drinks the cool water now. He is happy and clever.
