Boris is a big gray wolf. He walks in the green forest. He loves the tall trees.

Boris sees a small blue bird. The bird sits on the cold stone. It cannot fly to its nest.

Boris brings a big soft leaf. He places the leaf near the bird. The leaf is warm and dry.

The bird hops onto the soft leaf. It sings a sweet song for Boris. Boris smiles with his new friend.
