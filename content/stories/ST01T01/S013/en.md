Kibo the elephant stands near the river. His small friend Pip, a little grey mongoose, sits in the grass. They look at the blue sky.

Pip drops a bright yellow flower. The flower falls into the deep water. Pip feels very sad about the flower.

Kibo walks into the cool water. He reaches with his long trunk. He catches the wet yellow flower.

Kibo gives the flower to Pip. Pip smiles at his kind friend. Big friends can help small friends.
