Sammy the squirrel lives in a tall tree. Today, Sammy finds a big brown nut. He is very happy.

Sammy wants the nut in his home. The nut is very heavy. Sammy cannot lift the big nut.

Ben the rabbit walks to the tree. Ben helps Sammy with the heavy nut. They push the nut together.

The nut is now in the tree. Sammy shares sweet seeds with Ben. Good friends help each other.
