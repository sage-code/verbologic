Sam is a small brown squirrel. He sits under a tall tree. He sees a big red apple.

The sweet apple is very high. Sam jumps, but he is small. He cannot reach the red fruit.

Dan the tall deer walks here. Dan pushes the branch with his head. The red apple falls to the grass.

Sam smiles and takes the apple. He gives half to kind Dan. The two happy friends eat together.
