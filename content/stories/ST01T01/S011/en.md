Leo the lion rests on the grass. A small bird sits near Leo. Her name is Pip.

The afternoon sun is very hot. Pip is tired and hot. There is no shade for Pip.

Leo sits next to Pip. Leo makes a big shadow with his body. Now Pip is in the cool shadow.

Pip drinks water and sings a song. Leo smiles at his little friend. Good friends help each other.
