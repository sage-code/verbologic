Pip is a small yellow bird. He sits in a green garden. He wants some good food.

Pip sees a tall sunflower with seeds. The seeds are very hard. Pip cannot open the seeds alone.

Leo the brown mouse comes. Leo has sharp little teeth. He cracks the seeds easily.

Pip and Leo share the food. They eat together in the garden. Good friends help each other.
