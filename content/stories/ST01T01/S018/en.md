Coco is a big green crocodile. He rests near a sunny river. A small bird sits on a rock.

The bird holds a pretty red flower. The flower falls into the deep water. The bird is very sad.

Coco swims to the red flower. He carries the flower on his nose. Coco swims to the rock.

Coco gives the flower to the bird. The little bird is happy now. Coco smiles at his new friend.
