Pip the squirrel stands on a hill. He finds a large red strawberry. The strawberry looks very sweet.

Pip wants to take the strawberry home. The fruit is too heavy for him. He pushes it, but it stays still.

Sam the hedgehog walks to the hill. Sam sees Pip and the big fruit. Sam wants to help his friend.

Pip and Sam push the fruit together. The heavy fruit moves easily now. Both friends share the sweet red food.
