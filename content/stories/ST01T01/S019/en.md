Milo the monkey lives in a green jungle. He sits on a tall tree branch. He sees two sweet yellow bananas.

Milo takes the two bananas. He holds them in his hands. He wants to eat them all.

A small bird, Kiki, visits Milo. She has no food today. She is very hungry and sad.

Milo gives one sweet banana to Kiki. Kiki is happy and thanks Milo. The two friends eat together.
