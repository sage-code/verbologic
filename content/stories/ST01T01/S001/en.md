Pip is a very small brown mouse. He walks in a sunny forest. Pip finds sweet red berries near a rock.

A heavy stick falls to the grass. The stick traps Pip by his tail. Pip cannot move his tiny legs.

Bruno the big brown bear walks near Pip. Bruno sees the sad little mouse. The strong bear lifts the heavy stick.

Pip is free and very happy now. He gives a sweet berry to Bruno. Small friends can have big hearts.
