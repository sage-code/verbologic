Leo the frog sits by a pond. He sees a bright yellow flower.

The flower is on a high rock. Leo cannot reach the high rock. He feels very sad.

Sam the turtle swims to the frog. Leo sits on the turtle. Sam carries his small friend.

They stop at the high rock. Leo smells the bright yellow flower. Good friends always help each other.
