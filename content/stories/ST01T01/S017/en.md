Pip is a little green frog. He lives in a blue pond. Pip sits on a warm rock.

Pip sees a small ant in the water. The ant is on a thin stick. The ant is wet and cold.

Pip pushes a big leaf to the stick. Pip helps the little ant. The ant walks to the safe leaf.

The big leaf is warm and dry. Pip and the ant rest together. Pip smiles with his new friend.
