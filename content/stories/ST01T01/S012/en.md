Pip is a small brown mouse. Pip lives in a green garden. Pip finds a big yellow seed.

The seed is very big and heavy. Pip pushes the seed across the grass. But the seed does not move.

Toby the small bird sees Pip. Toby flies to the grass. Toby pushes the seed with Pip.

The seed rolls to Pip's home. Pip is glad and says thank you. Pip shares the sweet food with Toby.
