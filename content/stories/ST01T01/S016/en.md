Otto the owl sits in a tree. The night is dark and quiet. He has big, bright yellow eyes.

A small mouse stands under the tree. Her name is Mia. Mia is lost in the dark grass.

Otto flies to the soft ground. He walks beside his new friend. His big eyes see the small path.

Mia finds her warm home. She smiles and thanks the kind owl. Otto is happy to help a friend.
