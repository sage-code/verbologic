Toby the hedgehog walks in the forest. The cold wind blows. He finds many soft leaves.

Toby meets a little mouse. Her name is Nina. Nina is cold and sad.

Toby collects the soft leaves. He carries them on his back. Toby brings the leaves to Nina.

Nina makes a warm bed. The two friends rest together. They feel happy and warm.
