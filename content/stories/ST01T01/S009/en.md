Pip is a little brown mouse. Ollie is a happy green frog. They meet near a calm pond.

Pip holds a small red ball. The red ball falls into the water. Pip cannot swim and feels sad.

Ollie jumps into the pond. Ollie swims to the red ball. He pushes the ball to the grass.

Pip takes the wet ball. Pip thanks his kind friend Ollie. The two friends play happily together.
