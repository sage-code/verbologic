Bruno the bear sits by a river. Bruno is big and friendly. He has a basket of red berries.

A little rabbit hops near Bruno. The rabbit looks at the empty grass. The rabbit is very hungry.

Bruno pushes the basket to the rabbit. He smiles with warm brown eyes. The rabbit takes a sweet berry.

Bruno and the rabbit eat together. They sit under the warm sun. Good friends always share food.
