A big lion sleeps under a green tree. A small mouse walks near the lion.

A heavy net covers the big lion. The lion cannot move his legs. The big lion roars loudly.

The small mouse hears the loud lion. She bites the heavy net.

The big lion is free now. He smiles at the small mouse. Even small friends can give big help.
