Pip the rabbit hops in the garden. He sees a big apple on a tree.

Pip jumps high into the air. The red apple is too high. Pip sits on the green grass.

Max the hedgehog walks into the garden. Max pushes a sturdy wooden box. He helps his good friend Pip.

Pip stands on the strong box. He takes the sweet red apple. The two friends eat together happily.
