Penny is a small red hen. She lives near a fairy house. Penny finds a glowing golden seed.

Penny asks Leo the dog for help. Leo rests on the green grass. He does not want to dig.

Penny digs the soft ground alone. She puts the seed into the soil. A magic tree with sweet bread appears.

Leo smells the warm sweet bread. Penny shares the food with Leo. The two friends eat and smile.
