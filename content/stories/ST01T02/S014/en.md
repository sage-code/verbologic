Leo is a gentle brown horse. He lives in a magic forest. He walks among tall green trees.

Leo meets a small fairy named Flora. Flora sits on a gray stone. She is sad because her wings hurt.

Leo stands near the gray stone. He offers his back to Flora. Flora sits softly on the horse.

Leo walks to a tall pink flower. This flower is the home of Flora. Flora hugs Leo and says thank you.
