He was a small grey duckling who looked different from the other birds in the farmyard. Some ducks laughed, so the duckling wandered away and found a quiet place beside a lake. There, an old turtle shared the warmest patch of sunshine.

Winter passed. When spring returned, the duckling stretched his wings and saw graceful white swans on the water. He went closer and looked at his reflection. He was no longer a little duckling: he had grown into a swan.

The swans welcomed him, and two children, a boy and a girl, on the shore smiled at his bright feathers. The young bird felt at home at last. He remembered the lonely days and decided to be kind to anyone who seemed different. He had needed time to grow, and everyone deserves the chance to become themselves.
