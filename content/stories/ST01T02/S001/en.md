Little Red Riding Hood, a young girl, packed bread and apples for her grandmother. Her mother reminded her to follow the forest path and not stop to talk with strangers. Red promised and set off.

In the woods, a wolf asked where she was going. Red remembered her mother's words and did not tell him. She hurried to her grandmother's cottage. The grandmother heard the wolf at the door, closed it, and called a nearby forest ranger, a man.

When Red arrived, the ranger was there too. The wolf had to leave, and Red and her grandmother shared their picnic safely. Red learned that a familiar path and a trusted grown-up can help keep us safe.
