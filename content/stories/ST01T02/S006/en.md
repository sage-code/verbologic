Anna walks in the green forest. She sees a small bird on a rock. The bird is cold and sad.

Anna opens her warm brown coat. She puts soft leaves near the bird. She shares her sweet bread.

The bird eats the bread happily. Now the bird is warm and strong. It has magic golden feathers.

The bird gives a golden flower to Anna. Anna smiles and holds the pretty flower. They are good friends.
