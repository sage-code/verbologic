Barnaby is a small brown dog. He lives in a magic forest. He walks near a tall tree.

Barnaby sees a sad star on the grass. The little star is cold and lost. It cannot fly to the night sky.

Barnaby carries the star to a high hill. The walk is long and dark. Barnaby is small, but he is brave.

Barnaby places the star on a rock. The happy star flies to the sky. Barnaby wags his tail and smiles.
