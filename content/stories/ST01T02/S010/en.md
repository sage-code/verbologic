Benny is a gentle white rabbit. He lives in a quiet green forest. Benny has a shiny golden crown.

Benny hops near an old stone well. The golden crown sinks in the water. Benny feels very sad.

A green frog appears on a rock. The kind frog holds the golden crown. The frog smiles at Benny.

The frog gives the crown to Benny. Benny shares a sweet apple with him. They are happy friends in the forest.
