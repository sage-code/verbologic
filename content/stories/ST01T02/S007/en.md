Anna walks in the green garden. She sees a small fairy. The fairy sits on a red flower.

The fairy is sad. Her tiny wand is under a big stone. The fairy cannot move the stone.

Anna helps her small friend. She lifts the heavy stone with her hands. The fairy takes the wand.

Now the fairy is very happy. She makes bright lights over the garden. Good friends always help each other.
