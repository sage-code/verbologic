Bella is a friendly white cow. She lives in a green meadow. She meets a tiny fairy near a flower.

The fairy is very sad. Her little silver wand is in the bush. The bush has sharp thorns.

Bella wants to help the fairy. Bella moves the branches with her nose. The wand falls on the soft grass.

The happy fairy touches Bella with the wand. Now Bella has beautiful rainbow spots. The two friends dance in the sun.
