Sammy is a friendly cat. He lives in a sunny castle garden. He wears a shiny golden bell.

Sammy walks to a big stone. His golden bell drops into a dark hole. Sammy is sad without his bell.

A small fairy flies to the stone. She hears the sad cat. The fairy touches the hole with her wand.

The golden bell is safe now. The fairy gives the bell to Sammy. They are very happy together.
