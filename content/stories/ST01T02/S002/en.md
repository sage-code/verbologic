Three little pigs left home to build houses of their own. The first used straw, the second used sticks, and the third worked carefully with bricks.

Soon, a hungry wolf came by and blew down the first two houses. The pigs ran to their brother's strong brick house.

The wolf huffed and puffed, but the walls stayed firm. Tired, he sat outside. The pigs asked why he was so hungry. The wolf admitted that he had not looked for food. The pigs shared some soup and showed him where berries grew.

After that, the wolf learned to ask for help instead of frightening others. The pigs learned that careful work takes time, and kindness can turn a difficult meeting into a new beginning.
