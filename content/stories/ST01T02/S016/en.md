Bruno is a kind donkey. He walks in a quiet forest. The green trees are very tall.

Bruno sees a small fairy near a rock. The fairy has blue wings. She is sad and cries.

The fairy has no home today. Bruno carries big leaves to the fairy. He wants to help.

They make a little leaf house together. The fairy smiles and pats Bruno. They are happy friends.
