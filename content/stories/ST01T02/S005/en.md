Anna, a young girl, walks on the green hill. She meets a small dragon there. He has shiny blue wings.

The dragon sits on a gray rock. His golden ball is in deep water. The dragon is very sad today.

Anna wants to help her new friend. She takes a long wooden stick. She moves the ball to the grass.

The dragon holds his bright ball happily. He gives Anna a warm smile. The two friends play all day.
