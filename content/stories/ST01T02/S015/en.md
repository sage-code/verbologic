Pip is a small white goat. He lives on a green mountain. Pip loves sweet yellow flowers.

Pip sees a tiny fairy near a rock. The fairy is sad. A bush holds her blue dress.

Pip walks to the green bush. He eats the leaves very slowly. The fairy is free now.

The happy fairy touches the grass. Sweet yellow flowers grow everywhere. Pip eats and smiles with his new friend.
