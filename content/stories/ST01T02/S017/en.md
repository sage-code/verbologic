Dilly is a small white duck. She swims in a calm fairy pond.

A tiny fairy sits on a stone. She cries for her lost silver ring. The ring is in the deep water.

Dilly dives into the clear water. She finds the shiny ring on the sand. Dilly brings the ring to the fairy.

The fairy wears her bright silver ring. She kisses the sweet little duck. Dilly is happy with her new friend.
