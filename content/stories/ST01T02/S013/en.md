Toby is a small pink pig. He lives in a quiet forest. Toby loves bright red flowers.

A tiny fairy sits on a stone. Her wings are blue and shiny. The fairy is sad without her wand.

Toby walks across the green grass. He finds a small golden wand. The wand sparkles in the sunlight.

Toby gives the wand to the fairy. She makes pretty lights in the air. Toby and the fairy dance together.
