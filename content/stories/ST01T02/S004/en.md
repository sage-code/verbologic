Mia is a young princess. She plays in the palace garden. She throws her shiny gold ball.

The ball drops into the pond. The water is very deep. Mia is sad and she cries.

A small green frog sees Mia. The frog swims across the water. He brings the ball to Mia.

Mia smiles and takes her ball. She shares a sweet apple with him. Mia and the frog are good friends.
